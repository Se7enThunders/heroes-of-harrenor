# Houses & Politics

*Who holds which crown, who is bargaining with whom, and where the realm is cracking.*

## The Board Right Now

### King Godric's Crown

> [[Brightguard]], [[House Arden|Arden]] lion

[[House Arden|Arden]] ([[Godric Arden|Godric]], [[Godric Arden|King Godric I]], weeks from the fight); [[House Bruce|Bruce]] ([[Celia Bruce-Arden|Queen Celia]]'s family); Marshall (wolf lords); [[Lord Adam Croft|Croft]] (vassal, [[The Green Isles|Green Isles]]); [[House Grey|Grey]] (leaderless after [[Sanctuary]]); [[House Tempest|Tempest]], [[House Stelaris|Stelaris]], [[House Grimston|Grimston]] ([[House Grey|Grey]] bannermen, retreating); [[House Cromwell|Cromwell]] ([[House Grey|Grey]] bannerman, only house at full strength).

**Aim:** unseat [[William Arden|William]] and take [[Kingsmont]]; now must also save [[The South|the South]] from collapse and face the [[03 The Jihads|Jihad]].

### King William's Crown

> [[Kingsmont]], [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] coin & cavalry

[[House Arden|Arden]] ([[William Arden|William]], [[Godfrey Arden|Godfrey]]'s son); [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] ([[Titus Lancaster|Titus]], real power); [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] (turned at [[Sanctuary]] S30); [[House Fane|Fane]] ([[William Arden|William]]'s betrothed's house); [[House Ryder|Ryder]] (loyal to [[William Arden|William]]; [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] broke away); [[House Sanderfell|Sanderfell]], [[House Corbet|Corbet]] ([[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] bannermen).

**Aim:** keep crown in [[William Arden|William]]'s name with [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] behind it at any price. Murdering [[Henry Grey|Henry]] under guest rights shows how far they'll go.

### Ra'Haad

> the Kalifs, [[Sahirism]]

Kalifs rule in [[Habar]]; [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan]] in [[Na'Buchar]]; 100,000+ host marching on [[Grimhold]]. A [[22 The 7th Jihad|7th Jihad]] likely to strike within a month (S30). Not true allies of the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]; the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] tipped them off by accident, paying them to raid some [[Harrenor]] towns.

### Hidden Hands

> no banner

[[The Mockingbird]] ([[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]]'s spy network); [[Sable Wardens]] & [[The Black Hands|Black Hands]] (seekers of immortality); [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]] (servants of dark gods); [[Colonial Bank]] & [[The Iron Company|Iron Company]] (money and swords); [[Order of Holy Fire]] (at [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s side). Each has its own aim; most play the houses against each other.
