## How to read it

- **Solid lines** are loyalty or banners. **Dotted lines** are weaker ties.
- **CIVIL WAR**: [[Godric Arden|Godric]] and [[William Arden|William]] are both crowned [[House Arden|Ardens]].
- **BETRAYED**: [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] sold out [[Henry Grey]] at [[Sanctuary]] and the [[House Grey|Grey]] camp was routed.
- **Ryder**: [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Sir Garrik]] broke from [[William Arden|William]] to protect [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]], but did not join [[Godric Arden|Godric]].
- **Ra'Haad** is not a true ally of the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]. They paid [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] raiders to hit some towns, which showed [[Ra'Haad]] how weak [[Harrenor]] had become.
- **Hidden hands** such as the [[Sable Wardens]], [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]], [[Colonial Bank]] and [[The Iron Company|Iron Company]] play the houses against each other. The [[Order of Holy Fire]] stands at [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s side.
