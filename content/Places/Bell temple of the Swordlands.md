# Bell temple of the Swordlands

**Ruined temple** in [[The Swordlands]]  
**Held by:** Cleansed  
**First seen:** S15

## What happened here

Abandoned temple complex high on a cliff, rocky NW [[The Swordlands|Swordlands]], thousands of years old, built to worship [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]. Booming voice warns visitors away; [[Artorius|Sir Artoreus]] warded it long ago. No life until bell was rung. Bell tower: [[Dharshoon]] smashed his idol and rang the perfect, uninscribed bell; [[Anri]] appeared. Old cathedral: stair down to tunnels where ~20 [[House Hay]] men lay butchered. Deep hall: party killed [[Goroth]] and his dragon [[Ergond]] (S16). Afterward birds and wildlife returned.
