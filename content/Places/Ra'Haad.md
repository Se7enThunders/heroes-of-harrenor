**Region of [[Harrenor]]**

Seat [[Habar]]; the Kalifs, heirs of the prophet [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]. Southern kingdom of the faith of [[Sahirism]] (god Alnur). Six [[03 The Jihads|jihads]] vs [[Harrenor]], all lost, though 6th killed the [[House York|Yorks]]. Caste society: non-believers pay the jizya, refusers crucified; Dhimmi exiled or killed by S23. Elves sold as slaves. Not true allies of the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]], who tipped them off by paying them to raid some towns. Massing for a [[22 The 7th Jihad|7th Jihad]].
