# Kingsmont

**City** in [[The Gilded Lands]]  
**Held by:** [[William Arden|William]] & [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]]  
**First seen:** S5.5

## What happened here

One of the largest, finest cities on the continent. [[Godric Arden|Godric]] besieged it in S5.5, lost 10,000 when [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]]'s cavalry broke siege. Party returned as envoys S13-14 to trade [[Robert Lancaster]] for [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] (who had already escaped). Palace: party housed there; [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]] debriefed [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] overheard [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]] & [[Catherine Lancaster|Catherine]]. [[Colonial Bank]]: refused [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] 5,000gp; family owes 100,000. Great foundry: biggest for leagues; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] bought a shortsword. North gate: poor quarter where children saw [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] leave with a man in [[House Arden|Arden]] colors. Alleys: [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] traded candied fruit to street urchins for news.
