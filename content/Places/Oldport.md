**Port city** in [[The South]]  
**Held by:** [[House Tempest]]  
**First seen:** S10

## What happened here

[[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s home. Sacked by [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] pirates (women sold in [[Na'Buchar]]), retaken by [[Henry Grey]]. Once glorious; only citadel & castle untouched; [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] corpses hang from walls. Heavy unnatural snow S20-21. Castle Tempest: Lord [[Lord Irwin Tempest|Irwin]]'s hall of judgment; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s [[17 Theo's Trial by Combat|trial by combat]]. Stormbreaker Castle: [[Colonial Bank]] & [[The Iron Company|Iron Company]] seen outside (S23). Grand Cathedral: [[Brother Michael]]; [[St. Maximian]] window; old [[The Old Church Map|church map]] [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] copied. Hopping Horse Tavern: party's base in town. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s house: looted bare; where he found his father. Docks & old [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] camp: tattooed corpse; green-fire ships blown up; [[Constantine]] killed. Empty plaza with gallows: [[The bounty hunter|bounty hunter]]'s ambush.
