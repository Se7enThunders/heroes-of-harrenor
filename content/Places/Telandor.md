**Ruined city** in [[The Blight]]  
**Held by:** The red dragon; the dead  
**First seen:** S24

## What happened here

Dead center of [[The Blight|the Blight]], fallen 3,000 years ago to "winged fire." Looked whole until [[Dharshoon]] woke the Lady and the [[The Kilns|kiln]] went out, then showed true ruin. Banner: gold [[The Almighty|Almighty]] symbol over a mountain on purple. Guarded by the [[Swords of the Church]]; betrayed by [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]. Inner gate & bridge with a ballista. Palace and bell-gated bridge over a chasm ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rang it). Hilltop church: [[Guinevere]] and the [[The Kilns|kiln]], holy-water basins. Great church: 10,000 gold (the party took none), charred corpses, early [[Sable Wardens]]. Catacombs: barracks, archive, gargoyles, locked vault, underground [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite]] and [[The Watcher|the Watcher]]'s bow (chamber collapsed), [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]]'s hidden altar below. [[The Grand Marshal|Grand Marshal]]'s crypt and [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]'s throne room; the [[The Kilns|kiln]] on the altar opened a stair down.
