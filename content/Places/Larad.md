**Walled town** in [[Ra'Haad]]  
**Held by:** Evacuated  
**First seen:** S10

## What happened here

[[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s secret town of freed slaves near an oasis, a few days from [[Na'Buchar]]. One entrance, a belltower, mixed mercenary guards. Exposed and evacuated in S11; refugees attacked in open desert until [[Therond]] turned the sand to glass. [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] died there.
