# Na'Buchar

**Trading city** in [[Ra'Haad]]  
**Held by:** [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan]]  
**First seen:** S6

## What happened here

Trading hub east of [[The Blight|the Blight]]; crucified demis line its streets. [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s estate: abandoned when he fled (S10). [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan|Emir]]'s white palace: council where [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] met [[Salim al Muqtar]] and [[The man in black|the man in black]] eyed [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]. Slave markets: [[Harrenor|Harrenorian]] women from [[Oldport]] incl. [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]]. Ruined [[Harrenor|Harrenorian]] church (an old stable) in slums: secret stair to [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] lair with howlers, a library, slave cells, and the chained dragon [[Therond]]; [[Therond]] burned it out.
