# The South

**Region of [[Harrenor]]**

Seat [[Stag's Rest]]; [[House Grey]]. Suffered most from [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] [[03 The Jihads|jihads]]. [[Henry Grey]] ruled as [[Henry Grey|King in the South]], sworn to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] since S20, until stabbed at [[Sanctuary]] (S30). Who leads [[House Grey|Greys]] now is unclear. Bannermen: [[House Tempest|Tempest]], [[House Stelaris|Stelaris]], [[House Grimston|Grimston]], [[House Cromwell|Cromwell]]; [[House Douglas|Douglases]] turned on them.
