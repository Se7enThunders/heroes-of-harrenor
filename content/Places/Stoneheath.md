**Walled town** in [[The Lowlands]]  
**Held by:** [[House Grey|Greys]] ([[Audrey Grey]] as regent)  
**First seen:** S1

## What happened here

Held by [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] twin knights James & [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver Stone]] until party and [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]]'s 350 men took it in S3. [[Audrey Grey|Audrey]] then governed it for [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]], banned looting. Townsfolk had been vanishing into the [[The cult temple|western woods]]. North-side sewer culvert: secret way in (told by [[The old trader|old trader]]). Grates up into homes and main stronghold. Command post guarded on the south by longbowmen.
