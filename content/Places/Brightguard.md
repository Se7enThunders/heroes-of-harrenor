**Castle & town** in [[The Swordlands]]  
**Held by:** [[Godric Arden|King Godric]]  
**First seen:** S0

## What happened here

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] held it through a siege in [[04 The 6th Jihad|the 6th Jihad]]. The [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] held it until [[Godric Arden|Godric]] retook it in S0 when [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] & [[Elara]] opened the gates; 15,000 of his men camped around it. Now [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s court; where [[Sophia Grey]] is held. [[House Arden|Arden]] banners show [[The Almighty|Almighty]]'s flame above the lion. Sea cave on beach: hidden route into lower levels (Thomas & [[Gerrit]] knew). Courtyard where a thief of Lord [[William Arden|William]]'s hung for a month. Gothic chapel: [[Father James]] confirmed [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] (S15). Throne room & [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s study; armorer said plate takes months.
