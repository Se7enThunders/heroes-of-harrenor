# The Northeast

**Region of [[Harrenor]]**

Seat Frostmark; [[House Bruce]]. [[Celia Bruce-Arden|Queen Celia]]'s homeland. [[House Bruce|Bruces]] marched with [[Godric Arden|Godric]] to retake [[Brightguard]]. [[Godric Arden|Godric]] wintered here, cut off, after [[Kingsmont]] (S12). Pressed by raids out of [[Varanor]] as of S23. Frostmark never visited.
