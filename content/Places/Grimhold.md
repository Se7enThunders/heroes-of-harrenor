# Grimhold

**Border fortress** in [[The South]]  
**Held by:** [[House Grimston]]  
**First seen:** S6

## What happened here

Fortress at mouth of valley into [[Ra'Haad]]. People tall, hardy, northern-looking; recognize Varanoran. Only 1,000 men in S6; fully manned by S29 when 50 [[House Grimston|Grimston]] knights rescued party. Party's gateway to/from [[Ra'Haad]]. [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s grave just outside walls, kept with fresh flowers. [[Bishop John Speros]]'s church.
