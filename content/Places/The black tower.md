**Sorcerer's tower** in [[Ra'Haad]]  
**Held by:** Destroyed hold of the [[Sable Wardens]]  
**First seen:** S8

## What happened here

Huge tower of smooth black stone ringed by lush green in the desert, 2+ weeks' hard ride from [[Na'Buchar]]. Time runs wrong inside: three weeks passed in what felt like hours. Courtyard: the 9-ft Tower Guardian. Below: [[The mortician|mortician]]'s lab and cells of bled elves ([[Casius]]). Library of magically [[The Blank Scrolls|blank scrolls]]; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] can read the labels ("correspondences," [[Starbinder]]). Top: [[The man in black|man in black]]'s chamber and the [[The Palantir|black orb]] (the [[The Palantir|palantir]]).
