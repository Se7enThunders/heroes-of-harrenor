# Sunwatch

**Castle** in [[The South]]  
**Held by:** Lord [[Theodore Silver]] (in all but name)  
**First seen:** S18

## What happened here

Ruined fort of extinct [[House Aurelius]], wrecked in [[03 The Jihads|jihads]]; ~a week's ride west of [[Oldport]]. Cleared of bandits in S18. [[Brutus]] restoring it: roofs sealed, armory widened, full mess, refugees resettled, a beehive, four men-at-arms (twelve requested). Crypts behind hidden passage lit by ever-burning torches; [[Titus Aurelius]]'s tomb gave up [[Radiance]]. Master's chambers: [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]]'s portrait and the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|Everwhite wardrobe]] that opens to [[Varanor]], with blood on the handle. Old forges, library, chapel in need of masonry.
