# Ashfall

**Ruined city** in [[The Blight]]  
**Held by:** The undead  
**First seen:** S7

## What happened here

Once a great trading hub, now black & grey ruins full of things worse than wights. Statue of an old god of child sacrifice from [[Dharshoon]]'s homeland stands in center. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]]'s safehouse mansion fits horses on 2nd floor. Party killed a wyvern and fled a red dragon here.
