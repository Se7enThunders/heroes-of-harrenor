**Region of [[Harrenor]]**

No seat, no ruler, not a country. the frozen far north, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s homeland. Its raiders sack the coasts and once traded slaves with [[Ra'Haad]]. [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite trees]] grow in the west; their leaves turn red where people are sacrificed. Reached through the [[Sunwatch]] [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]].
