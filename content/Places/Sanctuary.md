**Monastery town**  
**Held by:** [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] & the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]  
**First seen:** S29

## What happened here

~2 weeks north of [[Grimhold]]. Small town around its church, monastery buildings much of it. Fields barren as anywhere war touched. Site of [[23 The Sanctuary Betrayal|Sanctuary betrayal]] (S30). Church: clergy offered bread, wine, guest rights but were [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers in disguise; [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] refused [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s terms and sprang trap; [[Jean Sanderfell]] stabbed [[Henry Grey|Henry]]; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] & [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] taken. Fields: [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]]'s army (larger than [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s) waited to the north; [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] & [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] forces routed [[House Grey|Grey]] camp while party fought free.
