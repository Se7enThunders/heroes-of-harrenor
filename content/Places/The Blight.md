# The Blight

**Region of [[Harrenor]]**

No seat; no one; cursed land. Massive desert between [[Ra'Haad]] and [[Othkar]], cursed since [[02 The Calamity|the Calamity]]. Animals turn skittish; wights, wyverns, floating shadow spirits, a red dragon roam it. Old wells still give good water.
