# Sable Wardens

**Stance, status, first seen:** enemy, S8  
**Signs:** black clothing; wrist tattoo of an eye w/ star above & flame below; black stone towers & black crystal orbs  
**Members:** [[The man in black|man in black]] (dead); [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] (deserter)  
**What they want:** immortality via elven blood & [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaidens]]; very old; [[Telandor]] scroll ties them to crime & trafficking in the east

## Run-ins

[[The black tower|black tower]] bloodletting (S8-9); recognized [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and ordered him spared (his vision calls him a "dark stricken creature"); early Wardens died in [[Telandor]]; hired [[The bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] & [[The Janissary|Janissary]] in [[Oldport]] (S20); [[Constantine]] and a dead man in [[Oldport]]'s [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] camp bore their tattoo.

## In the politics

Leans toward: hidden. After: immortality via elven blood and the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaidens]]. Seen: [[The man in black|man in black]] & [[The black tower|black tower]]; early Wardens dead in [[Telandor]]; [[The bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] on [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]; [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] is a deserter. Their [[The Black Hands|Black Hands]] want to become gods.
