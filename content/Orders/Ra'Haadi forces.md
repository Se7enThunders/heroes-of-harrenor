# Ra'Haadi forces

**Stance, status, first seen:** Enemy, massing, S6  
**Signs:** Scimitars, mounted archers, heavy cataphracts, Janissaries, northman-like berserkers.  
**Members:** The [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan|Emir]]'s guards; elite cataphracts in the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] lair; a [[The Janissary|Janissary]] now jailed by the [[House Grey|Greys]].  
**What they want:** [[22 The 7th Jihad|The 7th Jihad]]. 100,000+ men marching on [[Grimhold]]; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] expects an attack in force within a month (S30).

## Run-ins

Sacked [[Oldport]] and [[Stag's Rest]]. Hunted the [[Larad]] refugees. Ran the party down outside [[Grimhold]] (S29).
