**Stance, status, first seen:** enemy, S4  
**Signs:** abyssal flame emblem carved into victims & worn as medallion; victims' eyes burned out, hearts taken; walls of fire  
**Members:** unnamed cultists, the [[Na'Buchar]] slave buyer  
**What they want:** put evil gods in control via human sacrifice & demons; believe a man who sacrificed enough rose to the right hand of "the enemy"

## Run-ins

temple W of [[Stoneheath]] (S4); lair under [[Na'Buchar]] church w/ howlers, [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] guards, chained dragon [[Therond]] they tried to make break his vows (S10); spreading even into [[Varanor]]; dead with [[The Black Hands|Black Hands]] at [[Varanor]] bell temple; cultists in [[Telandor]] catacombs with a [[The Teleport Amulet|teleport amulet]] and a crimson [[The Crimson Gladius|life-stealing gladius]].

## In the politics

Leans toward: hidden. After: put their evil gods in control of the world via sacrifice and demons. Seen: [[Stoneheath]] woods temple, [[Na'Buchar]] church lair, [[Varanor]]. At war with [[The Black Hands|the Black Hands]] for ages.
