**Stance, status, first seen:** Enemy, active, S12  
**Signs:** Black garb and good armor; greatswords that burn with fire. When they fall, the fire passes to their leader, who rises again.  
**Members:** Unnamed assassins.  
**What they want:** Sworn to the [[Sable Wardens]]; want to become gods.

## Run-ins

Ambushed the party in [[Giant's Gap]] to avenge [[The man in black|the man in black]] (S12). [[Artorius]] killed one the night before the party met him. At war with the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|Cult]] for ages; dead together at the [[Varanor]] bell temple.
