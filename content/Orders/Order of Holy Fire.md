# Order of Holy Fire

**Stance, status, first seen:** ally, active, S1  
**Signs:** red masks & fire robes ([[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]]); crimson armor trimmed gold ([[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]]); fire emblem  
**Members:** [[Baylor Stelaris]], [[Sir Ignatius]]; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wants to join  
**What they want:** fight [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]] & darkness; members must follow [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] and have holy fire show in their soul; a knight can serve a king and the Order at once

## Run-ins

[[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] burned a summoned demon & disintegrated a [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] dagger near [[Stoneheath]] (S4); Order declared itself in party's debt and gave each member an item ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s [[Bracers of the Holy Order|bracers]]). [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] now advises [[Godric Arden|Godric]] (banners added [[The Almighty|Almighty]]'s flame). [[Artorius]] wears a crimson breastplate but says he isn't a member.

## In the politics

Leans toward: [[Godric Arden|Godric]]. After: fight the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|Cult]] and the darkness. Seen: [[Baylor Stelaris]]; [[Sir Ignatius]] advising [[Godric Arden|Godric]], whose banners now show [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]'s flame. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hopes to join.
