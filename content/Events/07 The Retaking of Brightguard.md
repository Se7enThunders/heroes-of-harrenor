# The Retaking of Brightguard

**When:** Session 0

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]], with the veterans [[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]], slipped into [[Brightguard]] through the sea cave, set a fire as a diversion and opened the gates. [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s army took the Arden seat back from the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] and he was hailed "King Godric I."

- [[Elara]]
- [[Godric Arden]]

- [[Gerrit]]
