**When:** Session 15

Guided by his dream of [[Beladishpi]], [[Dharshoon]] climbed the tower of [[Bell temple of the Swordlands|the Swordlands bell temple]], threw his family's idol onto the rocks below and rang the perfect, uninscribed bell. [[Anri]], first handmaiden of [[Lady Seraphyne]], appeared behind him.
