# The Fall of Telandor

**When:** ~3,000 years ago

[[Telandor]] fell to "winged fire" after [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]] betrayed it. [[Guinevere]] slept with the [[The Kilns|kiln]], her slumber holding the attackers' curse; [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]] fought on below.
