**When:** Session 24

In the hilltop church of [[Telandor]], [[Dharshoon]] woke the sleeping Lady, [[Guinevere]]. [[The Kilns|Her kiln]] went dark, she turned to bone, and the city's illusion fell away into true ruin. Her slumber had held the attackers' curse. The guide laughed that the kiln was spilled, and [[The molten demon|the molten demon]] rose.
