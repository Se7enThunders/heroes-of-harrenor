**When:** Just before Session 0

[[Edward Grey]], ruling for the grieving [[Godfrey Arden|King Godfrey]], called [[William Arden|William]] a bastard, the son of [[Sir Avery Mortimer]]. [[Catherine Lancaster]] captured him and William executed him for treason. The news reached [[Godric Arden|Godric]] at [[The Rampart]] in Session 0, along with word that [[Henry Grey]] had declared the South independent. It is the spark of the whole civil war.
