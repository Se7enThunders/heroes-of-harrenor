# The Slaying of Goroth

**When:** Session 16

Deep beneath the bell temple, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] split the head of the dragon [[Ergond]], then put his blade through [[Goroth]], the dragon master with wings of red lightning, who swore to bring Joran's "bastard head to father." Goroth died a skeletal husk. Joran kept [[Goroth's Helm|his helm]], and learned they shared a father.

## Who was there

- [[Joran Jotunsson]]
- [[Goroth]]
- [[Ergond]]
- [[Anri]]
