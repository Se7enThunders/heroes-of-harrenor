# The Winter Rebellion

**When:** Before the campaign

Minor houses rose; the [[House Arden|Ardens]], [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] and [[House Grey|Greys]] put them down. [[George Arden]] died; [[Godric Arden|Godric]] re-formed his forces. [[Titus Lancaster]] besieged [[House White]] and destroyed it.
