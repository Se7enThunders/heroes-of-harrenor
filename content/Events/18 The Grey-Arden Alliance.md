**When:** Session 20

In [[Oldport]], [[Henry Grey]] accepted [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s terms, delivered by [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]: fealty in exchange for his sister [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]], keeping his title as King in the South. Joran declared his task for Godric accomplished. Theo became Lord of [[Sunwatch]] in all but name.
