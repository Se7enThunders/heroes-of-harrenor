# The Larad Exodus

**When:** S11

[[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s freed slaves fled [[Larad]] across open desert; [[Therond]] turned the sand to glass to save them. [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] died of his wounds; ~600 reached [[Grimhold]].
