# The Fall of the Black Tower

**When:** Sessions 8-9

Tracking the abducted [[Elia]] across the desert, the party stormed [[The black tower]] of the [[Sable Wardens]]. They shattered its guardian, stopped [[The mortician]] bleeding elves, lost three weeks to the tower's tricks of time, and saw visions. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] killed [[The man in black]], who warned with his last breath that [[Larad]] had been found. They came away with [[The Palantir|the palantir]] and [[The Blank Scrolls|the blank scrolls]].
