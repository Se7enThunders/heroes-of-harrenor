**When:** Session 5

At the burning hamlet near [[Stoneheath]], [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] found his cousin [[Ulf]] leading [[Leo Lancaster]]'s raiders. Ulf shot Joran's horse out from under him; Joran dropped him. "Well played." "It's not a game." Joran took his head, and later set it before [[Lord Callum Douglas]], who called him a kinslayer. The first of Joran's own blood to die by his hand.
