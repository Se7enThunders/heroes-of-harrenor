**When:** Sessions 27-28

In [[Telandor]]'s throne room, [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]] snapped the neck of [[The Iron Fist's captive|his captive handmaiden]] and told [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] they shared a father. He grew with [[The Iron Fist's Ring|his ring]] and slammed Joran into stone, but Joran buried his sword to the hilt, held his half-brother as he died ("I wished being brothers had meant something other than this"), then broke his neck.
