# The Freeing of Therond

**When:** Session 10

Beneath a ruined church in [[Na'Buchar]], in the lair of the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]], the party rescued [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] and found the ancient dragon [[Therond]] chained and bound by vows of penance. [[Theo's Talisman|Theo's talisman]] opened his chains. Therond burned the lair out and pledged his aid to Beatrice.
