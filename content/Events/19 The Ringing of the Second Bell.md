# The Ringing of the Second Bell

**When:** Session 22

Through [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|the wardrobe]] in [[Varanor]], the party fought the dead with [[Artorius]] at [[Bell temple of Varanor|the temple of bells]]. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] destroyed [[The undead knight|the undead knight]] and claimed [[The Black Ring|the black ring]]. [[Dharshoon]] rang the bell; then [[Leana]] came out of the snow with her dire wolves and told them of a third. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] cursed his father in the name of the true God.
