# The Sanctuary Betrayal

**When:** S30

[[Henry Grey]] went to [[Sanctuary]] to take [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]]'s surrender. [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers posing as clergy sprang the trap; [[Jean Sanderfell]] stabbed [[Henry Grey|Henry]] "with [[Titus Lancaster]]'s regards"; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] were taken; the [[House Grey|Grey]] army was routed.
