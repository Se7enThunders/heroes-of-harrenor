**When:** Session 15

In the gothic chapel at [[Brightguard]], [[Father James]] walked [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] through the prayer and he "rose a new man," a follower of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]. That same day [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] pinned [[Brooch of the Almighty|the brooch of the Almighty]] on his armor. The turning point of Joran's story.
