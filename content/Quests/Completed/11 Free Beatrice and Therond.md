# Free Beatrice and Therond

**Quest 11** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S10

**Goal:** Free [[Beatrice Silver]], [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s sister, from the slavers and the cult that bought her.

**What happened:** The slave market sale led to a ruined church and a cult lair. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s tattoos glowed and he grew to nearly eight feet, scooping up women and charging past a demon. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] used the talisman to free the chained dragon [[Therond]], who burned the lair and pledged Beatrice his aid.
