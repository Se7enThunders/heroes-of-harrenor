**Quest 1** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S0

**Goal:** Retake [[Brightguard]] for [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] before the Lancasters could dig in.

**What happened:** The party slipped in through a sea cave with [[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]], lit a fire as a diversion, and opened the gate: "King Godric!" The Lancaster banners were burned. A rider then brought the news that [[Edward Grey]] had been executed and [[Henry Grey|Henry]] had declared the South independent.
