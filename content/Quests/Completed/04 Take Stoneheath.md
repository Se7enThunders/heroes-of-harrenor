**Quest 4** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S3-S4

**Goal:** Take the city of [[Stoneheath]] from the Stone twins.

**What happened:** The party crept in through a sewer culvert; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] lifted [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] through the grate. Raouen was caught and made to duel. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] hewed a plate knight in half with Starbinder. Both [[Sir James Stone|James]] and [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver Stone]] died. Joran buried commoners and sent knights home. [[Audrey Grey]] then forbade looting.
