**Quest 16** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S15-S22

**Goal:** Ring the bells of the old temples for the Almighty. The third bell is still to be found.

**What happened:** [[Dharshoon]] rang the first in the Swordlands and [[Anri]] appeared. In S21 the party stepped through the wardrobe to [[Varanor]] and met [[Artorius]]. In the temple there an undead knight dropped [[The Black Ring]]. The bell rang with nothing visible; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] prayed and felt peace. [[Leana]] told them of the third.
