# Wake Guinevere

**Quest 22** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S23-S28

**Goal:** Go to [[Telandor]], wake [[Guinevere]] and find out about the kiln.

**What happened:** [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] swelled to block the bridge and rang the bell-gate. Dharshoon woke Guinevere; the kiln died and she crumbled to bone, revealing the city as a ruin. The party killed the horned molten demon, fought the Grand Marshal's undead and escaped the dragon. In the throne room [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] killed the [[The Iron Fist|Iron Fist]], who said they shared a father. [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] joined them.
