# Avenge House Hay

**Quest 15** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S15-S16

**Goal:** Learn what killed about 20 men of [[House Hay]] in the Swordlands bell temple.

**What happened:** The killers were the dragon [[Ergond]] and the dark knight [[Goroth]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] severed Ergond's head, then drove his blade through Goroth, who died a skeletal husk after swearing to bring Joran's "bastard head to father." Joran kept [[Goroth's Helm]].
