**Quest 2** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S0-S21

**Goal:** Ride south with a sealed letter and win [[Henry Grey]] back to the king's side.

**What happened:** Henry first refused to kneel but was open to alliance. After a long road that included Stoneheath and the Larad trip, he accepted [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s terms in S20: his sister back for his fealty, and he kept the kingly title in the South. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] declared the task accomplished in S21 and bought food and drink for everyone.
