**Quest 7** · **Status:** <span class="st-alive">COMPLETED</span> · **Sessions:** S6-S8

**Goal:** Henry's charge: stop the raids, which meant riding into [[Ra'Haad]] itself.

**What happened:** [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] proposed the slaver disguise: the party as slaves and an envoy. They crossed [[The Blight]] past wights and wyverns, saw the crucifixions, and reached [[Na'Buchar]]. There [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] revealed he was [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]]'s spy and read the coded message ("the hunt has begun"). [[Salim al Muqtar]] was behind it. The raids were not stopped, but the party learned who was moving the pieces.
