# Find Draven's Sister and Relight the Flame

**Quest 26** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-3">BEACON 3</span> · **Since:** Session 29

**Goal:** Find the next [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]], [[Draven's sister]], and learn how the dead flame of [[Lady Seraphyne]] can be relit.

**Why this level:** This feels like the key to the old powers, but nothing here forces a deadline. It matters more once the war and the rescue are settled.

## What we know

- [[Leana]] said the kiln's flame is dead and only Seraphyne can relight it (S29).
- The next handmaiden is Draven's sister. Draven never knew her and thinks she is a half-sister through his father, a scoundrel city lord in the east.
- A second kiln, dun and lightless, sat on the altar in [[Telandor]]. Opening it revealed a stair (S28).
- Joran knelt before Leana at Sanctuary and threw sticks for her wolves.

## Leads

- [[Leana]] saw [[Artorius]] a few weeks before S29 and may visit again.
- Draven's father is in a city in the east. Draven's own past is the way in.
