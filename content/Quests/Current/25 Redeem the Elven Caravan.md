# Redeem the Elven Caravan

**Quest 25** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-4">BEACON 4</span> · **Since:** Session 29

**Goal:** Find the Bloody Knives and free the elven women the party watched pass and let go in S29.

**Why this level:** Joran has not forgiven himself, but the caravan has moved on and the Jihad is coming. It is Joran's quest more than the table's.

## What we know

- A Bloody Knives caravan of elven women with wildfire: about 30 slavers in the open, a camp twice that with a Ra'Haadi escort (S29, per Hadron).
- The party rode on to warn Grimhold and was chased by Ra'Haadi riders.
- Joran has not forgiven himself for riding on.

## Leads

- Whoever buys elves in Ra'Haad: [[Na'Buchar]]'s slave market, where Beatrice was sold, is the first place to look.
