**Quest 10** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-3">BEACON 3</span> · **Since:** Session 8

**Goal:** End the Sable Wardens and the Black Hands for good: their hunt for immortality, their elven-blood trade, and the bounty on Draven.

**Why this level:** The Wardens keep returning (black tower, Telandor, a bounty hunter) but they are a slow threat. Ranked below the war and the immediate rescues.

## What we know

- Wardens seek immortality through elven blood and the handmaidens. The Black Hands want to become gods.
- [[The man in black]] (S9) is dead and the black tower is gone.
- A bounty hunter on Draven was hired by the Sable Wardens (S20). [[The Janissary]] confirmed it.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] is a Sable Warden deserter who joined to see them thwarted.
- Telandor's archive recorded Warden crimes (S26).

## Leads

- Hadron knows how the Wardens are organized and who is left.
- [[The Janissary]] is held by the Greys. Whether his captors survived Sanctuary is unknown.
