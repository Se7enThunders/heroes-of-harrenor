**Quest 29** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-2">BEACON 2</span> · **Since:** Session 30

**Goal:** Make the Douglases and Lancasters answer for the murder at Sanctuary, and let the other houses see who really gave the order.

**Why this level:** Not as time-critical as the rescues or the Jihad, but the betrayal split the realm and hands the Lancasters the South if it goes unanswered.

## What we know

- [[Lord Callum Douglas]] pretended to surrender, then sprang a Lancaster-backed trap. Lancaster soldiers dressed as clergy opened it.
- [[Jean Sanderfell]] stabbed Henry "with Titus Lancaster's regards." A Lancaster knight struck the king down.
- Douglas men had already attacked Godric's men before Sanctuary. Douglas's larger army then routed the Grey camp.
- [[Titus Lancaster]] holds the real power behind [[William Arden|King William]] in Kingsmont.
- The party concluded the Lancasters paid raiders and so told Ra'Haad how weak Harrenor was (S30).

## Leads

- [[Sir Eamon Douglas]] was a hostage traded for [[Robert Lancaster]], and may still be with the Greys. He is leverage against Douglas.
- Find proof of the Lancaster-paid raids and put it before the houses and [[Godric Arden|Godric]].
- Raouen's father Jean is the knife in the story. That makes this personal for Raouen.
