**Quest 20** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-3">BEACON 3</span> · **Since:** Session 22

**Goal:** Find and ring the third bell, far south in the desert sands. When all three ring, the Seraphim may return.

**Why this level:** Big payoff, but the bell is in the far desert and Ra'Haad stands between the party and it. It also has no clock on it.

## What we know

- First bell: Swordlands temple (S15). [[Dharshoon]] rang it and [[Anri]] appeared.
- Second bell: the [[Bell temple of Varanor]] (S22). Nothing visible happened; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] prayed and felt peace.
- [[Leana]] told them the third bell lies far south in the desert sands.
- Dharshoon dreamed of bells, white trees with red leaves, and angels trapped (S19).
- Joran copied [[The Old Church Map]] (S23): old churches across the land, mostly in the north, few in the south.

## Leads

- The party knows the way into the desert from the Larad and Na'Buchar trips. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim]] knows the Blight.
- The church map might show a temple in the south.
