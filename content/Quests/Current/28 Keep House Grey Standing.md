**Quest 28** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-2">BEACON 2</span> · **Since:** Session 30

**Goal:** Stop the South from collapsing now that [[Henry Grey]] has fallen, and keep [[Sophia Grey]] safe, because she may now be the Greys' best claim.

**Why this level:** The South suffered most from the jihads and is now leaderless in front of the biggest one yet. This is urgent, but it needs the army and politics side of the party, so it sits just under the two rescues.

## What we know

- Henry was stabbed at [[Sanctuary]] and is presumed dead. His brother [[Carter Grey]] already died when [[Stag's Rest]] was sacked.
- Who leads the South is unclear. What is left: [[Audrey Grey]], Sophia (held at [[Brightguard]] as [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s surety), and the southern lords.
- Grey bannermen: [[House Tempest]], [[House Stelaris]] and [[House Grimston]] are retreating; [[House Cromwell]] is the only house at full strength.
- Draven's vision (S22) showed Sophia carried off by unknown men. [[Sir Garrik Ryder]] broke with the Lancasters to protect her.
- The betrothals tied to Henry (Meredith Douglas for Henry, a Douglas son for Sophia) are void.

## Leads

- Reach [[Audrey Grey]] and the southern lords before Cromwell and the others choose a side.
- Cromwell and Douglas were seen talking often at Oldport (S20). Nothing ties Cromwell to the betrayal yet, so watch him.
- [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] broke fealty to William to protect Sophia, but would not join Godric. He could still help guard her.
