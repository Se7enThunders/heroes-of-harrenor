# Free Theo and Raouen

**Quest 27** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-1">BEACON 1</span> · **Since:** Session 30

**Goal:** Get [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] out of Lancaster and Douglas hands alive. At Sanctuary [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] begged the others to retreat and left them behind. His vow: "let me be the one who opens their cage."

**Why this level:** Two party members are prisoners right now, and the people holding them just murdered a king under guest rights. [[Edward Grey]] was executed by the same side. Every day gives the captors more time to decide what to do with them. This competes with the Grimhold quest for the party's time, which is why the two are both at the top level.

## What we know

- Taken at [[Sanctuary]] (S30) in the Douglas + Lancaster ambush. Raouen was spared on his father's order; Theo was taken alive.
- [[Jean Sanderfell]], Raouen's father, is the man who stabbed [[Henry Grey]] "with Titus Lancaster's regards."
- Theo is Lord of [[Sunwatch]]. At the council [[Henry Grey|Henry]] asked for Theo and Raouen by name ("Names draw arrows").
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is also [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]]'s spy, and Baring holds his sister [[Alanna Selmy]]. He is worth a lot to more than one side.
- Retreated with the Grey survivors: [[Dharshoon]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] and Joran.

## Leads

- [[Sir Eamon Douglas]] may still be held by the Greys (unconfirmed). He would be leverage against [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]].
- [[Brutus]] at [[Sunwatch]] can be reached for men or news, if the party has any way to send a message.
- Where the prisoners are being held is not in the notes yet.
