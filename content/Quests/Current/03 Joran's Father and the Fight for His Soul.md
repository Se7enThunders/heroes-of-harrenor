# Joran's Father and the Fight for His Soul

**Quest 3** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-4">BEACON 4</span> · **Since:** Session 0

**Goal:** Face the dark figure who calls himself Joran's father, and win the war for Joran's soul.

**Why this level:** The biggest arc in the campaign, but the visions and half-siblings have been warnings, not a countdown. Nothing here forces action before the Jihad.

## What we know

- A tall thin figure in Joran's visions: "I am thy father. Thou art my progeny." He offered Joran conquest of Varanor and Joran said no (S16.5).
- [[Goroth]] and the [[The Iron Fist|Iron Fist]] both claimed to share Joran's father. Both are dead, and Joran killed both.
- [[Sir Ignatius]]'s guess: the father is the lieutenant of a fallen archangel.
- In S22 Joran shouted at the sky: he will lose, and Joran prays to be the instrument of his destruction.
- Joran's own words: "The war is not just for a throne. It is for my soul."

## Leads

- [[Artorius]] has warned of the coming darkness and Joran has asked him twice whether he is an angel.
- The Everwhite wardrobe at [[Sunwatch]] leads to [[Varanor]], where the cult is spreading.
