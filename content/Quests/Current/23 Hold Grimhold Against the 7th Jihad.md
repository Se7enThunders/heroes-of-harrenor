**Quest 23** · **Status:** <span class="st-captive">CURRENT</span> · **Urgency:** <span class="bc-1">BEACON 1</span> · **Since:** Session 28

**Goal:** Help [[Grimhold]] survive the Ra'Haadi host and warn the rest of Harrenor in time. The border fortress is the party's gateway to and from [[Ra'Haad]] and the first thing in the way.

**Why this level:** 100,000+ men were seen massing at the border (S28). Theo expects an attack in force within a month (S30). This is the largest threat on the board, but there is some time, and the party already warned [[Stag's Rest]] and [[Sunwatch]].

## What we know

- [[Ra'Haadi forces]] mass at the border; the 7th Jihad is coming. The camp was relatively unorganized at first sight (S29, [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]]).
- Grimhold had only about 1,000 men in S6, but by S29 it was fully manned and [[Torren Grimston]] brought 50 knights to rescue the party.
- About 600 freed slaves from [[Larad]] settled at Grimhold (S12), and [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s grave is just outside the walls.
- The Grey army broke at Sanctuary. [[House Grey]] is leaderless, so the South cannot be counted on.
- The Lancasters likely told Ra'Haad how weak Harrenor was by paying raiders to hit its towns.

## Leads

- [[Beatrice Silver]] can call [[Therond]], who pledged her his aid. Joran once suggested enlisting them for the war (S12).
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim]] knows the Blight and the Ra'Haadi routes.
- Persuade the surviving Grey bannermen ([[House Tempest]], [[House Stelaris]], [[House Grimston]]) to hold the line together.
- Send word to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] about the host, though he is weeks from the fight.
