**mysterious ancient warrior**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S13

## What we know

Strangely warm hand, very old gear, ruby-pommel sword in gilded scabbard "gifted in the line of duty," crimson breastplate though not in [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]]. Moves inhumanly fast, calls down holy fire. Guided party through [[Varanor]]; warned of ancient darkness returning. Bell temple was warded long ago by a "Sir Artoreus." His camp beyond the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] now empty.
