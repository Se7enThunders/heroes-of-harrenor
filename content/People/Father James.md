**Bishop of [[Brightguard]]**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S15

## What we know

Tends gothic chapel at [[Brightguard]]. Confirmed [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] into the faith of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]. Told [[Dharshoon]] his dream fit the theology and to destroy his idol. Doesn't interpret dreams; warned [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] not to trust them too much. Also found at [[Oldport]]'s cathedral (S23), where he let [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] copy the old [[The Old Church Map|church map]].
