**[[House Arden|Arden]] knight sworn to [[Godric Arden|Godric]]**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S5.5

## What we know

Interesting-looking longsword. Party fought under him as battalion lord at [[Kingsmont]]; rallied men to protect the king. Seen at [[Brightguard]] (S14, S16) and outside [[Oldport|Stormbreaker Castle]] in [[Oldport]] with [[Lord Adam Croft|Lord Croft]] (S23). Not the teen son from S0 (confirmed by the DM). His father, [[Lord William Marshall]], is one of [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s most trusted vassals.
