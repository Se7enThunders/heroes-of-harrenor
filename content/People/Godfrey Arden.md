# Godfrey Arden

**former King of [[Harrenor]]; Warden of [[Brightguard]] & [[The Stormlands|Stormlands]]**  
Status: <span class="st-dead">DEAD</span>, First seen: Lore

## What we know

Drove [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] out; married [[Catherine Lancaster]]. Relieved [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s siege at [[Brightguard]], sent him after [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] fleet; disappointed when [[Godric Arden|Godric]] couldn't finish it, gave [[Brightguard]] to [[George Arden|George]]. Depression after [[George Arden|George]] died, made [[Edward Grey]] his second. Died of mysterious illness; [[Godric Arden|Godric]] suspects [[Catherine Lancaster|Catherine]] had him killed.
