# Joran Jotunsson

![[Joran Jotunsson portrait.png|320]]

**Northman of Varanor, fighter (Battle Master), Jordan**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S0

## What we know

[[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s personal guard (~2 yrs at S0). From [[Varanor]]; became follower of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] in S15. Has killed cousin [[Ulf]] and half-brother [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]. Haunted by visions of his father.

## Backstory

- Son of the North ([[Varanor]]); raised in warrior culture; grew to reject raiding/pillaging; became outcast.
- Stowed away on ship south to [[Harrenor]]; at [[Godric Arden|Lord Godric]] [[House Arden|Arden]]'s estate found fellow Northmen came as invaders; sided with defenders, earned [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s respect.
- Now member of [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s personal guard; protector of [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s children, esp. young daughter (surrogate niece reminding him of what he lost). Civil war looming. Name often mispronounced by southerners.
