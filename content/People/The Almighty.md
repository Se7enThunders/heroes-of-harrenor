**The one true God**  
Status: —, First seen: S8

## What we know

Worshiped in [[Harrenor]]; outlawed in [[Ra'Haad]] since its conquest. Teaches resurrection. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] was confirmed into the faith at [[Brightguard]] (S15) and asked that the old gods' claim on him be severed (S21). [[Beladishpi]] and [[Lady Seraphyne]] both serve Him; [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s banners now bear His flame.
