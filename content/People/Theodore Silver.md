**Human rogue, Preston**  
Status: <span class="st-captive">CAPTIVE</span>, First seen: S1

## What we know

From [[Oldport]]; father [[Matthew Silver|Matthew]], sister [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]]. Lord of [[Sunwatch]] in all but name; bears eternium sword [[Radiance]]. Won [[17 Theo's Trial by Combat|trial by combat]] for [[House Tempest]] (S19). [[Henry Grey]] asked for him by name; briefed lords on [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] host and rallied party before [[Sanctuary]]. Taken prisoner by [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] & [[House Douglas|Douglases]] in [[Sanctuary]] church (S30). Lost 50gp to [[Roose Cromwell]] at darts.
