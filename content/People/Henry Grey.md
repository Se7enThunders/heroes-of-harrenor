**rebel king, then [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s ally**  
Status: <span class="st-dead">DEAD (presumed)</span>, First seen: S0

## What we know

Declared himself king after [[Edward Grey]]'s execution; refused to bend knee in S1. Lost a third of his footmen at [[Green Harbor]], then retook [[Oldport]] and [[Stag's Rest]]. Accepted [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s terms (S20). Promised [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] official lordship of [[Sunwatch]] after the war. Sent envoys across [[Harrenor]] seeking truce vs [[Ra'Haad]]; doubted [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] were behind invasion. Went to [[Sanctuary]] to take [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]]'s surrender, walked into trap: stabbed by [[Jean Sanderfell]] (S30). Presumed dead; army routed.
