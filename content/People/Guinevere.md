# Guinevere

**Lady of [[Telandor]] (not a [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]])**  
Status: <span class="st-dead">DEAD</span>, First seen: S24

## What we know

Auburn hair, white robes, asleep with the [[The Kilns|kiln]] for 3,000 years. Her slumber held the curse the attackers laid on [[Telandor]]. Woke when [[Dharshoon]] touched her and turned to bones, and the city's true ruin showed. [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] had come looking for her and took her [[The Kilns|kiln]].
