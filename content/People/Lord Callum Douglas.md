# Lord Callum Douglas

**Lord of [[The Lowlands|the Lowlands]] ([[Red Den]])**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S5

## What we know

Banner red fox on green. Older, not much of a soldier; disdainful; party thinks he's racist. History with [[Elara]]. Called [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] a kinslayer. Wanted his children married into royalty. Conferred with [[House Cromwell|Cromwell]]; one of his men passed parchment to a [[The Mockingbird|mockingbird]] courier. At [[Sanctuary]] (S30) mocked [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], laughed off [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s surrender terms, revealed deal with [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]], cried "Kill them!" His army, larger than [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s, fell on the [[House Grey|Grey]] camp. Traitor to [[House Grey|Grey]] and [[Godric Arden|Godric]].
