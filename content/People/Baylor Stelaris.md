# Baylor Stelaris

**[[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]]'s twin; [[Order of Holy Fire]]**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S1

## What we know

Red mask and fire robes. Wanted [[Starbinder]] but wasn't heir, so found [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] through another relic. Burned a summoned demon and disintegrated a ritual dagger (S4). Gave [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] the [[Bracers of the Holy Order]]. Says joining [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] requires holy fire to manifest in your soul.
