# Raouen Sanderfell

![[Raouen portrait.jpeg|260]]

**Human rogue, Josh**  
Status: <span class="st-captive">CAPTIVE</span>, First seen: S1

## What we know

Son of [[Jean Sanderfell]] ([[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] bannerman); former spy for [[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Baring]], came clean S8. Sister [[Alanna Selmy|Alanna]] is [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]]'s captive. Can sense/use the [[The Palantir|palantir]]. At [[Sanctuary]] (S30) spotted trap, watched his own father stab [[Henry Grey]]; father ordered him spared; Raouen spat in his face, carried off prisoner. Visions of "someone who looks like Raouen" killing [[Henry Grey|Henry]] were about his father.
