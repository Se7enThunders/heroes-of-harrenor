Status: <span class="st-dead">DEAD</span>, First seen: S15

Promised to bring [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s "bastard head to father," so likely shares [[Joran's father]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] killed him and kept the helm.
