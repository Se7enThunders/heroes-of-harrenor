# Godric Arden

**middle brother; King of [[Harrenor]]; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s liege**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S0

## What we know

Given [[Brightguard]] in [[04 The 6th Jihad|6th Jihad]], held it under siege until [[Godfrey Arden|Godfrey]] arrived. Sent to destroy [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] fleet holding [[The Green Isles|the Green Isles]]; beat it but couldn't destroy it, so [[Godfrey Arden|Godfrey]] gave [[Brightguard]] to [[George Arden|George]]; Godric got [[The Rampart|the Rampart]] of [[The Green Isles|the Green Isles]] instead. Believes [[Edward Grey]] was right that [[William Arden|William]] is a bastard and throne is owed to him; declared [[William Arden|William]] a bastard and took the crown. Suspects [[Catherine Lancaster|Catherine]] had [[Godfrey Arden|Godfrey]] killed to cover her sin. Retook [[Brightguard]] (S0); lost 10,000 men when [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]] broke his [[10 The Siege of Kingsmont|siege of Kingsmont]] (S5.5). Banner now adds [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]'s flame above the lion. Growing wary/short-tempered; keeps [[Sophia Grey]] as surety for [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s fealty. Uses citrus juice to reveal hidden messages. [[Henry Grey|Henry]] expected him at [[Sanctuary]] but [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] said he was weeks away (S30). Godric was coming, but was not as close as the [[House Grey|Greys]] were led to believe; [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]]'s claim may also have been a lie.
