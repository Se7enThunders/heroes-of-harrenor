**southern knight; wielder of [[Starbinder]]**  
Status: <span class="st-alive">ALIVE</span>, First seen: S1

## What we know

Primary heir of old southern [[House Stelaris]] (sigil: upward sword, purple field). [[Starbinder]] is aeternium, shines with starlight; dispelled [[The molten demon|molten demon]]'s darkness. Took [[Stoneheath]] with 350 men. Traveled with party since S23; says his hope has dimmed but not gone. Vision shows him dead on a rampart.
