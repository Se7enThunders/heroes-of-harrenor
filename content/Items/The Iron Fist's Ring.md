# The Iron Fist's Ring

**Where it is:** [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]]  
**First seen:** S28

Made [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]] grow. Adds 1d8 necrotic to a blow struck in anger; the price, if your will slips, is hearing the voices of everyone you killed that day. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] pocketed it, wouldn't wear it, and gave it to [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] (S29).
