Aeternium greatsword of [[House Stelaris]]: indestructible, never dulls, shines with starlight. Burst [[The molten demon|the molten demon]]'s darkness in [[Telandor]]. [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] wanted it but wasn't the heir. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] saw an image of it on [[The black tower|the black tower]]'s scroll labels.
