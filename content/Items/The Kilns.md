# The Kilns

**Where it is:** [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] ([[Guinevere]]'s); the altar beneath [[Telandor]] (the captive's)  
**First seen:** S24

Half-spheres holding an ember of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]'s fire, kept by the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaidens]]. [[Guinevere]]'s went dark when she woke; [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] carries it. [[The Iron Fist]]'s captive's kiln, set on the altar beneath [[Telandor]], opened the way down and was left there. Only [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]] can relight them.
