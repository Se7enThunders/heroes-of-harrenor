**Where it is:** Western [[Varanor]]; beneath [[Telandor]]  
**First seen:** S18

Their leaves turn red where people are sacrificed. One grows under [[Telandor]] in perpetual moonlight, guarded by the dead [[The Watcher|Watcher]]. [[St. Maximian]] built a church from one.
