**Quests in play:** [[06 Relieve Kingsmont|Relieve Kingsmont]]

Party: Traveling with [[Elara]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], [[Dharshoon]].
[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]: Needs maul/armor; Savage Attacker reminder.

Summary:

[[Stoneheath]] taken; [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] defeated; party rewarded.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gifted [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Order]] [[Bracers of the Holy Order|bracers]].

Meets [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] (arrogant/racist); tasked to save a hamlet.

Hamlet attacked by [[Leo Lancaster]]’s mercs; [[Ulf]] ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]’s cousin) leads them.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] kills [[Ulf]]; mercs scatter; [[Constantine]] ([[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]’s past) escapes.

Party aids village; earns 150 GP.

Debrief with [[Audrey Grey|Lady Grey]]/[[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]]; [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] vouched for.

Negotiations: marriage alliances with [[House Douglas]].

Resting; awaiting next orders.

## Now

- [[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tasked with heading back to [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] [[House Arden|Arden]].
- We say our goodbyes to our comrades and head east.
- Not a lot of fighting along the way.
- Towards the end of the journey we stop at an inn to clean up and relax for a night.
- Inn is off the side of the road on the border of [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]] and Guilded Lands.
	- [[Elara]] changes her appearance to be human before we go inside. ![[Session 5.5 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- Inside we see some armed men. Maybe mercs or armed merchants.
	- Some jobs for both armies are posted on the board.
	- The place seems busy. The innkeeper says the war hasn't gotten this far yet.
	- A sellsword walks up to us and asks if he have a side in the war, looking for better pay. ![[Session 5.5 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- He's unprincipled and annoying.
	- He gets along with [[Elara]] better.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gets fed up with the man and tells him off.
	- [[Elara]] thinks it's worth trying to get him to head to the [[House Arden|Arden]] camp and look for work there.
	- She goes to convince him. His name is [[Jamie]].
	- He and his 9 companions wants 70gp a man.
	- She attempts to negotiate, convincing them to sign up for 60gp a man.
	- They agree. She's made a bold assumption about King [[House Arden|Arden]] paying them.
	- They ask her to play her lute. She does so and gets some good tips from the patrons. ![[Session 5.5 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] looks around the room while she plays looking for any trouble. ![[Session 5.5 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- He notices a man that looks to be of darker complexion talking to another patron, speaking quietly in a foreign tongue.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] walks around outside and comes inside to tell [[Elara]] about the strange men.
	- She's too drunk to know much.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] ends up having to take her upstairs.
	- They both turn in.
	- In the morning they grab breakfast and hit the road.
	- As we leave, we see [[Jamie]] and his crew following suite. They'll be a ways behind us.
- We travel for the next few days until arriving at [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s army. It's nearly 65,000 men. ![[Session 5.5 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We see [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s son [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]] walking around the camp with [[Sir Willis Braxton|Sir Braxton]].
- They take us to see [[Godric Arden|King Godric]].
- Many lords and important people are here in the war camp.
- We gain audience with the king and [[Elara]] discloses her hiring of the mercs with the king's money.
- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] isn't pleased, but agrees to put them in the vanguard.
- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] invites them to the war council.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] sees [[Sir John Marshall]] there. He has an interesting looking longsword at his side.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] starts going into his plans for the war.
	- [[Lord William Marshall]] (John's father) says no scouts have returned from [[Kingsmont]].
	- We're to fall in line under [[Sir John Marshall]] as battalion lord.
	- The meeting ends and we prepare to move out.
- [[Jamie]] and his band of sellswords arrive.
	- They agree to their position. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] accepts their allegiance and threatens death on then if they renege.
- The army moves out and makes it [[Kingsmont]] in a the span of a few days, beginning the siege.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]]'s group is assigned to defend the siege towers.
	- The battle ensues.

## Combat

- A Lanister man in plate charges the field. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] attacks him but misses.
- [[Elara]] blinds a city guard commander.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] knocks his attacker prone with a riposte and caves his head in on his next attack. ![[Session 5.5 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] moves into position behind the city guard commander.
- [[Elara]] casts hold person on the city guard commander.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes advantage and hits the man, toppling him brutally.
- [[Elara]] takes a shot a crossbowman with her crossbow and the hit connects.
- The battle is going well when a cavalry charge of [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men.
- The cavalry charges in doing massive damage to [[House Arden|Arden]] forces.
- A call for retreat is sounded.
- [[Sir John Marshall]] cries to protect the king.
- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is knocked off his horse, his men try to pull him up as he screams to stand and fight.
- His men help him retreat anyway.
- We manage to retreat in full with heavy casualties.

## End Combat

- [[Titus Lancaster]] is the one who's men charged the field.
	- They made safe the city and did not pursue [[House Arden|Arden]] forces.
	- [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] is furious, asking for answers.
	- 10k men are dead. More are wounded.
	- [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] reinforcements are estimated to be 50k.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s men encourage him to withdraw as a siege can no longer be maintained.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] dejectedly agrees and issues to order to regroup in [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]].
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] dismisses everyone but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]].
- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] pours a glass of wine for himself and says he's proud of how well he fought, specifically [[Elara]].
	- He tells us he has a new mission for us: to go back to the [[House Grey|Grey]]'s and fight the [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]]'s on his behalf.
	- He wants us to accompany him to back to [[Brightguard]] first.
- We see [[Jamie]] and the sellswords in the camp, having all survived.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tells him he's glad he was wrong about him.
	- They're interested in staying for the right price.
- Before nightfall, [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]] finds us and comes to talk.
	- He's glad we're safe and is excited to see his sister [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] again back home.
- The army rests for the night and then marches back to [[Brightguard]] over the next few days.
- When we arrive, [[Godric Arden|Godric]] rewards up with new weaponry.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is given a +1 Greatsword.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] speaks to the armorer about plate armor, but is told it will take months and the king has not approved any to be made.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks for the armorer to put in a good word for him and prepares to leave.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gets to talk to [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] before they leave. He loves her like a daughter.
