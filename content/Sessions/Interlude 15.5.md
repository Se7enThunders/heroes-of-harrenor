The cold sweat is still drying on my skin. I sit awake, though the morning sun has not yet touched the windows of [[Brightguard]]. I thought I had come home, but the dreams—they follow. They are relentless.

I woke in complete darkness, the silence itself a weapon. The faint, familiar sounds of the castle watch were gone. It was wrong. I armed myself and went to the hall. Nothing. But the air was thick with a presence, a heavy, silent malice that raised the hairs on my neck.

I saw a figure—tall, thin, carrying a spear—and pursued it through the dark corridors. I don't remember putting on my plate armor, but it was there, solid and cold. I followed the figure to the massive courtyard doors. Then, a deep, vast voice whispered in my mind, a language of pure darkness. It commanded me to open the gates.

I did. And I was greeted by fire.

The castle was a funeral pyre, the sky burning an ominous amber. The courtyard was a field of slaughter. Pikes, twenty feet tall, held the impaled bodies of every man in [[Brightguard]]. And there, speared alongside them—**[[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], [[Dharshoon]]**. My friends. My company.

My sword fell. I dropped to my knees, unable to breathe.

The figure with the spear stood over me. He held a severed head, which he threw at my feet. The head of **[[Goroth]]**. He told me the destruction I saw was my own work, that I was a mighty sword.

Then came the true horror. Another figure stepped forward. It was me. My own face, my own colossal frame, clad in black, with cold, yellow eyes. He showed me a **scorched wooden lion**—the one I carved for [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]]. He spoke with the voice of the dark figure.

Rage—pure, cleansing fire—consumed me. I attacked myself. My greatsword found the gap in his armor. He seized the blade, but I forced it deeper, right into his core. He fell.

The dark figure mounted a massive dragon, larger than [[Therond]]. He spoke a truth I have fought my entire life to deny: "I am thy father. Thou art my progeny." He offered me conquest, my birthright in the land of my birth, **[[Varanor]]**.
I gathered the last of my resolve, grasping my sword, the cross of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] steady in my focus.

"No," I told him.

He unleashed the wounds of my fallen self, who rose again, eyes now burning with [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|abyssal fire]]. He disarmed me, his hands pressing against my throat and smashing my head into the stone. He swore my "dark soul" was his.

I woke up, tears burning my face. I knelt by my bed. I confessed my origin, my cursed blood, to [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]. I don't know if He heard me, but for the first time since that vision, I felt a spark of comfort amidst the fear. Small, but real.

I know now that the war is not just for a throne. It is for my soul. The enemy is in the North, waiting. And he will not stop until he has turned me into the beast he created. I must move. I must prepare. I cannot let that prophecy come true.
