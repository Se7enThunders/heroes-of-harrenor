## Previously

- The party defeats a powerful [[The undead knight|undead knight]] leading a horde; [[Artorius]] displays inhuman speed and holy fire. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] lands the final blow, and the knight crumbles to ash. Evidence of long war between the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]] and the [[The Black Hands|Black Hand]] is found in the ruins.
- [[Dharshoon]] rings the belltower, but no visible effect follows. The party discovers healing potions and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] claims a strange [[The Black Ring|black ring]] from the fallen knight.
- A platinum-haired girl named [[Leana]], [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] of Lady [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphim]], appears with 30 dire wolves. She reveals a third bell lies far south in the desert; ringing all three will restore [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphim]] to the world.
- The party uses the everwhite [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] to return. [[Artorius]] departs. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] privately renounces and curses his father before stepping back through.
- The [[The Black Ring|black ring]] reacts to [[The Palantir|the Palantir]]. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] uses it and sees a controlled vision: [[Henry Grey]] murdered (by one resembling [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]), [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] taken, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stellaris]] dead, and a towering hellfire-cloaked figure. The ring and [[The Palantir|Palantir]] are kept separate but in the party’s possession.

## Now

- The party discusses what to do next and agrees to head to [[Oldport]]. From there, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] will send word to [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] as to his whereabouts, as the party intends to head south once more to ring the final bell.
- It's been a harsh winter, but even the heaviest snow is melting away. The ground is soft and the sun is warm. The war campaigns will pick up again soon. ![[Session 23 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- We travel to [[Oldport]] and note that many more ships are at port now that the city has been liberated. ![[Session 23 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- We note a few ships bearing the insignia of the colonial bank.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] splits off and offers to meet the group at the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]] at nightfall.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes that [[Sir John Marshall]] stands outside stand outside [[Oldport|Stormbreaker Castle]] with [[Lord Adam Croft|Sir Adam Croft]]. [[Lord Adam Croft|Croft]] being a vassal of [[House Arden]].
	- He asks if [[Lord Adam Croft|Croft]] can deliver a message to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] for him. [[Lord Adam Croft|Croft]] agrees.
	- [[Lord Adam Croft|Croft]] mentions that [[The Northeast|the Northeast]] is being pressed by raids from [[Varanor]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] agrees to deliver a sealed letter before midday the next day and leaves.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] then heads to the Cathedral, seeking information on the location of the third tower in the south.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] meets up with [[Henry Grey|King Grey]]. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]] is there.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] demands [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s service in the war. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] attempts to explain how important his current task of summoning Lady [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphim]] is.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] demands that [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] heads west and scouts their enemies.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] asks [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s opinion on Lords [[House Cromwell|Cromwell]] and [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]]. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] has a low regard for both.
	- The agrees to his orders and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] departs.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], and [[Dharshoon]] hang out outside castle Stormbreaker. They note the colonial bank has with them, [[The Iron Company|the Iron Company]], the largest mercenary army on the continent. They're highly regarded and being used as body guards in this instance.
	- They are here guarding high ranking bankers here to do business. ![[Session 23 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] notices he's caught the attention of one of the bankers.
	- He approaches [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and tells him, he's surprised to see [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] here, knowing his father's allegiances.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] shrugs, not wanting the conversation continue.
	- The man persists, explaining he helped with his father's accounts, reminding him his family was allied with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].
	- The man states he means no offense, and slyly implies that [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]'s family remains in the bank's debt and when his father passes, that debt will be his.
	- As the man departs, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] notices his signet ring. <mark>A mocking bird</mark>.
	- The three a shaken and consider how deep the corruption of this mocking bird organization goes.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] heads into the Cathedral, joining a service in progress. ![[Session 23 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
	- The preacher speaks on perseverance and seeking wisdom from wise counselors and prayer to [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks for a private audience. [[Father James]] agrees and takes him to his parsonage.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tells all and asks for his opinion on the matter and if he knows where we might find information on the final belltower's location.
	- [[Father James]] doesn't have much to offer, for which he's apologetic. The church's records have been lost over the years in wars with the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'haadi]].
	- As [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] departs, he notices a very old map on the wall of the Father's home.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks to purchase it, the Father says it belongs to the church and declines.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks to copy it and is allowed to do so, spending the next few hours doing so, leaving a gold piece for the Father, upping it to 10 after [[Father James]] says it can go to a needy family.
- The party convenes at the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]]. It's very busy, with all sorts of people groups inside.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] breaks the news he can't go to [[Ra'Haad]] right away, noting his orders from [[Henry Grey|King Grey]], being sent on a scouting under command of [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stelaris]].
	- We discuss what our options are to relieve [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] of his duties, deciding to approach [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] about the party's holy mission, asking him to speak with [[Henry Grey]] on our behalf.
- After a few drinks, we search out Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]]. We're told he's gone to meet someone outside the western city gates.
	- We walk up on him showing his sword to a hooded figure.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] calls out so the party doesn't look like they're spying.
	- We realize the hooded figure is [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]], [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]]'s brother. They beckon us join them.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is thrilled to see them both.
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] explains he's seen some strange things lately. The agree, and we head back into the city.
- We arrive back at the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rents out the private room and pays extra to keep the good food and drink coming while we speak privately.
	- The party, who trusts [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] and [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] as much as anyone, tell them everything we've experienced since meeting with them last. Ending with the request to speak with [[Henry Grey]] on [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s behalf.
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] agrees to speak with [[Henry Grey]], requesting that both [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] can accompany the rest of the party southward.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gets out the map to notate the location of the churches. They are concentrated in the north, with only a few in [[Ra'Haad]].
	- We suspect the map is older as we've been to a few of these sites where no churches remain.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notes the single church in the dead center of [[The Blight|the Blight]]. We agree this is our first target.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires again about joining [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] in [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]].
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] explains the crucial element of joining the order is a manifestation of the holy fire within one's soul.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] doesn't quite understand, but promises to learn more.
- The next day we're given approval to head south with [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] writes a message to [[Godric Arden|King Godric]], sealing it, and delivering it to [[Lord Adam Croft|Lord Croft]] to relay to [[Godric Arden|Godric]], explaining his actions and where he will be joining.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] and his squire [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] gather their things and join us.
- We travel the few weeks journey to the fortress of [[Grimhold]], being greeted by Sir [[Torren Grimston]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] inquires as to [[Ahmed Al-Zahim]]. He's recently returned to [[Grimhold]].
	- While we await [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] to arrive, we pay our respects to [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s grave, marked just outside the fortress, noting freshly faced flowers.
	- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] agrees to join us before even being asked, noting that we're insane, asking for a set of plate mail from [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s king. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] laughingly agrees.
- We all share a meal together, enjoying one last night of hot foot and warm beds before heading into [[The Blight|the Blight]].
- As we travel through [[Ra'Haad]], we notice the few towns we come through have either exiled or killed their Dhimmi population.
	- We try to lay low, staying out of population areas, sticking to the river flowing into [[The Blight|the Blight]].
	- We notice a lower amount of soldiers than we did last time we were here.
	- We stock up on supplies at one of the last towns before entering [[The Blight|the Blight]], though we have difficulty finding many vendors who will deal with us as foreigners.
	- We buy a camel as a pack animal from a Dhimmi. Some guards see him selling to foreigners and get upset, making a scene.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] steps up, threatening to draw his sword, spitting on the ground to insult them, infuriating the guards who attack him immediately.
	- The's face is splattered in blood as [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] takes off the heads of both guards in one blow.
