# Session 24

← [[Session 23]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 24|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 25]] →

## Previously

- Party departs [[Sunwatch]] for [[Oldport]]; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] sends word to [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] via [[Lord Adam Croft|Lord Croft]] while noting renewed war pressure in [[The Northeast|the Northeast]].
- Tensions rise in [[Oldport]]: [[Henry Grey]] orders [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] into military service under [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stelaris]]; [[Colonial Bank]] presence and [[The Iron Company|Iron Company]] signal deeper political/financial influence (==[[The Mockingbird|mockingbird]] faction resurfaces==).
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] consults [[Father James]] at the Cathedral; uncovers and copies an old map pointing to southern sites—identifies a likely target in [[The Blight|the Blight]] for the final bell.
- Party secures support from [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] and [[Baylor Stelaris]], gaining approval to pursue the [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]] mission south despite [[House Grey|Grey]]’s demands.
- Journey to [[Grimhold]]: reunite with [[Ahmed Al-Zahim]], honor [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]’s grave, and prepare for desert crossing into [[Ra'Haad|Ra’Haad]].
- Travel through [[Ra'Haad|Ra’Haad]] reveals escalating brutality (Dhimmi persecution, fewer organized troops); party keeps a low profile while resupplying.
- Conflict erupts in final town before [[The Blight|the Blight]]: [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] provokes hostile guards, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] executes two instantly, and the party is drawn into open combat as more enemies converge.

## Now (Wrapping Combat)

- We defeat the guards who attacked us, but as reinforcements are summoned we make our way out of town quickly.
- We travel directly toward the blight, coming to the border with the flowing river that still looks as putrid as diseased still water. ![[Session 24 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
- We approach a bridge still in decent repair, but our mounts give us trouble. They're spooked.
- [[Dharshoon]] manages to calm them and keep them moving forward.
- As we cross over, several of the party members have a vision of a ruined city covered in ash and still burning fires. ![[Session 24 art 1.png|hh-pop]] ![[Session 24 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- We note a cloud of dust being kicked up the way we came, likely pursing us. We move into [[The Blight|the Blight]] to avoid.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes [[Dharshoon]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] are notably shaken by the vision. He prays for them before they make camp and sleep for the night.
- The same three have a dream of a [[The molten demon|demon]] with a molten sword, it's skin looks like molten rock. ![[Session 24 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- We awake and continue onward, being inflicted with a sandstorm.
- In the distance we can make out shadows floating above the ground, hovering in the air, like dark spirits. ![[Session 24 art 5.png|hh-pop]]
- We continue onward and they keep their distance, but seem to moving alongside us.
	- If we try moving toward them, they keep the same distance regardless.
- We continue on and as we settle in for the another night.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] quizzes us more on what we expect to do once we find the last belltower.
- [[Dharshoon]] fills him in.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] unsheathes his sword ([[Starbinder]]) while settling down, and it still shines with starlight despite the darkness.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is mesmerized.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] explains it's been with his family since before records were kept.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] confides that his hope has dimmed in recent days, but has not been extinguished. He worries that despite our efforts here, those on the side of evil do the same.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] explains the hope he's found in [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] in recent days and that he's as hopeful as he's ever been.
- We all take our rest as [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] takes first watch.
	- The shadows still watch us in the air. One moves closer and seems to be fascinated by [[Starbinder]], which is still partially unsheathed and glowing.
	- It notices [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] watching it and points the direction of the city we're traveling to.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] threatens it and it recedes back into the night.
- We awaken and the spirits are gone, the sandstorm has lifted, and the sun, though dim, is shining.
- Things seem strangely calm.
- We finally crest a ridge and see the ruins of a city in the distance, with the church on a hilltop in the center.
	- Strangely, it seems to be eerily intact, thankfully with no signs of activity.
	- We take a moment to examine the city. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] fells like something is off, but can't put his finger on it.
	- We note the city appears to resemble ancient [[Harrenor]] more so than [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]].
- We approach the outskirts of the city, now noticing fields and grass that appear to be attended to.
- Fearing an illusion, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] touches some. It seems real.
- We approach the gate, which is wide open. There is no one around, aside from one singular man.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] approaches the man and attempts to speak with him, but he replies in elvish.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] continues in elvish. The man welcomes us to [[Telandor]].
	- We ask how long he's been here. He stammers, not able to answer.
	- We ask where everyone else is. He says they're gone. They left some time ago.
	- He begins to tell us a story of how their matron (the lady) was put into a slumber. As she has slept, the darkness has crept over.
	- He explains he is all that remains, bound to the city, waiting for someone to come to make things right.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] explains the situation and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] seems more excited than the rest to finish the mission.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] explains he's worried none of this is real, a powerful illusion.
- Despite our misgivings, we move to the church to ring the final bell.
- As we walk through the city, we see standards of the city, a ==purple banner with a gold symbol of the almighty above a mountain==. ![[Session 24 art 6.png|hh-pop]]
- The man explains a great order of knights once protected the city and flew this banner.
- We pass shops still stocked with arms, armor, food, and notice the city was prepared for a fight.
- The man explains they were expecting to fight "winged fire."
- We come to an inner gate of the city, guarding the palace and the church deeper in the city.
	- It is guarded by at least 10 men.
	- Our [[Telandor's guide|guide]] notes they are attackers. Hostiles. They've been here as long as he has.
	- There is a loaded ballista on the wall, unmanned at the moment.
	- There are 4 on the bridge in front of the wall. We decide to go in hot.

## Combat

- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] opens the encounter by charging in and giving his all against 2 of the attackers, killing one and injuring the other.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] uses his cape of the mountebank to teleport himself and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] on top of the bridge and man the ballista.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rushes the bridge and uses his ancestral magic to grow to giant size, creating a choke point.
- He pushes one solider off the bridge, causing him to drown in his heavy armor.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Dharshoon]] engage.
- [[Dharshoon]] casts rageful nimbus on [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] mans the ballista and targets the leader, impaling him and pinning him to the ground, killing him instantly.
- [[Baylor Stelaris|Balor]] handles several other soldiers deftly. We end the encounter in short order.

## End Combat

- The man asks The and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] to come down the other side and open the doors.
- We ask the man's name. He doesn't even remember. He's never been inside the inner gate.
- We head up the hill along a spiraling path, through the palace grounds toward the church.
- We see corpses of soldiers. Men of [[Telandor]], other warriors wearing dark colors which look like the attire of the men we just fought.
	- The bodies look freshly killed.
- We ask the man what the last year he remembers is. It was 3000 years ago. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is physically shaken.
- As we press in, we find journals mentioning great bellowing in the distance, the city under siege, and drums in the night.
- Yet another logbook says "the grand [[The Grand Captain|captain]] of the swords of the church says our lady and the [[The Kilns|kiln]] are to be protected at all costs."
- The man tells us the [[The Kilns|kiln]] was said to have fire from the spirit of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]. If it was ever spilled, the city would perish.
- We move through the palace and come to a narrow bridge leading to the church. A gate on our side guards it.
	- We see no means to open it, only a roped tied to a large bell.
	- [[Dharshoon]] determines that ringing the bell should open the gate. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rings it.
	- It sounds hauntingly similar to the church bells we've rang previously. The gate opens.
	- A chasm lies below the bridge. We cross it quickly leery of what lies below.
- We reach the church and hear the same voice we've heard on the past telling us to turn back.
- We ignore it as before, and move inside.
- [[Dharshoon]] opens the gate. The voice summons [[The Grand Captain|the Grand Captain]].
	- The interior is lined with bodies of the dead. A shut door on the other side.
	- One of the bodies appears to be a large warlord and a knight with a [[The Winged Helm|winged helm]] and a pitch black sword.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] examines the sword and sword and hands it to [[Dharshoon]] for safe keeping.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] swaps his helm for the [[The Winged Helm|winged helm]] of [[The Grand Captain|the Grand Captain]].
- We open the final door and see a woman in auburn hair in white robes, sleeping on a couch, clutching a half sphere, the reflection of fire flickering inside. ![[Session 24 art 7.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Dharshoon]] gently wakes her. The light in orb goes out. This was the [[The Kilns|kiln]]. Her form is replaced by a skeleton. ![[Session 24 art 8.png|hh-pop]]
- We see now the city is truly in ruins.
- Everything around is decomposed, ruined, rusted, the fires in the city that should be out still burn, we hear horrific noises from the streets.
- The man chuckles deviously behind us.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] draws his blade and points it at the man who we see now is withered and still lives only through dark magic.
- He says we've spilled the [[The Kilns|kiln]] and its contents will be scattered through the world.
- He laughs a final time and dies.
- The lady's body clasps a note that says =="The scourge and the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] wait in the dark below."==
- We explore the area and note that two basins of [[Holy Water of Telandor|holy water]] we saw when we came in are still intact.
- We're unsure what to do with it, so [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hastily anoints the party and our weapons. We feel empowered.
- There is no bell in the church, and we find no entrance to the "dark below" within the church.
- We go back the way we came, seeing everything in ruins again, but the corpses of the men we killed are unchanged.
- We smell sulfur as we pass through the gate. We hear the sound of flesh catching fire.
- We turn and see the leader of the men we fought at the gate, rising. He's nearly unrecognizable, his skin black and glowing molten. He takes on the form of the [[The molten demon|demon]] the three dreamed of.
