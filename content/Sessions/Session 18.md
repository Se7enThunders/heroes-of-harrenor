## Previously

- The party discussed their shared prophetic dreams ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]), including [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s visions of stabbing [[Henry Grey]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s vision of disaster.
- The party confronted [[Jingle Bell]] (the [[Jingle Bell|jester]]) in [[Brightguard]] after he delivered cryptic verses, convincing [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] he is an oracle. [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] was informed but remained mostly unconcerned.
- The group learned [[Sir Eamon Douglas|Aemond]] [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] was traded for [[Robert Lancaster]], and [[Sophia Grey]] would remain in [[Brightguard]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] privately confided about dreams and visions with [[Father James]] before departing. He had little insight.
- Traveling south, the party was ambushed by highwaymen—disguised and bearing the sigil of [[House Croft]]—as they entered [[The South|the South]], marking a betrayal of House [[Godric Arden|Godric]].
- The party defeated the highwaymen, but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] was briefly knocked down. They took the leader prisoner and decided to investigate a nearby structure in the hills.

## Now

- We opt to heal up and <mark>rest</mark> briefly outside the ruins.
- On closer inspection, the ruins appear to be that of an old castle. ![[Session 18 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Dharshoon]] recalls how devastating the [[03 The Jihads|jihads]] were in the south in years past.
	- He recalls this was a fort ruined in the [[03 The Jihads|jihads]], belonging to an extinct house, [[House Aurelius]].
- The ruins look recently occupied. Ground has been cleared and there are lots of tracks.
- [[Dharshoon]] quickly scouts the area, noticing there are crossbowmen guarding from the towers.
	- The bandits atop the towers are also wearing stolen house heraldry.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests disposing of the bandits would be a sign a good faith. The party agrees.
	- We decide on a plan of stealthily infiltrate from the towers and kill from the top down.
- We get [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] moving to ascend the tallest turret stealthily.
	- The two successfully get to a window and inside the turret tower.
	- They note the bandits have managed to pilfer a lot of provisions.
	- They ascend and are almost caught tripping over some debris, but [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] manages to tackle and stab the guard, killing him before he alerts anyone.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] signals up to come around.
	- We move toward the tower, but a bowman at the other tower, notices [[Dharshoon]] approaching the fort.
	- He's shot and hit by a heavy crossbow.
	- He retaliates but misses both shots.
	- Guards come around the corner and combat ensures, after [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] assassinates one stealthily.

## Combat

- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] kills the second guard who came around the corner in short order.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] runs out around the tower into the open, loudly cursing out the bandits, attempting to get their attention. ![[Session 18 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]] ![[Session 18 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We make short work of the bandits, taking little damage.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] takes the most, but still isn't bloodied.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] captures the last bandit, and dangles him over the turret, threatening him for information.

## End Combat

- The bandit says that their leader the castellan is in the interior building as well as 500 gp.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] tosses him over, killing him with the fall.
- As we discuss what to do with bandit leader, the door is thrown open from the inside.
- We see an older man sitting at the hearth, with a glass of wine.
- The bandit leader offers us wealth and access to his network of spies of we let him live.
	- He says the castellan is alive in the cellar.
	- [[Dharshoon]] goes to check on him and finds him ill, but not beaten.
	- The castellan asks to be freed. [[Dharshoon]] picks the lock and frees him.
	- The castellan names himself [[Brutus]], previously tasked with overseeing this place years ago. ![[Session 18 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] questions [[Brutus]] about his thoughts on the bandit leader.
	- [[Brutus]], who has stolen a dagger from [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], quickly kills the bandit leader.
	- He apologetically returns the dagger.
	- He shows us the emblem of a [[The Mockingbird|mockingbird]] on his person, though we never got his name.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] recalls this is the sigil of lord [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]].
	- [[Brutus]] states he's been here for 16 years. Since the [[04 The 6th Jihad|last jihad]].
	- He asks us to take lordship of the castle.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] reminds us we have a prisoner.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests to spend the night here to rest.
- [[Brutus]] is eager to give us a tour, which we oblige.
	- We tell him we'll discuss our potential lordship over an evening meal and let him know in the morning.
- As he gives us the tour, he shows us the old forges, the library, and the crypts.
	- The crypts are well preserved and accessed only by hidden passageway.
	- Inside is a banner of a <mark>gold eagle with wings stretched overhead, wreathed in a golden sun, on a field of red</mark>, the sigil of house [[House Aurelius|Aurelius]], lit by magical ever-burning torches.
- As we tour the crypts, we notice the house is very old, and each coffin has the same sword carved into it.
	- As we see the tomb of [[Titus Aurelius]], we note his visage wears a [[The Winged Helm|winged helm]].
- [[Brutus]] mentions that the sword we see on all the tombs is called [[Radiance]].
	- It is an ancestral heirloom made of eternium. As is [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]]' helm, as well as a greataxe named [[Darkeater]], all lost in the [[03 The Jihads|jihad]].
- [[Brutus]] shows us the final room of note above, the master's chambers.
	- Inside is a painting of [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]]. He has red hair and beard.
	- There's also door made of [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite]] wood, a tree often associated in rituals of the people of [[Varanor]].
	- The asks to open the door. He does so. It seems to only be a closet, but the air that comes forth is freezing cold.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] does the same with no change, though he swears he sees another chamber beyond for a moment.
- We convene in the hall for a meal that [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] cooks and [[Dharshoon]] seasons.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] is insistent we deal with the prisoner.
	- The idea is put forward to behead the man, but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] insists we don't.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wants him presented alive to house [[House Grey|Grey]] as a token of good faith.
- We retrieve the prisoner, feed and water him, and put him a cell within the castle.
- We eat and discuss the idea of lordship of the castle.
	- We determine [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Dharshoon]] are qualified, as they both reside here under the lordship of [[House Grey]].
	- We decide [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] should assume lordship for now, with the caveat that [[Dharshoon]] be allowed to make a home here as well.
- We retire for the night.
	- But not before [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] heads back to the master's chambers and takes in the majesty of [[Titus Aurelius|Lord Titus]]' painting. ![[Session 18 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- He's notices something and decides to go look at the crypts to look at his tomb again.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notices that the material around the arms and the sword are newer than the rest of the statue.
	- He's convinced something us up, but can't figure out what, departing for his bed.
	- He dreams of the phrase [[Brutus]] spoke. "<mark>[[Titus Lancaster|Titus]]' sword is fit for a lord.</mark>"

## Long Rest

- We awake to a warmer day with more sunshine. It feels like spring is on its way.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notices cardinals outside the castle grounds while walking about.
	- [[Brutus]] notices and elaborates they are native here and are a sign of spring and life.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] confides what he discovered last evening with [[Brutus]], inquiring about it.
	- [[Brutus]] has noticed this, but doesn't know what it means.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] decides to bring us with him to further investigate the tomb of [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]].
- We all take turns holding the hilt of the sword.
	- Nothing happens for any of us except [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], who feels like the statue begins to stir, but doesn't.
	- [[Dharshoon]] notices there is magic within the statue, tied to one of lordship.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests that [[Brutus]] declare in this crypt, [[Theodore Silver]] as lord of [[Sunwatch]].
- As we all walk away dejected, we hear something metallic clatter to the ground behind us.
