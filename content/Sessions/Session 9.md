**Quests in play:** [[09 Find Elia and Raze the Black Tower|Find Elia and Raze the Black Tower]], [[10 Break the Sable Wardens|Break the Sable Wardens]]

Previously

- The party, consisting of **[[Joran Jotunsson]]** **(me)**, **[[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen Lawson]]**, **[[Theodore Silver]]**, **[[Draven Throssenheiser]]**, and **[[Dharshoon]]**, traveled from [[Brightguard]] to [[Na'Buchar]], posing as slavers, to investigate a **[[House Lancaster|Lancaster]]–[[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’Haadi]] alliance**.
- They survived encounters in [[The Blight]], including fighting wights, avoiding threats in [[Ashfall]], killing a wyvern, and escaping a [[The red dragon of Ashfall|red dragon]], before reaching [[Na'Buchar]].
- In [[Na'Buchar]], they met **[[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]]**, warned him of the alliance, and planned to join him at [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan|the Emir]]'s court, where they would pose as merchants.
- During the court meeting with **[[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan]]**, a mysterious **[[The man in black|man in black]]** (suspected [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] agent or [[Sable Wardens|Sable Warden]]) was present and seemed to recognize [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]].
- **[[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]** was confronted by **[[Salim al Muqtar]]** regarding a message for "[[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Barring]]," revealing [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] was a **[[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] agent** tasked with infiltrating the [[House Grey|Grey]] camp and that the message he received from [[Lord Thomas Baring|Barring]] alluded to an upcoming "[[03 The Jihads|jihad]]."
- After returning to [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s estate, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] confessed his true mission and the implications for his family, leading the party to consider stopping [[Salim al Muqtar|Salim]].
- [[The man in black]] later appeared at [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s estate, attempting to buy [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s servant, **[[Elia]]**, and vaguely threatening [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]], who refused the sale.
- [[Elia]] was subsequently abducted, prompting the party to pursue her, though they were unsuccessful after several days of tracking.
- Their pursuit led them to a **massive [[The black tower|black tower]]** in the desert, where they engaged in combat with a **nine-foot-tall Tower Guardian** and [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] elite soldiers.
- The party defeated the Tower Guardian and the soldiers, now recognizing the tower as both magical and menacing, and are left to investigate further.

## Now

- The [[The black tower|tower guardian]] has dissolved to ash. His armor and weapon are left behind. It does not look magical nor useful.
- (because the player is gone for a session) [[Dharshoon]] offers to keep an eye out while we go inside to search for [[Elia]].
- We approach the tower, with [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] taking the lead.
- The tower is made of a strange smooth black stone. We enter and see a staircase going both up and down.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] thinks he hears some faint noise coming from below. We opt to go down.
- We find a door. There are torches lit.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] grabs a torch and it triggers a trap arrow. He barely dodges it.
- We see now there are many slots where arrows could fire from.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notice tiles in the floor and discern a safe path through it as to not trigger more trap arrows.
- As we open the door, we hear a mechanism that we discern has deactivated any traps on the immediate area.
- We open the door and see a [[The mortician|mortician]]'s workspace. The tools are well maintained and crafted quite finely. They match the towers construction.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], being at the head of the group, first sees a [[The man in black|man in black]] robes attending a corpse, draining blood from the body, a male elf.
- The man spots us and is afraid. He drops what he's doing and falls to his knees, asking what we're doing here.
- The man attempts to flee down a corridor as [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] mentions [[Elia]]. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] catches him and interrogates him.
- As we're discussing what to do next, we realize there are other prisoners being kept here, some still alive, pleading for help.
- One of the prisoners is a another male elf, malnourished, likely from blood loss at the hands of [[The mortician|the mortician]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] locates the key for the cells and begins to unlock them.
- The male elf he releases is missing an eye and has skin marked up with cuts and incision.
- We ask [[The mortician|the mortician]] WHY.
- [[The mortician]] refuses to answer until [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] bashes his head against the wall.
- [[The mortician]] then confesses they are bloodletting the elves in an attempt to create a serum to extend the life of their leader.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] throws [[The mortician|the mortician]] in the cell and locks it.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] puts the keys on the table and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tells the prisoners they can have their justice now. They should stay put until we return.
- One of the elves grills [[The mortician|the mortician]] about a safe way out of the dungeon, implying he'll have [[The mortician|the mortician]] killed via the arrow traps once they escape.
- As we exit, the elf and [[The mortician|the mortician]] walk out, the elf shoving [[The mortician|the mortician]] into the arrow traps, killing him.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks the elf his name. It is [[Casius]]. He explains he was from one [[The colonies|the colonies]]. He was set upon by men in all black and captured, brought here.
- [[Casius]] escorts the rest of the captives as well as the body we found when we first entered.
- He says he didn't know the man, but he deserves a burial far from here.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thank the man and hope they meet again in the future.
- We head upstairs. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] feels like his sister is calling to him, then sees the door to his childhood home. As he calls back to the group, he doesn't see us anymore.
- The rest of us see our own personal visions, dark visions from out pasts.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hears [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] ([[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s daughter) cry out to him. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] runs to find her. As he turns the corner he sees [[Godric Arden|Godric]] and his son [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]]. [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]] lies dead.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] mutters they took [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] swears to find her.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hears [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] scream again and looks back toward [[Godric Arden|Godric]] who has vanished.
	- [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]]'s screams against farther away. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] sees a tattered [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] banner by [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]]'s body.
	- He catches up to the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] that have [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]]. Just has he does so, his vision ends.
- Everyone else is there and acknowledge we've all had discomforting visions.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notices the moon is in different phase. It was half full. It's now waxing toward full.
- [[Dharshoon]] comes in and tells us we were gone for 3 weeks.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] questions if what we're seeing now is real. [[Dharshoon]] has a bee sting him, which feels real enough.
- [[Dharshoon]] fills us in on getting the elves provisions and tending to the horses, saying he thought he saw glimpses of us, but was never really sure.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] punches a wall in frustration and it phases through.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] walks through, dagger drawn. We all follow suite.
- We pass through and see a room with candles burning, a library.
- We investigate and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] finds only [[The Blank Scrolls|blank scrolls]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] puts it to candle light and it reveals nothing.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes that his liege lord [[Godric Arden|Godric]] as used the juice of a citrus fruit to reveal hidden messages before. We have an orange with us and try it, to no avail.
- We know there's a message here, but are unsure how to discern it.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] feels it could only be magic that is hiding the messages from us. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] realizes then it needs to be dispelled.
- There are dozens and dozens of scrolls. All with the same magic used on them to make them appear blank.
- We grab as many scrolls as we can carry. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] attempts to burn one. It burns up with no other effects.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hears a voice telling them not to burn the scrolls and to come see him in the tower keep.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] threatens to burn the scrolls unless the voice comes to find them.
- The voice calls his bluff, and says to go for it. He says he has [[Elia]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] burns another scroll to no reply.
- We head upstairs and find a circular room. [[The man in black]] from [[Na'Buchar]] is there with [[Elia]].
- He begins monologuing on why [[Elia]]'s blood is valuable to him. The share a descendant and he can therefore use her blood to extend his life.
- With a sudden flash of light, [[Elia]] is gone and 3 apparitions of [[The man in black|the man in black]] appear.
- A [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] solder, an assassin, and a crossbowman appear from the shadows.
- In the center of the room is a monolith topped by a black crystal orb.
- [[The man in black]] tells them to spare the monk ([[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]), and kill the rest.

COMBAT!

- Combat ensues and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] is paralyzed by magic cast by [[The man in black|the man in black]].
- We surround the soldier and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] gets the kill on him.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] charges [[The man in black|the man in black]] and tears into him.
- [[The man in black]] uses magic to create duplicates of every enemy combatant in the room.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] regains his composure and rushes [[The man in black|the man in black]], destroying his final illusion.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] gets behind the bowman and gets the kill, moving to the assassin as his target.
- [[The man in black]] continues to use illusion magic to escape taking blows.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] lands the final blow on [[The man in black|the man in black]], plunging his greatsword through his abdomen, looking into his eyes as they quickly dim, telling him, "<mark>no amount of elven blood will extend your life now. Die</mark>."
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] gets the final kill on the assassin.

## End Combat

- [[The man in black]] through fits of coughing up blood laughs at us, telling us <mark>they found where [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s freed-slave city is located</mark>. He then dies.
- We find [[Elia]] alive and unscathed at the top of the tower. She rushes to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and embraces him, thanking him for the rescue.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] takes a hard look at the orb on the pylon in the center of the room. He is urged to grab it.
- Just as he's about to put it down, it explodes with light and magic.
- On the other side, he sees a man in all black, his face covered, staring back.
	- He looks at [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and says the line that [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s heard before in his strange dream calling him a "stricken creature."
	- He sees [[Grimhold]] under siege, stars falling from the sky, and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] killing [[Henry Grey]] at the behest of [[Titus Lancaster]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] prods him for what information he saw, but [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] doesn't speak of it.
- We find a chest with 50 pieces of gold a man as well as other loot (will be noted by DM the near future).
- As we're leaving [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices the bookshelves has labels he can read now. The rest of the group cannot.
	- We surmise [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] can read them now because he landed the killing blow on [[The man in black|the man in black]].
- The scrolls that catch his eye are labeled "correspondences."
- He sees another scroll showing an image of the sword [[Starbinder]], [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stelaris]]' sword.
- We exit the tower and begin going through the scrolls we have, explaining everything that happened to [[Dharshoon]] along the way.
- We make the long journey back to [[Na'Buchar]] with [[Elia]] in tow.
- Upon our return, we see [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s estate all but empty.
