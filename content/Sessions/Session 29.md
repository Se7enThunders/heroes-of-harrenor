## Now

 - We perched nearby, out of site, taking in the amassing troops at the border of [[Ra'Haad]] and [[Harrenor]]. ![[Session 29 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Grimhold]] is barely a few days ride from here.
- It'll be difficult to get around the force unnoticed especially in a timely manner.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] puts forth the idea of sabotaging their supplies or something similar before we leave.
- We look for anything we can see from here that we could adequately sabotage, but see nothing.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] for his [[The Sending Stone|sending stone]] and sends a message to [[Brutus]], the castellan of [[Sunwatch]].
	- He warns him of the coming danger and commands him to send messengers to [[Stag's Rest]] to warn them.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] if he has any expertise in this matter.
	- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] notices that the army is relatively unorganized at least as of yet.
- Likely we could sneak into the camp at nightfall and wreak some havoc with minimal risk to ourselves. Possibly delaying their advance.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests 1 or 2 of the party infiltrates if any, noting that [[Dharshoon]] would have the best likelihood of not getting caught, as he looks [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] and speaks the language.
	- [[Dharshoon]] suggests seeking out an incoming supply [[Bloody Knives slavers|caravan]] and destroying it, then making haste to [[Grimhold]].
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] come up with a plan to ride ahead and poison wells along the way. The party warms up to this idea quickly.
	- We land on this plan.
- As we wait for nightfall, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] paces nervously. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] comforts him as best he can.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is trained in poisoning and has the required kit to create it, while [[Dharshoon]] has knowledge of plant life that can be made into poison.
- We set out, making sure to look for any plants that can aid in poisoning.
- We poison any wells on the way we see, avoiding local patrols successfully until sunset.
- We travel several days, resting during the days, traveling at night for the cool and cover of darkness.
- Eventually, as we near [[Grimhold]], we find a [[Bloody Knives slavers|caravan]] holding slaves. They're from the east, not [[Ra'Haad]], as [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notices.
	- The slaves are exclusively elven females. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] instantly commits to freeing them and destroying the [[Bloody Knives slavers|slavers]], which we notice based on their cargo, may be tied to [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s former crime syndicate affiliation, [[The Bloody Knives|the Bloody Knives]].
	- Several of the crates have a faint green glow from the interior, indicating they may be filled with wildfire casks.
	- We estimate 25-30 [[Bloody Knives slavers|slavers]], likely more nearby.
	- While [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thinks we stand a chance, [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] does the math. There's no way we succeed in direct combat.
	- Ultimately we decide there's nothing we can do to help, as much as we wish we could.
	- On our way past, we notice their camp was twice as large as we thought, AND they're being escorted by [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] soldiers.
- We poison the next well, continuing on through the night, heading out and onto another well shortly after.
- As [[Dharshoon]] prepares to drop poison into the well, he hears bleating of sheep nearby.
	- A [[The shepherd|shepherd]] stirs from his sleep and sees [[Dharshoon]].
	- In a panic, [[Dharshoon]] tells him abruptly, "don't drink from this." then throws the poison in, quickly returning to the party and informing them what happened.
- We move on, shortly after hearing the ground rumble. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] puts his ear to the ground. It's the rumbling of horses.
	- We're caught in to open.
	- [[Dharshoon]] sees a small outcropping of trees that's our only hope for cover.
	- [[Dharshoon]] climbs a tree quickly and spies out the source of the sound, seeing several hundred torch lights moving rapidly towards us.
	- We make a break for it, heading toward [[Grimhold]], still a day's travel.
	- [[Dharshoon]] casts spike growth, holding them back slightly when the cross it.
	- We ride hard, getting to within 300 yards of [[Grimhold]] before the riders catch up and get within weapons range.
	- The riders start shooting volleys of arrows, a damaging several of the party.
	- The second volley does more damage to all but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
	- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]], [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]]'s horses are shot out from beneath them, as is squire [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]]'s.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] turn and gives his horse to [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] while taking his large form, becoming faster and picking up [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], running at a rapid pace, as fast as a horse can.
- We hear a horn sound from the direction of [[Grimhold]]. 50 knights crest the hill, forming a wedge.
	- Their lines spread just enough for us to pass through, then crash into the horde of [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] riders.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] commands the party make for the castle. We obey.
- As we look back running, we turn and see the knights have broken the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] charge, and they have broken and retreated. [[Grimhold]] has won the day.
	- Only 1 of the 50 knights were killed. Several injured.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] seeks out the leader of the knights to give the party's thanks.
- All knights wear the sigil of [[House Grimston]], a bear on its hindlegs afront a silver field.
- The lead knight removes his helm. It's none other than [[Torren Grimston]].
	- He got the message [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] sent to [[Stag's Rest]]. It's the only reason they were prepared to rescue us.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] recognizes everyone but [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]]. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] introduces them.
- We look around and see the castle is fully manned and battle ready.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] beckons us to his quarters to debrief.
	- He offers us water in the absence of wine.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] has summoned us. He has rallied his men and marches south.
	- The [[House Douglas|Douglases]] are causing trouble for him since we left.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] and [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] are meeting at a nearby church to attempt to reconcile their differences after the [[House Douglas|Douglases]] attacked some of [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] [[House Arden|Arden]]'s men.
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes note of this, his allegiance to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] still firm.
	- He offers us lodging for the night, then will speed us on our way north to the meeting.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] if he has anyone who can fix the damaged greatsword he recovered from [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]] back in [[Telandor]], as well as identify [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]'s green ring.
	- He does not. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hangs onto the items.
- After the meeting concludes, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] approaches [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] about the ring.
	- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] determines the ring lets the user deal an extra 1d8 necrotic damage on attack once per turn, with the curse of beating a DC 12 WIS or hear the voices of all those he's slain for that day.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is not interested and hands the ring to [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]], telling him to do with it what he will.
- In the morning, we're told the name of the town we're headed to is [[Sanctuary]]. It's 2 weeks journey.
- We've been gone for 2 months. It's late spring now.
- We've also been informed that riders have been sent to all the lands of [[Harrenor]], warning them of the coming [[03 The Jihads|jihad]] and pleading to set aside differences and unite against the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] invasion.
- We head out, making a detour to castle [[Sunwatch]] along the way.
	- Its repairs are coming along nicely. The roofs are fixed and no longer leaking. Some meager defenses have been erected.
	- [[Brutus]] greats us, apologizing that more hasn't been done. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] waves him off and thanks him for the work done.
	- [[Brutus]] informs us he expanded the armory, to better defend the lands the castle oversees.
	- He has recruited 4 men at arms, now at [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s disposal. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks him to triple that number, noting the growing threat of invasion.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] recommends they make a quick run through the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] to get the advice of the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] who guards the chapel to get advice on how to proceed in bringing back lady [[Lady Seraphyne|Serephyne]].
	- All but [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] goes through, he staying back behind to attend to the castle's needs.
	- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] is mesmerized by the magic [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] and the blood ritual it takes to get through.
- The party steps through into the bitter cold, into the abandoned village.
- We check [[Artorius]]' camp, finding it long abandoned.
- We notice there has been some activity in the old village, seeing signs of cook fires as well as a fresh grave nearby.
- The weather is good, so we set out for the chapel, looking for the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] as we go.
- We're unbothered on our travel, but feel a sense of dread as we see the tall looming mountains in the distance, remember the deathly howls we heard from that direction last we were here.
- As we approach the chapel, we see fresh wolf tracks. We follow them, coming to a forest clearing.
- In the clearing is a thick bramble of tree roots, making a wolf den. We enter.
- Large direwolves are within but are unbothered by our presence. The [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] [[Leana]] is there.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] kneels before her, pleading for her aid.
- She asks about [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] introduces him, as does he. He shows her the [[The Kilns|kiln]] we rescued from [[Guinevere]], [[Guinevere|the Lady of Telandor]].
	- She asks what happened to the flame within, noting it's gone out and that only the lady [[Lady Seraphyne|Serephyne]] can restore its light.
	- She says another [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] can be found.
	- Her focus is on [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]. She says he would know her, and that they're related, in fact she's [[Draven's sister]].
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] has no knowledge of a sister. [[Leana]] is insistent.
	- She mentions she saw [[Artorius]] just a few weeks ago.
	- Still utterly childlike, even in her quasi-immortal state, she asks us to play fetch with the wolves. We acquiesce.
	- She has no other information for us, but says she may come to visit sometime. We welcome this.
	- She giggles and pets one of the wolves, wishing us safe travels.
- We return to [[Sunwatch]] and inform [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] of what we learned, resting for the night and preparing to head north to [[Sanctuary]].
