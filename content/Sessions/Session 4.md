# Session 4

← [[Session 3]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 4|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 5]] →

## Goals

- Now that [[Stoneheath]] has been taken, get new orders, likely from [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]].
- Traveling with [[Elara]] (Rebekah) (aasimar), [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen Lawson]] (human) (Josh), [[Theodore Silver]] (human) (Preston), [[Draven Throssenheiser]] (half-elf) (Jake), [[Dharshoon]] (human) (Atmeos).
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] needs to find a maul and/or better armor.
- ==REMEMBER TO ROLL DAMAGE DICE TWICE (once per turn)! (savage attacker)==

## Now

- It's around midnight after having infiltrated and taken [[Stoneheath]] with [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] and his force of 350 men.
- Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]] rewards us 25 GP for our services.
- We rest for the night (level up).

## Long Rest

- [[Audrey Grey]] arrives and takes up the role of temporary leader of the town, on behalf of [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]].
	- She bans looting. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] approves of this.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] oversees the burial of the common soldiers and sending home of the bodies of the knights.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says an old pagan prayer in Varanoran over the bodies of [[Sir James Stone|Sir James]] and [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver Stone]], noting their honor in defense of their lord.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] breaks the bank to upgrade to splint armor. ![[Session 4 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] grabs some supplies including a 6 ft iron pole, grappling hook, and caltrops.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] speaks with [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]].
- [[Dharshoon]] finds a bowyer and trades beeswax for having his bow refurbished.
- [[Elara]] finds some nice deck of cards. ![[Session 4 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
- A [[House Stelaris|Stelaris]] man approaches us, requesting we meet to speak with [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] (star metal sword guy).
- [[Baylor Stelaris|Bealor]] is there as well. (guy in the red mask)
	- He tells us there are people from the town missing.
	- The missing people would head west into the forest and were never heard from again.
		- Mostly women.
		- Disappearances started a month ago, BEFORE the war started.
		- The [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] are also missing 5 men.
		- Several escapees of the [[08 The Battle of Stoneheath|battle for Stoneheath]] ran that way as well.
- We head into the forest, [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] with us for a time, but [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] steps out on his own into the forest, leaving us on our own.
	- We come upon a lone house, built into a cleft on a hill. ![[Session 4 art 4.jpg|hh-pop|25]]
	- Footprints are all over.
	- [[Elara]] opens the door to the house with her mage hand, then uses one of her cards to light to room.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] steps inside.
	- The house looks like it's been rummaged through, there's streaks of blood and scorch marks.
	- 2 corpses are in a closet. A father and a son (12y/o).
		- Eyes are burned out of their sockets.
	- [[Elara]] notices there's an emblem of the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] of [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|abyssal fire]] carved into the father's head.
		- The boy's heart is missing.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] stays outside while the rest of the group goes in to investigate. ![[Session 4 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gets sick to his stomach and exits the room.
		- He steps on a doll and picks it up. There's blood on it.
		- He shows it to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], who swears vengeance on whoever did this.
	- [[Elara]] investigate and finds a evidence a mother and daughter lived here as well.
	- [[Dharshoon]] finds the house has a beehive. He finds many of them dead, but the queen is alive.
		- He takes the queen for himself to protect.
- We hear a scream from further down the path.
	- We decide to follow.
	- We run down the path with torchlight extinguished.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] takes then lead.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] sees 4 bodies and a head on a pike. ![[Session 4 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
		- The bodies are clearly [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers.
	- We follow footsteps away from the bodies up a hill. Upon on cresting it, we see a [[The cult temple|temple]] complex in the valley below. ![[Session 4 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- There is an altar before them, covered in blood.
- Voices call out to us from the [[The cult temple|temple]] as six figures and a bound woman appear from the darkness within.
	- 12 [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and a single female prisoner emerge. ![[Session 4 art 7.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Rouen]] fire first, but the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] put up a wall of fire which consumes the arrow. ![[Session 4 art 9.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- The [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] put the woman back in the [[The cult temple|temple]].
	- [[Elara]] takes on a dark visage, intimidating the nearest [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]]. ![[Session 4 art 8.jpg|hh-pop]]

## Combat

- We fight the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and do well against them.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], who is normally very hesitant to kill, destroys these [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] with reckless abandon, reveling in their deaths. ![[Session 4 art 10.jpg|hh-pop]]
- As one of the last [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] dies, it summons a demon, covered in metal armor, holding a spear. ![[Session 4 art 11.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] attacks it and forces it to drop the spear.
	- The demon knocks all back but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], then grabs him threatening to crush his neck.
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] jumps into the fray eviscerating the creature in holy fire, saving [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. ![[Session 4 art 12.jpg|hh-pop]]

## End Combat

- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] steps into the [[The cult temple|temple]] where the hostage is being held, bringing her out to safety.
- [[Elara]] finds the ritual dagger, tries to grab it, but something dark repels her.
- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] grabs the dagger and disintegrates it.
	- He seems to wield great magical power, collapsing the temples with a snap of his fingers.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] beheads the bodies and prepares to burn them.
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] beats him to it, and burns the bodies with magic.
- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] thanks us and professes that his order ([[Order of Holy Fire]]) is in our debt. We are to be rewarded.
- We return to [[Stoneheath]] with the lone survivor and reunites her with her family.
- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] rewards us each with a unique item.
