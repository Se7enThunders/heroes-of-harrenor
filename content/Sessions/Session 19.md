# Session 19

← [[Session 18]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 19|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 20]] →

## Previously

- The party cleared an old fort of [[House Aurelius]] from bandits and freed [[Brutus]], the long-suffering Castellan. The now deceased bandit leader bore the sigil of [[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Baring]].
- The group decided that [[Theodore Silver]] will take lordship of the castle, called [[Sunwatch]], with [[Dharshoon]] also residing there.
- [[Brutus]] detailed the history of the extinct house and its lost eternium heirlooms: the sword [[Radiance]], the helm of [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]], and the axe [[Darkeater]].
- The party discovered a door of [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite]] wood in the master’s chambers that emitted a magical cold and offered a brief, supernatural glimpse of another room.
- After an investigation into the magical nature of [[Titus Aurelius|Lord Titus]]’ tomb, a ==mysterious metallic sound== echoed behind the party as they prepared to depart the crypt.

## Now

- We turn to see the sword the statue was holding now sitting on the ground. ![[Session 19 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- It's ==blade is silver-white with a gold pommel, black leather wrapping the handle, a ruby set near the base of the scabard==.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], newly made Lord of Castle [[Sunwatch]] by its castellan, [[Brutus]], picks up the weapon.
	- It fits his hand nicely. It is very sharp, as [[Brutus]] warns him. It is called [[Radiance]]. ![[Session 19 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We congratulate him.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] suggests to [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] to that he give [[Brutus]] one of the sending stones.
- [[Dharshoon]] takes stock of the surrounding area, noting that the land is fertile, but has grown fallow in the absense of anyone to farm it.
- He puts together a beehive and instructs [[Brutus]] on how to maintain it.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] helps clean up the chapel, though it needs masonry work.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gives [[Brutus]] 110 gp toward retrofitting and populating the castle.
	- There's already a chest of 500 gp here that [[Brutus]] will use to get it's reparations underway.
- We resume our mission to head to [[Oldport]] to inform [[Henry Grey]] that his sister [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] is in [[Godric Arden]]'s custody and he will gladly trade her back in exchange for his fealty as allies in the war.
- As we make our way to [[Oldport]], we see dire signs of the war, buildings burned and sick and dying people, doing their best to survive.
- In one of the towns we pass, we notice a [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] prisoner in a cage, terribly malnourished.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires as to what happened.
	- A woman says they raided the village and that suffering like this is better than he deserves.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] both plead to kill him and be done with his suffering.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] joins in, and asks the villagers to head to castle [[Sunwatch]] for safety and a new life.
	- They agree and begin to make preparations to leave. Roughly 30 of them.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] pulls the prisoner out, is informed he killed their children, then escorts him with some rope to the nearest tree for a short drop.
	- We're presented with another idea by one of the villagers, to take him to [[Oldport]] to present him to Lord [[House Grey|Grey]] and answer for his crimes there.
- On our way to [[Oldport]], we nurse the man back to relative health, while [[Dharshoon]] tries to glean information from him. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] helps.
	- He was with 15,000 other men originally.
	- His particular raiding party was ambushed by the villagers and he was captured.
	- He gives no other relevant info.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] let's [[Brutus]] know of the incoming villagers via [[The Sending Stone|sending stone]].
- We continue to travel for several days.
- Things look war torn here, but on the mend.
- We make it to [[Oldport]], seeing it has been through the hell of battle.
	- The only places that look untouched are the citadel and the castle.
- We enter the city, prisoner in tow.
- Hanging from the walls are the corpses of dead [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]], crows picking at their flesh.
- We encounter the guards at the entrance.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] takes the lead and gets us through with little issue.
	- The guards tell us [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] left recently to deal with some [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] raiders. He should return today.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notes the head guard if from house [[House Astor|Astor]].
- We discern this war torn city was once glorious in its prime. Despite the war, it has fared decently well.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is noticeably less discreet when on the streets of [[Oldport]].
- We head to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s house. It is picked bare, assuming the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] has looted it in recent conflicts.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] hears a voice behind him, and recognizes it has his father [[Matthew Silver|Matthew]].
- They discuss the whereabouts of his sister. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] knows she's safe, to his father's grateful surprise.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] introduces us.
- When [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] introduces [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] as a [[House Sanderfell|Sanderfell]], [[Matthew Silver|Matthew]] recoils. They explain [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] is a defector and is not a threat.
- [[Matthew Silver|Matthew]] is hesitant to trust [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], but is persuaded, but threatens [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] to stay straight.
- We head to castle [[House Tempest|Tempest]], prisoner still in tow.
- We are permitted within, led to the hall of [[Lord Irwin Tempest]], noting he is an old man surrounded by his family.
	- They are sitting in judgement of prisoners. Mostly [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]], but some turncoats as well.
	- Justice is being metered out here in the hall by the executioner.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] steps forward and explains who we are and the prisoner we've brought with us.
- We hear a horn blow, announcing the return of Lord [[House Grey|Grey]].
- He enters the hall in full armor, bloodied from battle. ![[Session 19 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] recognizes [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and addresses him, asking about the prisoner.
- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] has [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] explain the situation, then decrees he should be the one to swing the sword. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] does so, ending the life of the prisoner.
- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] retires to his quarters.
- Another knight in the hall is accused of treason. He claims innocence and demands [[17 Theo's Trial by Combat|trial by combat]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] interjects and accepts the challenge on behalf of his home of [[Oldport]] and house [[House Tempest|Tempest]].
- The knight is amused, and accepts.
- Combat ensues, and the knight makes his attack first.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], well versed in combat with heavily armored opponents, coaches [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] in his fight against the knight.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] performs well, and kills the knight. He presents his sword to Lord [[Lord Irwin Tempest|Irwin]].
- [[Lord Irwin Tempest|Irwin]] congratulates him, urging us to speak with Lord [[House Grey|Grey]] about our business.
- As a party, we approach [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s quarters, noticing it is [[House Cromwell|Cromwell]] men guarding the door, likely associated with [[The Black Stags|the Black Stags]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] recognizes them as Thomas [[Sir Thomas Colridge|Colridge]] and [[Sir John Braxton|John Braxton]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks and audience and is told to wait, as Lord [[House Grey|Grey]] is busy with [[Lord Otto Cromwell|Lord Cromwell]].
- The men are belligerent, but we hold our ground, waiting for an audience next.
- Eventually [[Lord Otto Cromwell|Otto Cromwell]] finishes with Lord [[House Grey|Grey]] and exits the room, noticing [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
- They trade jabs, signaling bad blood between them to the rest of the party.
- [[Lord Otto Cromwell|Otto]] and his knights take their leave.
- We head in, noting several guards and a squire, helping Lord [[House Grey|Grey]] out of his armor.
- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] sees them, and inquiries about his sister.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] acts as the mouthpiece for the terms [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] has been charged with delivering.
- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] inquires more of [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], who explains the situation further, begging him to accept and save his kingdom.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] shares he is now lord of [[Sunwatch]] and the bearer of [[Radiance]].
- He says he'll have an answer by morning, bidding us leave. We do.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notices a figure in the courtyard in all back, slinking through the shadows.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] notices him as well, but also detects also notices [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] interacting with [[Lord Otto Cromwell|Lord Cromwell]].
- We all go back to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s house for the night.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] discloses someone slipped a not into his pocket.
	- The note reads that his sister is safe, but that he has a new job soon. Do it or she'll end up like her husband. [[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Barring]] knows where we are.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] thinks [[The man in black|the man in black]] is most likely with the [[Sable Wardens]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests the stealthier members of the party investigate.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] agrees to stay back while the other 4 party members take to skulking about and investigating.
- They make their way to the docks to where the old [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] camp was located.
- They find a large tent in the center of the old camp.
- Inside they investigate, finding it recently ransacked, a pool of blood on the floor, partially buried under debris.
	- He lifts up the debris, and sees a dead [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] man, throat slit.
	- He notices a tattoo on the man's wrist, ==all in black in the shape of an eye, a star above, a flame below==.
	- [[Dharshoon]] notices the tattoo aligns with the iconography in [[The black tower|the black tower]] in [[Ra'Haad]].
- [[Dharshoon]] leads them to the docks, noting several ships at port.
	- They note several are [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]], likely commandeered after the invasion.
- They board one, noting quiet creaking from below.
- Heading down, they note a single candle illuminating the lower decks. It's burning very low.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Rouen]] notices a green substance below the candle.
	- The substance ignites, green flame quickly engulfing the ship.
	- The party RUNS topside and off the ship as fast as possible, getting just clear of the ship as it explodes in a massive green fireball.
- After taking massive damage, the party collects themselves and notices the other [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] ship leaving port.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Dharshoon]] both take shots at the cloaked figure at the helm.
- They managed to land their shots, killing the man. The body falling into the water.
	- [[Dharshoon]] goes into the water after him, retrieving the body.
- The second boat, ignited by the lightning arrow that [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] shot, erupts in a green fireball.
- City bells toll and guards start rushing the area.
- The body, looking to be from [[The eastern continent|the eastern continent]], bears the same tattoo as the man in the shop.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] identifies this as [[Constantine]], an old rival of [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s, who works for one of the most dangerous crime lords where he comes from.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] fought him when [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] fought and killed [[Ulf]].
- The guards arrive and the party explains what happened. The party is not held, as the guards get things sorted.
- The party reunites with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], grabbing drinks and explaining the situation before they all check in for bed.
- [[Dharshoon]] dreams of a white tree with red leaves, hearing bells in the distance.
	- He recalls the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] in castle [[Sunwatch]].

## Long Rest

- "Bells!" [[Dharshoon]] loudly proclaims as he wakes. Explaining we need to go north and ring some kind of sacred bell, recalling an angel named [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphin]], who is trapped and needs released.
	- He's in quite a state, the rest of us confused and trying to keep up.
	- He takes a breath, reminding us the goddess he used to serve is no goddess at all, but a servant of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
	- She is sealed away by magic bells in the north.
	- [[Anri]] was also one like here.
	- He thinks the bells are connected to the white trees with red leaves, and the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] in [[Sunwatch]].
	- We agree to help if we can.
