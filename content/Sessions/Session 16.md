## Previously

 - [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] was confirmed into the Faith of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] by [[Father James]] after a disturbing dream recounted by [[Dharshoon]] confirmed the theology. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] sought out the faith after speaking with [[Sir Ignatius]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]].
- [[Godric Arden|King Godric]], informed that [[Henry Grey]] was losing the war, granted the party leave to search for [[Sophia Grey]], who had been spotted near [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]]. [[Sir Ignatius]] was tasked to accompany them.
- The party investigated an ancient temple where [[Dharshoon]]'s dream originated. After [[Dharshoon]] destroyed his family's idol by shattering it on the rocks, he rang a bell, summoning a divine, corporeal servant of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] named [[Anri]], who became an ally.
- [[Anri]] led the party underground where they found a massacre of approximately 20 men from [[House Hay]], who were brutally corrupted after being slain by an unknown "beast" that escaped.
- The party encountered a knight in black armor named [[Goroth]], dragon master, who recognized [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and vowed to kill them. The session ended as [[Goroth]], his dragon ([[Ergond]]), and his minions attacked.

## Now

COMBAT (continues)
 - [[Anri]] casts divine magic that allows us all to recover 5 HP at the start of our turns if we're bloodied if we're within 30 ft of her.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] breaks the neck of one of the enemy knights. When he does so, an emblem of fire on the other knights' armor flares. ![[Session 16 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- The dragon flies over near [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and torches them both with its flame breathe. ![[Session 16 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] kills the dragon by cutting through its head as it lunges at him with a missing attack.
- [[Goroth]], the dragon's master, becomes enraged, sprouting wings of red lightning from his back and enshrouding his blade. ![[Session 16 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Goroth]] is all who's left on the battlefield. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] demands his surrender. [[Goroth]] declines, citing "I will bring your bastard head to father." and attacks [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], nearly killing him.
- [[Anri]] continues keeping the party healed up with her holy magic.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], enraged, rushes the man and beats him down, putting a blade through his gullet, ripping the helm off of his head, not recognizing him.
- As [[Goroth]] dies, he swears that "father" has plans for him. The man transforms into a skeletal husk.
- [[Goroth]]'s armor ages rapidly, but his helm, which [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] still holds does not.END COMBAT
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes a hard look at the helmet, and decides he may have use for it. He keeps it, planning to change its appearance to suit his ends.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks [[Anri]] if her task is finished her. She says it is not. She says she will call on us when she is in heed.
- [[Dharshoon]] loots the acolyte he slew and finds the man kept a log of [[Goroth]].
	- He tells us that the log speaks of [[Goroth]] being a skilled dragon tamer, passing on trained dragons to his half siblings. The acolyte worshipped [[Goroth]] as a god. [[Goroth]] intended to gain power through the first dragon he claimed as his own, [[Ergond]]. His intent was to gain enough power to slay his father for the crime of killing [[Goroth]]'s mother [[Tyria]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks for [[Sir Ignatius]]' insight on all of this.
	- He talks of an [[The fallen archangel|archangel]] who fell from the heavens and was imprisoned for his betrayal. He has a powerful lieutenant who does his bidding on the earth. He surmises that [[Goroth]]'s father may in fact be this lieutenant.
- [[Anri]] says while her task is not yet complete, she will carry on on her own and call of us when she has need of us.
- We take a short rest and then get back on the road.
- We emerge outside again and it is early evening.
- Where once we heard utter silence, now we hear birds and wildlife again in the old rafters of the temple.
- [[Sir Ignatius]] informs us he's going to stay behind and see after [[Anri]].
- We return to our horses and set out continuing north along the coast in our search for [[Sophia Grey]]. She is traveling with the rogue knight, [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Sir Garrik]] of [[House Ryder]], a house loyal to [[William Arden]].
- His house if further north along the coast. We suspect we'll find him there.
- We find a small village with an inn further north as the sun begins to set.
- We eat some food and down some ales, discussing the events of the day.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] recounts a dream that he, [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], and [[Dharshoon]] shared.
	- <mark>A dead stag laying in a baren wasteland, a dead next to it filled with magots, a humanoid figure made of shadow reached out them individually before they woke.</mark>
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] shares the dream he and [[Elara]] shared way back before they met up.
	- <mark>They see a field that turns dark as if on fire. We see a city on a hill in the distance burning. In the field below it, we see the boney corpse of a stag with many wounds. A dead lion lies next to it. Between them is a broken sword and crown made of steel and rusting, like they've been there a long time. No sign of life anywhere around. Everything is grey, black, and ash. In the distance we see a dark figure staring at the burning city.</mark>
- We surmise the stag represented [[House Grey]] and the lion represented [[House Arden]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires of the barkeep after a holy man here in the village. He informs us there is a bishop here that holds a service in the morning.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tips the barkeep a gold. The barkeep protests saying that others in the village need it more. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says to pay it forward.
- Just then a man and a girl walk in, both wearing hooded cloaks. They book a room. Perhaps our quarry?
- The man and girl head upstairs to their room. We discuss our suspicions.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] informs the barkeep of our task. The barkeep is spooked. We promise not to involve him.
- We decide on [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] knocking on the door to confront the man and girl, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] waits downstairs in case they get past, and [[Dharshoon]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] position outside their window in case they jump.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] attempts to coax the man out. He aggressively declines. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] throws his shoulder at the door. The man punches him in the face as he bursts in.
- The man is still defiant. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] loses his nerve. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is not backing down.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hears commotion upstairs and heads up.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gets tired of waiting outside and comes inside.
- The man confirms his and [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]]'s identity, putting a blade to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s neck.
- He doesn't believe who we are.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] tosses an amulet of the black stag to [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] so she can hopefully confirm it's legit.
- [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] looks it over and tosses it back to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] without a word.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks her if she wants to go home, she says she doesn't know.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] attempts to grapple [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] uses the opportunity to disengage, but gets neck nicked as he tries to back away.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] runs outside to get [[Dharshoon]] to help heal [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s wound.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] gets [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] alive with a healing potion.
- [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] busts out the window and helps [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] onto the roof, then jumping off onto their horses.
- The party pursues and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] shouts out again in pleading to release the girl to them before things escalate to violence.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] puts [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] in his crosshairs.
- [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] starts crying and agrees to come with us.
- [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] throws down his sword and surrenders her to the party.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] comforts him, telling him he did the right thing. He asks [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] to come back with them and make his case to [[Godric Arden|King Godric]].
- [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] is hesitant, worried he'll be punished for swearing fealty to [[William Arden]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says that [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is in need of allies in a losing war and that he'll vouch for him. [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] declines.
- The town guard comes and we convince them to stand down.
- We're not welcome in the inn so make camp outside of town, then return to [[Brightguard]].
- On the return trip, [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] tells us how she was threatened by the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]. [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] heard all of this and broke his fealty to [[William Arden]] and the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]], worried for her safety.
- We arrive a few days later in Brightgaurd, turning over [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] to the guard there.
- We come across [[Sir John Marshall|John Marshall]] who congratulates us.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is told to wait outside [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s chambers while he talks to the queen.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] overhears them discussing a "madman" who's half mad that she wants nowhere near their daughter, but [[Godric Arden|Godric]] thinks he's harmless.
- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is pleased that [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] has been captured. He's disappointed that we didn't apprehend [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks about the "madman" he overheard about.
	- A shipwreck from the east a few days ago washed up a fool ([[Jingle Bell|jester]]) who's half mad.
- [[Henry Grey]] and his armies were able to recapture [[Oldport]].
- We are to deliver terms to [[Henry Grey]]. He will have his sister returned to him once he swears fealty to [[Godric Arden|Godric]], still able to retain his title of [[Henry Grey|King in the South]].
- We hear coming from a voice in the castle: <mark>“The red wolves feast on stags in the forest, to burning men cheering in dreadful chorus! I know! I know! Oh yes I know!”</mark> In a sing-song voice.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] explains what he was told about the half crazed fool they found in the shipwreck.
