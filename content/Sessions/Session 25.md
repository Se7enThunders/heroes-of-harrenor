**Quests in play:** [[22 Wake Guinevere|Wake Guinevere]]

## Previously

 - Party escapes hostile [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’Haadi]] town after killing pursuing guards and crosses into the [[The Blight]], where several members suffer visions of a burning ruined city and dreams of a [[The molten demon|molten demon]].
- Strange floating shadow spirits stalk the group through sandstorms; [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stelaris]] reveals the ancient glowing sword [[Starbinder]] and shares his fading but persistent hope.
- Group discovers the lost city of [[Telandor]], apparently preserved and guarded by a nameless immortal survivor who claims to have waited 3,000 years for deliverance.
- Party fights through hostile defenders at [[Telandor]]’s inner gate, then reaches the hilltop church where records speak of protecting “[[The Kilns|the Kiln]]” and a sleeping Lady tied to the final bell.
- [[Dharshoon]] awakens the sleeping woman, extinguishing [[The Kilns|the Kiln]] and collapsing [[Telandor]]’s illusion into true ruin; [[Telandor's guide|the guide]] reveals this has scattered its power into the world, and [[The molten demon|the molten demon]] from the party’s nightmares rises behind them.

## Now

## Combat

- The party begins attacking the [[The molten demon|demon]], noting physical attacks light us on fire, and it's hide resists most attacks. ![[Session 25 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices this and runs quickly to [[Dharshoon]] who quickly blesses his weapon for the fight. ![[Session 25 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- The party and their allies ([[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]], [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] ([[Godfrey the Squire|Balon's squire]]), and [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]]) quickly surround the [[The molten demon|demon]].
- The [[The molten demon|demon]] takes advantage of this and takes a huge swing with its cleaver, damaging many of the party.
- It begins to burn brighter as it pulls on the flame from the areas around us, then suddenly we are in total darkness.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] beckons us to silence.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] shouts out to [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] to see he can clear the magical darkness. He thinks he might be able to.
- He holds [[Starbinder]] aloft, it's glow shining as bright as we've ever seen, dispelling the darkness. ![[Session 25 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- The [[The molten demon|demon]] reappears on a spire of rock nearby. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] attacks it and is knocked back, unconscious.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] charges the [[The molten demon|demon]] and knocks it off the spire.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], sensing an opportunity, rushes the [[The molten demon|demon]], coming from behind, and strikes quickly, slashing its gut then neck with precise blows, decapitating it.
- It explodes in a blast of flame as it dies damaging several, but killing none.END COMBAT - The remains of the [[The molten demon|demon]] crumble to ash and float away in the wind.
- We notice the fires that were burning have now gone out. It is dark and the cloudy sky makes it darker.
- The only light we see is from remaining fires too distant for the [[The molten demon|demon]] to have drawn upon.
- We do notice a glowing ember from the [[The molten demon|demon]] that does not dim with time. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] investigates it.
	- It's a crystal with something moving inside of it.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] takes it, wrapping it, and storing it to investigate it later, giving it to his squire.
- We move out, not resting first.
- We see a flash of orange light in the sky in the distance, then see glowing embers rain down throughout the city.
- We wonder as to the nature of the "Dark Below" we were told to seek out.
- A large church in the distance seems to be the best place to start.
- As we head back into the city, the state of the ruined city is impossibly bad.
	- Bodies hang and rot, still decaying after 3000 years.
	- A nearly dead husk of a person crawls along our path, dragging its feet behind it.
	- We hear it muttering "[[Guinevere|Gwenivere]], please help us. The red beast is come to eat us."
	- We offer to help, but it only calls out for [[Guinevere|Gwenivere]], pointing at the church, then dying.
- As we approach the church we hear wolf-like howls in the distance, only wrong.
- Most of us hear what we agree are the wings of a large creature and duck to cover.
- We feel wind and dust kick up as a massive creature flies overhead.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] risk a peak and looks out, seeing the form of a massive [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]].
- We see a single shaft of moonlight that illuminates a section of the city for just a moment.
- We can tell the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] is patrolling the city, likely looking for us.
- We decide to check out the church first, then whatever the light ray was illuminating, as the church is closer.
- [[Dharshoon]] shrouds us in magically from detection.
- We find our path blocked by undead.
	- We decide to try and sneak around them after the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]]'s next pass. We get in position.
	- On the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]]'s next pass, we notice it's scales are red, but are blackened as well.
	- We sneak past the undead successfully.
- The path to the church is now open before us.
- We approach the church, noting the broken gates and the corpses that surround it, many church guards.
	- Among the dead we note bodies wearing all black.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] deduces these are early members of the [[Sable Wardens]].
- While the roof is mostly collapsed, there is enough cover inside to protect us from the church.

## Short Rest

- Inside the church we see a great number of charred corpses and upwards of 10,000 gold coins scattered about.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] finds a body strewn across on alter, clutching a notebook talking about catacombs beneath the church.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] digs through the coins, assessing the wealth and claiming some of it. He causes a clatter and we hear a roar.
	- We assess we have roughly a minute before the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] is upon us.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] opens a passageway below the alter. We all head inside right before the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] arrives. ![[Session 25 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
	- We escape below unscathed, but it knows we're here now.
- Caskets line the walls. Some of which appear to have been opened, the bodies dragged out, but long ago.
- In one of the caskets, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] finds a skeleton holding a parchment in elven script.
	- The parchment mentions the [[Swords of the Church]], describing a rite of penitence.
- The catacombs appear to be vast and expansive. We begin our exploration.
	- The first room we explore is a barracks, just off the main hallway.
	- We find 80 gold and a roster of the men who resided here.
	- We find a grate on the south side of the room, that [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] discovers a mechanism to open and does so.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] picks the lock on another door, behind which are 3 zombies. He sneaks in and attacks.
	- We make short work of the zombies, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] killing one, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] killing two.
	- A room connected to the one we found the zombies in contains a corpse. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] investigates.
		- It's holding a key.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] also sees a small group of northmen-like berserkers.
	- We make quick work of them as well, taking minimal damage.
	- [[Dharshoon]] opens a door within this room, revealing another corridor.
	- He finds gold and several gems within, as well as a note explaining an [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] in the heart of the city that always receives light each day.
