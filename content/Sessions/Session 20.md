# Session 20

← [[Session 19]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 20|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 21]] →

## Previously

- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is named lord of [[Sunwatch]] and claims _Radiance_. The party invests gold, starts repairs, and lays groundwork to repopulate the castle.
- Traveling to [[Oldport]], the party sees widespread devastation. A [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’Haadi]] prisoner is spared execution and brought along for judgment; refugees are sent to [[Sunwatch]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] reunites with his father. [[Henry Grey|King Henry Grey]] returns from battle and has [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] execute the prisoner. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] also wins a [[17 Theo's Trial by Combat|trial by combat]], earning respect.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] present [[Godric Arden]]’s offer—[[Sophia Grey]] for [[Henry Grey|Henry]]’s fealty. [[Lord Otto Cromwell|Otto Cromwell]]’s hostility signals looming internal conflict.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is threatened over his sister. Investigation at the docks reveals saboteurs tied to the [[The black tower|Black Tower]]; green fire destroys ships. [[Constantine]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]’s old rival, is killed.
- [[Dharshoon]] dreams of white trees and bells, claiming an angel ([[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphin]]) is bound in the north. The party agrees to pursue this lead.NOW
- The group gears up for the coming journey, while still in [[Oldport]].
- The weather has turned and a significant amount of snow is coming down.
- Several guards hold us up from going to see [[Henry Grey]] to get his answer.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stelaris]] has arrived and vouches for us.
	- He has [[Sir Eamon Douglas|Eamon Douglas]] as a hostage, to ensure Lord Callum's daughter weds [[Henry Grey]].
	- Lord Callum is here presently.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks after [[Baylor Stelaris|Balor]]. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] is unsure as to his whereabouts.
	- Both parties enter the castle and they part ways.
- Inside, there are many VIPs gathered. The hearths warm the interior.
- We approach the king's quarters, guarded by 2 of his household knights.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks after [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s decision. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] are expected and permitted entry.
	- Inside the king warms himself and beckons us to his table, furnished with a decanter of wine.
	- He begs us sit. He explains he has accepted [[Godric Arden|King Godric]]'s offer. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is elated.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Henry]] declares a toast and has his cupbearer pour wine for all of us. We toast.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] asks for a private word with [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]. The rest of us step out.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] declares that once the war is over, he'll officially declare [[Sunwatch]] to be under [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s lordship.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] will still be lord in all but name in the meantime.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] says he'll tell the other lords about the alliance with the [[House Arden|Ardens]] within the next few hours.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] says we'll be there.
- Meanwhile [[Dharshoon]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] wait outside.
	- [[Dharshoon]] notices a man in house [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] colors (red fox) talked to a man with blonde hair and nothing marking any house allegiance.
	- The [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] man gives the blonde man a parchment discretely.
	- [[Dharshoon]] opts to follow the blonde man out of the castle grounds.
	- The entire party sans [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] chase after the blonde man.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] finishes up and sees us in the courtyard. He catches up quickly.
	- [[Dharshoon]] tracks the man, leading the charge. Snow falling even more heavily.
	- The trail leads us to the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]] Tavern.
	- We approach the tavern. It's very busy.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] advises caution considering the fresh agreement to alliance and how tenuous that agreement is.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] waits outside while the rest of the party ventures in.
	- [[Dharshoon]] spots the man meeting a woman at a corner table.
		- She is finely dressed and well put together. She sticks out in the drab tavern.
	- The party tries to look casual. [[Dharshoon]] finds a nearby table and listens in on the conversation.
	- He overhears them discussing a reward and a mutual friend, he wishing to see her again, and she wishing the opposite.
	- She gets up and leaves after planting a kiss on his cheek. She is pretty, has black hair, and light skin.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] follows her.
	- Once outside, [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notices her looking for tails.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] opt to stay behind and get a drink.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] follows the woman. [[Dharshoon]] follows [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] tails the woman she notices him. She continues onward.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] ducks into a bakery for a moment so she thinks she lost him. The baker is confused.
	- [[Dharshoon]] catches up. He asks the baker about honey buns. The baker is out of stock. But not to worry! [[Dharshoon]] has his own honey for regular buns! (funny moment)
- Meanwhile back at the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] is getting stared down by a man in a brown cloak with a dagger.
	- He clocks the man and carries on a conversation with [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]], noting the man to him.
	- The man appears to be having a conversation with someone.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] opts to leave, bumping into the blonde man.
	- He notices the blonde man is wearing a pin of a silver mocking bird.
	- Once outside, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] attacks the blonde man. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] follows.
	- The man is understandably upset. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] says they're going to talk about the pin.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] grabs the pin and tosses it at [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]'s feet. It's the same as the Bandit Lord at [[Sunwatch]].
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] has [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] fetch [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] sees [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] pinning the man down.
	- They show [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] the pin. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] grabs the man and pins him against the wall, asking him to explain himself.
	- The man says he's an information runner. It's the woman we should be looking for.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tosses him to the side, noting he's just a courier. The man curses us and runs off.
- [[Dharshoon]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] return, explaining they lost the woman.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] says we should track the man the blonde man was conversing with.
	- [[Dharshoon]] begins tracking him, taking us to a plaza in the city, eerily with not a soul present.
	- It's roughly 11:00 AM.
	- We do see a lone man standing on a platform in the center of the plaza.
	- He's a very tall man in far-eastern style armor.
	- Gallows lie behind him.
	- The snow lessens in intensity.
	- We notice the ways in and out of the plaza are blocked.
	- He addresses [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], noting the bounty on his head.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] asks for a name. He isn't given one.
	- 5 other men comes from the shadows.

## Combat

 - [[The bounty hunter]] and his retinue are incredibly skilled a durable, some equipped with magic weapons.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] manages to knock out [[The Janissary|the Janissary]], leaving him alive.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] kills the sellsword, uppercutting him and breaking his neck.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] takes out the axeman with a 1-2 slash of his rapier and dagger, opening the man's neck.
- [[Dharshoon]] puts an arrow through [[The bounty hunter|the bounty hunter]]'s skull as [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] fights a losing battle against him.
- We clean up the remaining fighters.

## End Combat

 - [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] shakes awake [[The Janissary|the Janissary]] awake who tries to attack and get a way. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] crushes his hands.
	- The man is only speaking [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]]. [[Dharshoon]] says they were hired by the men in black, the [[Sable Wardens]].
	- The party wants to hang [[The Janissary|the Janissary]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wants no part of it and walks out of the plaza.
	- The group discusses and decides instead to hand him over to the [[House Grey|Greys]].
- We loot the remaining bodies and each claim 100 gp per man.
