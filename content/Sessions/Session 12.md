# Session 12

← [[Session 11]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 12|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 13]] →

## Previously

- The party arrived at the freed-slave city of [[Larad]] and learned from [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]] that its location had been compromised, forcing a hurried evacuation north.
- A messenger from [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan|the Emir]] arrived, demanding [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] turn over the slaves, confirming the city was a target.
- The party and [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s mercenaries, along with newly-armed freemen including a [[Harrenor|Harrenorian]] named [[Tiberius]], defended the refugees against a massive [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] force in the open desert.
- During the climactic battle, [[Therond]] the dragon arrived and single-handedly destroyed the enemy army, fulfilling his vow to [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] and saving the refugees.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] was mortally wounded during the fight; with his dying breath, he entrusted [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] with leading the people to safety. The party then began the long march to [[Grimhold]].

## Now

 - After nearly 3 months in [[Ra'Haad]], the party returns to [[Harrenor]] territory through [[Grimhold]].
- Winter has come. The land and castle are covered in snow.
- [[Torren Grimston]] greets the party and the large group of freedmen who now join us.
- A total of nearly 600 souls have come with us north the [[Harrenor]].
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] lead the discussion with him, catching him up.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s body has been bound and preserved by [[Dharshoon]], and is still carried by the freedmen.
- [[House Grimston|Grimston]] says he'll be buried at [[Grimhold]] with honors.
- [[House Grimston|Grimston]] wastes no time getting us into quarters, wishing to meet with us to debrief within the hour.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] has words about his distaste for heat and sand, and how he's glad to be back north in cold weather.
- [[Tiberius]] is united with his family--a wife, daughter, and son.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] shares a letter he was handed by a spy here in [[Grimhold]] on their return.
	- It details his sister and her husband being held at the [[Blackbear Inn]], 3 days ride from here.
	- He has a 2 week deadline to show up.
	- He is to "come alone" if he's to see them alive again. An obvious trap.
- We arrive with [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] at [[House Grimston|Grimston]]'s meeting, a small feast prepared for us.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] prays over the food and toasts [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]], inviting us to eat.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] TEARS into the food, famished and ready for red meat again.
	- He tears into an entire rack of beef ribs.
- While we were away, the war has not gone well.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] knows little of [[Godric Arden|Godric]] and his forces. He's cut off and holed up in the northeast for the winter.
- Before the winter set in, [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] pirates sacked [[Oldport]].
- They also sacked [[Stag's Rest]] and killed [[Carter Grey]], [[Henry Grey]]'s younger brother.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] is back to [[Ra'Haad]] to remain a spy there.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] presses [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] on if he's [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s son.
	- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] doesn't know for sure, but he feels he might have been.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] brings up the letter he showed [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], outing himself as a turncoat spy.
- [[Lord Thomas Baring|Thomas Baring]] is the man he worked for. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] recommends he be assassinated.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests asking about enlisting [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] and through her [[Therond]] to aid in the war.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is extremely hesitant. The group is interested, but doesn't want to step on toes.
- We table the dragon idea and discuss more specific options for [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]'s family dilemma.
- We agree subterfuge would be idea in this situation and discuss a plan.
- We discuss recruiting any men from among the freedmen who want
- We decide to stay for [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s funeral tomorrow, then hammer out the finer details of the plan on the road.
- We rest for the evening and awaken to an additional half foot of snow.
- The funeral for [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] is solemn and lovely. ![[Session 12 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
	- A resurrection is mentioned. This confuses [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is standing next to [[Tiberius]] and asks about the resurrection.
	- [[Tiberius]] explains as best he can, but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is still confused.
- The bishop stops [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] after the ceremony, stating he'd heard the questions he was asking. He offers to answer questions.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is happy for this, but is pressed for time to move out to help [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]]'s family.
	- The bishop speaks a blessing on [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] who welcomes in earnestly.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] says his goodbyes to [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]], entrusting her to summon [[Therond]] if necessary.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] talks to [[Tiberius]] about joining. He does not, but gives intel on the [[Blackbear Inn]].
	- It's by a river and is very isolated.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gathers the freedmen who will listen to him and gives a speech, begging some of them to help rescue Rauoen's sister and brother-in-law.
- Only two men, [[Marcus Freeman|Marcus]] and [[Lucias Freeman]], are the names of the men who agree to help us. ![[Session 12 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- A smith and a merchant from [[The colonies|the colonies]].
	- They have some, but limited combat experience. Better with swords than bows.
- The seven of us set off.
- We travel through the [[Giant's Gap]]. A large valley through mountains on [[Harrenor]]'s border with [[Ra'Haad]].
- On our second night, we travel through small wooded area. Finding a clearing as the sun sets.
- As we're about to make camp, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] sees a flash a fire, a persistent pillar of flame, about a hundred yards away.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] sneaks up and sees it is a greatsword that is burning, driven into the snow and ground.
	- He sees no one around it.
	- He waves the party forward.
- As we approach, a [[The man in black|man in black]] garb (whom [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] didn't see) approaches the sword, grabs it while glowing hot and faces the party.
- We are quickly surrounded by three other men dressed and armed similarly.
- One of the men addresses us with disdain, swearing vengeance for killing their lord.
	- They are [[The Black Hands|Black Hands]], sworn to the [[Sable Wardens]].

COMBAT!

- [[The Black Hands]] are fast, deadly, well-armed, and armored.
- Their blades cut deep, but we focus our attacks and kill 3 of them in short order.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] yells out, "Your master died in vain, and so shall you!"
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] does an excellent job of making his way behind engaged targets, finishing them off quickly.
- Eventually one remains. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] engages, making sure he knows his master died a whimpering coward.
- The final [[The Black Hands|Black Hand]] stands his ground, dealing a massive blow against [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], knocking him out.
- As the last [[The Black Hands|Black Hand]] falls, the fire from the swords of the fallen leaves the swords, turning them to ash, and coalesces around the lead knight. ![[Session 12 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rushes the resurrected fire knight, dealing damage, but getting pummeled in return. [[Session 12 art 4.mp4|🖼]]
- The fire knight does serious damage before [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] finally sneaks in and scores a hit through the visor slit in his helmet, finally killing him.

## End Combat

 - As we collect ourselves and recover from the battle, [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] feels [[The Palantir|the Palantir]] brimming with energy.
- He uncovers it and senses it will harm him if he touches it with his bare hands, covering it again.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] is aghast that he actually stole it.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] feels called to it, sensing he has the ability to use it.
- We find 200 GP on the bodies.
- We finish setting up camp.
