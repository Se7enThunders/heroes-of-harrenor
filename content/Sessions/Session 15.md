## Previously

 - The party learned that [[Sophia Grey]] had escaped. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] was denied funds at the bank (his family owes 100k gold), while [[Dharshoon]] received an outlandish franchising offer from [[James Goldblum]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] questioned local children and learned [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] was seen leaving [[Kingsmont]] with someone disguised in [[House Arden|Arden]] colors. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] overheard [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]] and [[Catherine Lancaster]] confirming her escape and revealing their new hostage: [[Sir Eamon Douglas]].
- The next morning, the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] offered to trade [[Sir Eamon Douglas|Eamon Douglas]] instead of [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]]. After the exchange was tabled, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]] ordered the party to ride to [[Brightguard]] to check if [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] had [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
- En route to [[Brightguard]], the party salvaged the gear of a fallen [[House Arden|Arden]] knight (including a sword with a green emerald) and restocked on supplies from the mysterious old man with the donkey cart.
- At [[Brightguard]], [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] gifted [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] a set of plate armor and introduced them to [[Sir Ignatius]], a knight of [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]], before tasking the party to visit with [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] while he considered an alliance with [[Henry Grey]].

## Now

 - [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] armors up and heads back to the king's chambers to speak with [[Sir Ignatius]].
- [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] asks about more the party's time in south and their encounters with [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]].
- [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] mentions they're spreading all over the continent including [[Varanor]], [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s home.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires about joining [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]].
- He's told it is possible to remain in service to a king and be in [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] all at once, though he must join the faith of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] to do so.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is interested in this and will seek out the bishop of the castle to discuss joining the faith.
- [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] seeks out [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and reunites with him. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gives her a [[Strange Coins|strange coin]]. She gives him a [[Brooch of the Almighty|broach of the Almighty]] to pin to his armor. He does so.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes the rest of the day to reintegrate with the other soldiers and [[The King's Guard|King's Guard]] in the castle, noting they've suffered minimal losses and moral is still up.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] seeks after the family of the fallen soldier to deliver the sword and report on his death and burial.
- [[Dharshoon]] is seeking out a holy man and finds [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] for directions. They both go together.
- We arrive at the chapel. It is beautiful gothic architecture.
- [[Father James]] comes to speak with us.
- [[Dharshoon]] begins to recount a disturbing dream he had the previous night.
	- He recalls the goddess of his family, [[Beladishpi]], deity of nature.
	- He dreamt she came to him and told him she serves [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
	- He was in a clearing in a wood, with a bell tower in the center.
	- A creature of pure light (the goddess) amongst many woodland creatures.
	- She tells him not to worship him and that she is just a steward of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
	- She tells him to ring the bell in the tower. He does so and wakes up.
- [[Father James]] explains that this dream supports the theology within the worship of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
- He recommends destroying the idol [[Dharshoon]] possesses as they are often cursed.
- [[Dharshoon]] considers all of this. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] comforts him, telling him he is lucky to have a goddess who still serves [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]. Those of his home are corrupt and evil and desire worship and sacrifice.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] talks to [[Father James]] and expresses his desire to join the faith.
- [[Father James]] walks him through the steps of the prayer to become a follower of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] prays and rises a new man, reborn in the faith of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
- After [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s confirmation, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] steps out of the shadows and confronts [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
	- He is thinking much more about religion now than he ever has.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], who was also hiding in the shadows, leaves and goes for a walk to clear his head.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], also there, sits in a back row pew and takes it all in.
- [[Dharshoon]] concludes he is going to destroy the idol, but is stricken by a feeling of dread.
- A guard comes and retrieves [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], noting the king has urgent news.
	- John and [[Lord William Marshall|William Marshall]] are present, as well as [[Lord Adam Croft]].
	- We're told [[Henry Grey]] just lost a third of his footmen trying [[Green Harbor]] in [[The Gilded Lands|the Gilded Lands]].
	- The king's spies are bringing word that [[Sophia Grey]] and the mysterious knight with her have been spotted in the area around [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks to take leave to search for her. He is granted this. [[Sir Ignatius]] is tasked to go with them.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] party departs that day at midday.
- It is raining.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] departs momentarily to deliver the sword of the fallen knight ([[Sir Amory]]) to family of the man. He tells us he'll catch up shortly.
	- He delivers the sword. The family didn't know. His young boy is there. They are devastated. ![[Session 15 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
	- The widow is too distraught to speak to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] comforts the boy and hands him a [[Strange Coins|strange coin]], telling him he's the man of the house now.
	- He departs and reunites with the party.
- The countryside is sparse in the winter months and people are stretched to the edge of their means.
- We're heading northwest through [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]].
- As we get into rockier terrain, we note an seemingly abandoned temple complex high atop a cliffside. ![[Session 15 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Dharshoon]] notices a bell tower standing tall in the structure that reminds him of his dream.
- [[Dharshoon]] asks [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] about it and how it might tie to his dream.
- [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] thinks it's worth a look and says to bring it to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s attention.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thinks it's worth a look as well and asks [[Dharshoon]] to discuss it with the party.
- [[Dharshoon]] shares about his dream. The party agrees to investigate.
- We notices fresh horse tracks. [[Dharshoon]] estimates 20-30 sets of horse tracks.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] scouts ahead with [[Dharshoon]].
	- They don't see any signs of life in the temple area. They wave the rest of the group forward.
- We investigate the ruins and as [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] touches the door, we hear a booming voice warning us away.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] reminds them that we're in [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]] and searching for a lost noble here. We have every right to be here.
- We open the gate to proceed and no one is here.
- We continue to investigate and find no signs of life, not even rats or mice.
- [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] informs us it's thousands of years old and was once meant to worship [[The Almighty|the Almighty]].
- There are no tracks past the steps outside the temple.
- We find the base of the belltower. It is precarious, but [[Dharshoon]] starts a climb anyway.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] attempts to join him, but can't make the climb.
- [[Dharshoon]] reaches the belltower, noting the bell is in perfect condition. There are no inscriptions on it.
	- It is the same bell from his dream.
- [[Dharshoon]] grabs his idol and throws it out the window, shattering it on the rocks below.
- [[Dharshoon]] feels freed of a dark burden.
- Then, he rings the bell. It rings clear and clean It sounds perfect.
- As the ringing softens, [[Dharshoon]] feels like there's someone behind him.
- He turns to see a young woman in her early 20s. She wears a green robe depicting nature and the wilds. ![[Session 15 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
	- She says she's a [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] of the her mistress [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]]. ![[Session 15 art 4.png|hh-pop]] [[Session 15 art 5.mp4|🖼]]
	- She descends down stairs that no longer exist.
	- The party can see her, and see that she is divine in nature.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is intensely curious about her nature.
	- She is human and corporeal. She volunteered herself to be hidden away until a time would come to cleanse this place.
	- She is a servant of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]], which makes her an ally.
	- Her name is [[Anri]].
- She tells us she is bidden to show us what once plagued the holy site. She guides us to an old cathedral on the temple grounds.
- The doors open on their own for her. She notes others came here first.
- There is a stairway leading down.
- She says long ago, [[Artorius|Sir Artoreus]] placed a boon over the site to ward of those who do not belong.
- We follow her down the stairway. It is very dark.
- [[Dharshoon]] casts light on [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s staff to light the way.
- We descend about 30 ft and continue through the depths of the passageway below.
- Freshly slain bodies of men wearing white tunics emblazoned with the symbol of the almighty and crossed battle axes beneath them litter the hallways.
- [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] says they're men of house [[House Hay|Hay]].
- The bodies have been butchered brutally. Where the slash and stab wounds have corruption around them.
- There are roughly 20 bodies.
- We hear a man coughing up ahead. We approach cautiously.
- He wound is grievous. He will die soon. He is still conscious.
- He speaks of [[Sir Horace]], the knight who led them here and a beast who slew them all and escaped.
- He tells us it's imperative it isn't allowed to out of here.
- We come into large room, where a [[The man in black|man in black]] armor with a black blade fighting a knight of [[House Hay]], scoring a killing blow on him the moment we arrive.
- He pulls his sword from the body and challenges us.
- He knows who [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is, stating his Lord Father will want to know of him.
- He names himself as [[Goroth]], dragon master and vows to kill us.
- He, his dragon ([[Ergond]]), and several minions attack
