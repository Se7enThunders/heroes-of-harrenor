# Session 27

← [[Session 26]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 27|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 28]] →

## Previously

- The party uncovered ancient records beneath [[Telandor]] revealing the history of the [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]], the holy [[The Kilns|kiln]], [[Guinevere|Gwenivere]], [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]]'s hidden altar, and the [[Sable Wardens]]' pursuit of immortality, confirming the catacombs hold far more than a simple crypt.
- The heroes discovered the underground [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] bathed in perpetual moonlight, but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] accidentally triggered an ancient alarm, drawing the blackened [[The red dragon of Telandor|red dragon]] that collapsed the chamber and forced a tactical retreat before the magical bow beside [[The Watcher|the Watcher]]'s body could be recovered.
- While regrouping, the party found the catacombs subtly changing around them as gargoyle statues appeared to move and previously unseen passages opened, suggesting powerful magic or an active guardian within the ruins.
- An ambush against a group of [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] led to a running fight through hidden corridors. Despite one [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]]'s attempted escape through a secret door using a magical amulet, the party hunted him down, recovered a life-stealing [[The Crimson Gladius|crimson gladius]], and continued deeper underground.
- Descending into the lowest chambers, the party discovered a vast crypt filled with the fallen [[Swords of the Church]], where the legendary [[The Grand Marshal|Grand Marshal]] still stood wielding a radiant [[The Grand Marshal's Sword|white blade]] against undead warriors before turning to confront the intruders as more corpses began to rise.NOW - COMBAT - [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] calls out to focus our attention on [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]], theorizing the undead under his command may fall if/when he does.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] charges in first, attacking [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]] with blows that land well.
- The party all begins attacking [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]]. ![[Session 27 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- We notice our non-magical attacks deal limited damage.
- We get solid hits in on him, but he quickly commands his undead forces to attack us too.
- We realize that as the undead are being destroyed, part of their essence is absorbed by [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]].
- [[Dharshoon]] heals [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] with a healing prayer.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] charges in with [[Starbinder]], to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s defense.
- [[Dharshoon]] discerns that [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]] is not the entity controlling the undead. He shouts out to the party.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] lands the finishing blow on [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]], the undead around him falling dead once more.
- As we celebrate the kill, dark arcane energy flows through the room and into [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]].
- The body of [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]] bursts with dark arcane energy, springing back to life and attacking [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. ![[Session 27 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] lands the killing blow by cutting off [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]]'s hand, catching his blade, then decapitating him with both blades at once.

## End Combat

 - [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] holds the blade and sees its ==magical shine as if white glass== quickly fade as the curse holding it in time.
	- It is magical, but damaged. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] stores it on his person to investigate later.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices Varanorian runes sealing a nearby door, the runes glowing like embers.
- We decide to take short rest to recover.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] starts putting bodies in the pyre while the others investigate the room for a way to open the door.
- We find an old parchment on the body of [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]].
	- It reads that he was under orders from the city lord to retreat to the catacombs and protect the [[The Iron Fist's captive|maiden]]. The city above was charged its defense to the "[[The Iron Fist|Iron Fist]]" from the north, but he betrayed them. [[The Grand Marshal]] was trapped here to either be rescued or die trapped.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] continues his investigation on how to open the door.
	- He notices there a 8+1 symbols on the door, and 8 braziers in the room + the big pyre in the middle.
	- All runes are glowing on the door, all the braziers and the pyre is lit.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] extinguishes one of the braziers and the corresponding symbol on goes dim, accompanied by a terrible scream.
	- He continues to do so, each rune on the door going out and agonizing screams accompanying with them.
	- Once all braziers are extinguished, from the pyre in the center we see 9 figures with the flames stand and their essence dispersed with a sigh of relief.
	- The door opens revealing a long hallway.
- As we move in, we hear some noise from behind us.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] offers to cover our six with his squire and [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] offers his thanks and blessing if they don't meet again.
- We move through the hallway in a grand hall with a throne, on which sits a massive Northman, [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]. ![[Session 27 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
	- His hand rests on the shoulder of a young woman. He's flanked by two lackeys.
	- He taunts us, then notices [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], then turns to the woman saying "he won't be needing her now" and snaps her neck.
	- His attention is focused on [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], mostly, but he notices [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], noting that they share a father.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says it's a pity there are as many of them as there are and promises to end [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]'s life.

## Combat

 - [[The Iron Fist]] flies swiftly from his throne and closes the distance to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], attacking him, then spinning off to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
- [[Dharshoon]] rushes to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and lays his hands and a blessing on his blade, making it magical.
- After a few rounds of combat, [[Dharshoon]] dispatches one of the henchmen.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is knocked out by [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]].
