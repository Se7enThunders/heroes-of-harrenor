# Session 17

← [[Interlude 16.5]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 17|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 18]] →

## Previously

- The party completed the grueling combat at the temple: [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] killed the dragon ([[Ergond]]), and the party defeated [[Goroth]], who was revealed to be a powerful minion whose father is likely the lieutenant of a [[The fallen archangel|fallen Archangel]].
- The party tracked their target and found [[Sophia Grey]] with [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Sir Garrik]] of [[House Ryder]] at a village inn. The confrontation failed, resulting in a brief fight where [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]] nicked [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] before escaping onto the roof.
- [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] ultimately surrendered to the party after [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] pleaded with [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]], who then broke fealty with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] and surrendered her. The party did not apprehend [[Sir Garrik Ryder|Garrik]], respecting his motives for protecting [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
- The party returned to [[Brightguard]] and handed [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] over. They learned [[Henry Grey]] recaptured [[Oldport]] and that [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] was preparing terms to force [[House Grey|Grey]] into fealty in exchange for his sister.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] overheard [[Godric Arden|Godric]] and the Queen discussing a new court fixture: a "madman" [[Jingle Bell|jester]] found in a shipwreck. The session concluded with the [[Jingle Bell|jester]]'s chilling, sing-song prophecy about "==red wolves feast[ing] on stags==."[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] has an unsettling dream wherein he's confronted by his father and a dark version of himself, calling on him to fulfill his purpose as the son of the god of war.

## Now

- We rest and awake the next day, preparing to head out.
- ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wants to go see a priest of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] and inquire as to the meanings of the party's dreams)
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wants to find out more about this broken minded fool who speaks in riddles.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] addresses [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] in the morning, wondering how he feels about the situation with [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] doesn't love it, but understands war is war.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] agrees, citing he is no ally of the [[House Grey|Greys]].
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] states he's not planning on swearing fealty to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] any time soon, but does agree with him in this.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] leaves to gather breakfast.
- The party gathers in dining hall to throw down a filling meal before hitting the road.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] shares part of his dream with the party from the night before.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] recalls a vision he had when holding the [[The Palantir|palantir]].
	- One was of Rowan stabbing [[Henry Grey]] in the heart.
	- Another of [[Grimhold]] on fire.
	- Lastly, a vision of stars falling.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] shares of a dream he had of stabbing [[Henry Grey]] as well, months before.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wonders aloud who or what could help them through these shared visions.
- [[Dharshoon]] asks [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] if he thinks [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] has rescued him from this past of his.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thinks so, but still worries.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thanks the group for coming as far as they have with him and that he values their friendship.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] wishes to discuss the fate of [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] privately with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
- We finish and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] step aside.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] desperately wishes a scenario exists where [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] can be freed and returned home.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wants this too, but sees no way forward with it.
	- This upsets [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], but he's understanding.
	- The conversation ends with an understanding between the two.
- Meanwhile, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] is still in the mess, doing his best to get drunk.
- He gets a good buzz and meets up with [[Dharshoon]]. They agree to find the [[Jingle Bell|jester]] and question him.
- They find him accompanying [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] and her escort.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] carries a lute, and starts singing a song to get the [[Jingle Bell|jester]]'s attention.
	- The [[Jingle Bell|jester]] seems completely oblivious to it. Sing strangly the verse,
	- The two feel awkward about approaching the princess so go to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and ask him to come along.
	- He does so, wondering as to their awkwardness.
	- He finds her in the courtyard reading a book, the [[Jingle Bell|jester]] nearby.
	- [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] says she calls the [[Jingle Bell|jester]] [[Jingle Bell]] and that he's harmless.
	- Her guards aren't so sure, and are with [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] by order of the Queen.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] charges [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] with being a good friend to [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] while they're away. She agrees. ![[Session 17 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] gives [[Olivia Arden|Oliva]] her [[The Black Stag Pendant|black stag pendant]] to give to [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] confronts the [[Jingle Bell|jester]] and tells him that if any harm comes to [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] by his hand, he'll suffer by [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s.
	- [[Jingle Bell]] seemingly ignores [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
	- As the party departs, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] just hears [[Jingle Bell]] say,
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is convinced the [[Jingle Bell|jester]] is an oracle of some kind and wishes to report this to [[Godric Arden|King Godric]].
	- The party aside from [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] joins him.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] heads to [[Father James]] to discuss the dreams the party has been having. He tells all.
	- [[Father James]] admits he isn't someone who interprets dreams, but warns not to put too much trust in them.
	- He only speculates.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and the rest of the party (sans [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]) head to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] to discuss the matter of the [[Jingle Bell|jester]].
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] and his lords are looking at a military map, which is quickly concealed.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is growing increasingly wary and it shows. He's angrier than normal.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] informs [[Godric Arden|Godric]] of the [[Jingle Bell|jester]]'s words.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] seems mostly unconcerned, but agrees to have him watched and report on what he speaks.
	- He then goes on a rant about losing the war and craving justice.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s only advice is to temper justice with mercy, as to not be king of the ashes.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] says that's what he's sending them to [[Henry Grey]] for.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and the party is dismissed.
- The party prepares to depart [[Brightguard]].
	- Our plans have changed from taking a boat to [[Oldport]] due to inclement weather.
	- We plan on riding on horseback overland.
- We see a rider approach the gate wearing colors of [[House Grey]].
	- We recognize him as one of the guards escorting [[Robert Lancaster]].
	- He's here for us, and passes a letter from Lord [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]].
	- The rider leaves in haste, with a message from [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] to [[House Grey]] that [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] will remain here in [[Brightguard]].
	- The shares that [[Sir Eamon Douglas|Aemond]] [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] ([[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s future brother in law) was traded to [[House Grey]] in exchange for [[Robert Lancaster]].
	- [[Sir Eamon Douglas|Aemond]]'s sister [[Meredith Douglas|Meredith]] is promised to [[Henry Grey]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] also shares his conversation with [[Father James]] was mostly fruitless.
	- While this feels like a dead end, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] encourages us that we may find answers in [[Oldport]] and elsewhere.
- We mount up and head south.(Reminder, the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] had taken both [[Oldport]] and [[Stag's Rest]]. [[Henry Grey]] has since retaken both cities.)
- We get through [[The Gilded Lands|the Gilded Lands]] (controlled by [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]) with no issues (and some luck).
- As we enter [[The South|the South]], we come upon a patrol from [[House Grey]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] salutes them with the southern salute. They do not return it.
	- It quickly becomes clear they aren't who they claim to be.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notices their attire seems to be looted. He also sees a sigil of [[House Croft]], a house supposed to be loyal to House [[Godric Arden|Godric]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] holds up the missive from [[Godric Arden|King Godric]], but the men ignore it, leveling swords and crossbows at us.
	- There are men hiding in the bushes as well.
