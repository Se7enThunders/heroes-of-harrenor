**Quests in play:** [[02 Bring Henry Grey to Heel|Bring Henry Grey to Heel]]

Previously…

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] summons [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]].

- He sees [[Henry Grey|Henry Gray]] as a traitor and tasks us with joining him and bringing him to heel.
- The [[House Grey|Grays]] currently have captured [[Robert Lancaster]], chief heir to their house.
- The [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] have captured [[Sophia Grey|Sophia Gray]], [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s sister.
	- Either [[Henry Grey|Henry]] renounce his kingship and bend the knee, or at the very least become an ally for the time being.
	- We accept the task and ride south.

## Now

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] gave [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] a sealed letter to be delivered to [[Henry Grey|Henry Gray]] and no one else.

We ride through the lowlands and come across a group of [[House Grey|Gray]] soldiers gathered around a campfire.

We decide to approach them openly. [[Elara]] does a minor performance, and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] holds both horses as they walk toward the FOUR soldiers.

We tell them we're looking to deliver a message from [[Godric Arden]] to [[Henry Grey]].

They are on edge but tell us he's near [[Greentower]], a few days away.

They demand 2 gold for payment, which we give and move on.

We move further on and rest for the night.

----

As we move on, we are moving toward the south, further toward the lowlands.

We approach a homestead and see bodies and an injured man.

[[Elara]] heals him, under his protest, wishing to die with his family.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] picks him up and pins him against the wall of his homestead, trying to talk sense into him.

The bodies are his wife and daughters. He says they raped them and killed them and made him watch.

The [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers did this.

The [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]]'s did nothing, his liege lords.

We stay and help him bury them.

We head out toward the direction the 20 men who did this, and see a town with [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] colors hanging from the walls.

[[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men are being hanged over the wall, being executed for something.

We trot up to the town walls. We trot up to a town guard and ask if the men being hanged are those responsible for the death of [[The farmer|the farmer]]'s family.

He says they are, mentioning this is [[Knight's Stone]] business. We thank him for his time and move on.

(currently in southeastern [[The Heartlands|Heartlands]])

He introduces himself as [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen Lawson]]. A human man who allies with [[Henry Grey|Henry Gray]]. We get along well.

A guard comes to escort us to Lord [[House Grey|Gray]] and does so.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hands him the letter and addresses him as "lord" instead of "grace." It doesn't seem to faze him.

[[Henry Grey|Henry Gray]] refuses to bend the knee and wishes to stay independent.

- He won't write off an alliance, but will not bend the knee.

He offers to have a letter composed for us and sent to [[Godric Arden]] on our behalf. We accept.

We are led out of [[House Grey|Gray]]'s chambers.
