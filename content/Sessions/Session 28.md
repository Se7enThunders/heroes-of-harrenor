## Previously

- The party vanquished the undead [[The Grand Marshal|Grand Marshal]] after discovering he was absorbing defeated minions, only for dark magic to revive him until [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] executed a two-blade decapitation.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] claimed the <mark>Marshal's fading magical blade</mark> while an old order revealed the Marshal was betrayed and trapped by the "[[The Iron Fist|Iron Fist]]"—a northern commander charged with defending the city.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] unsealed the rune-locked door by extinguishing eight braziers and the central pyre, freeing nine agonizingly bound spirits into the afterlife.
- Entering the throne room, the party confronted the massive [[The Iron Fist|Iron Fist]], who brutally snapped a captive maiden's neck and revealed he is [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s half-brother, before attacking the party.

## Now

## Combat

 - [[The Iron Fist]] flies swiftly from his throne and closes the distance to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], attacking him, then spinning off to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
- [[Dharshoon]] rushes to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and lays his hands and a blessing on his blade, making it magical.
- After a few rounds of combat, [[Dharshoon]] dispatches one of the henchmen.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is knocked out by [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]].
- [[The Iron Fist]] attempts to grapple [[Dharshoon]], but [[Dharshoon]] deftly dodges and shoots and lands an arrow at him.
- [[Dharshoon]] shoots an empowered shot that revives [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and damages [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]] and his henchman.
- [[The Iron Fist]], activates his magic ring and becomes taller and stronger yet.
- The room shakes and rocks fall, battering some of the party.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] moves swiftly and dispatches the final henchman.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rushes [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]], his half-brother, and attacks. [[The Iron Fist]] grabs [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] by the head and slams him into the ground, pummeling him, then throwing him aside with incredible strength.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rushes [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]] and buries his blade in the chest of the man. As he's dying, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] embraces him by the head, whispering that he wished being brothers meant more than this, the breaks [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]'s neck, killing him.

## End Combat

 - [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] examines [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]'s green and [[The Black Ring|black ring]], pocketing it to examine further later.
- We loot roughly 100 gp per man from the corpses.
- We hear the din of battle behind us swell. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]], and [[Godfrey the Squire|Baylon's squire]] are being pushed back.
- We notice the woman that [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]] killed at the start looks very much like the other [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaidens]] we've encountered.
- We notice a similar hollow sphere to the one we discovered on the surface near where she rests. It is dun now.
- The light in here is dim, but the braziers burn somewhat brighter now that the battle has concluded.
- We focus on finding a way out before the battle moves in here and we and our allies are killed.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] picks up the sphere and puts it back in the hands of the deceased maiden. Nothing happens.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] then puts the sphere ([[The Kilns|kiln]]) into one of the braziers. After a moment, the [[The Kilns|kiln]] illuminates.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] removes it from the brazier and puts it back in the hands of the maiden hoping to revive her, which it doesn't.
- Finally, he places it on the alter where it appears it should rest. The room shakes and reveals a stairway leading down.
- At the same moment, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]], [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], and (squire) [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] retreat into the room, swarmed by undead.
- We cover their retreat in the stairway. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] warns us of something terrible coming this way.
- As we retreat, a gaunt creature made of [[The shadow-and-flame creature|shadow and flame]] lumbers into the room. It's time to go.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] both attempt to knock the [[The Kilns|kiln]] off the pedestal to close the door, but fail. We run.

(backtracking in time slightly)

- The scene pivots to the wizard [[Hadron Ironwood]] alone walking through [[Telandor]] at the time the city's true nature is revealed, noticing it looks more like a hellscape now and as a [[The red dragon of Telandor|red dragon]] flies overhead. ![[Session 28 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- He quickly deduces the strange magical goings on, also noting there is something wrong with [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]], perhaps a curse or illness.
- He doesn't realize it, but he's making his way through the path of corpses that the party fought on their way deeper into the city.
- As he explores further he hears voices, roughly eight of them. He sneaks closer, seeing a party of 8 people talking to a tired old man.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] recognizes the heraldry of several houses of [[Harrenor]] amongst the party, waiting for them to pass.
- He then makes his way into the church once they leave.
- He then witnesses the party fight the demon (from session 25) in the distance.
- He turns back into the church and find the body of [[Guinevere]], the person he's searching for, and the dun [[The Kilns|kiln]] she was holding. ![[Session 28 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] recovers the [[The Kilns|kiln]], hoping to make use of it in the future.
- He exits the church and sees the party continuing to fight the demon, deciding in that moment to risk it all and join the battle.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] attacks the demon, though it is shrouded in darkness. His attack wasn't noticed.
- The battle ends and he's still able to remain hidden, following them at a distance.
- The party goes inside a different church and makes a bunch of noise rustling through loot. The [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] takes note and flies directly toward the church, attempting to kill those within.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] waits for the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] to lose interest and investigate the church, wondering if the party survived.
- He notices once more, this [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] is acting strangely, almost manic. It does eventually lose interest and leave.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] goes in, quickly discovering the hidden passage into the catacombs.
- He continues to follow the party until he's met by the creature of [[The shadow-and-flame creature|shadow and flame]] and decides to backtrack back to the surface.
- He does so, then sees the party stumble out of an escape hatch. (we're caught back up to where we left off)
- The party has gained some ground from its' pursuers, but does need to move quickly to avoid being pursued.
- As we exit, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] sees [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] first. We're wary of him and he of us, but right now we need to move.
- The [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] still flies overhead, patrolling and looking for us. On top of that, the creature of [[The shadow-and-flame creature|shadow and flame]] materializes behind us, a black blade forming in its hand. It is 100 yards away.
- It appears to have gotten the attention of the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]], which lands and viciously attacks the creature.
- Our window to escape is short. We high tail it through the gates and back into [[The Blight|the Blight]], hard, long, and far.
- The titanic clash behind us rages the entire time, the last we see the beings battle atop a hill by the church before they're out of view.
- We find an outcropping that feels relatively safe, and take a breather.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes a potion of healing touch and returns the party to full health over a short span.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] conjures water for us all to drink.
- We make introductions, quickly seeing [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] as a friendly.
- We talk for a bit and he tells us he serves [[The Almighty|the Almighty]], did not find what he was looking for, and then states he's a [[Sable Wardens|Sable Warden]].
- We immediately draw arms and demand an explanation. [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] claims he's been separated from them for over a year and is coming to find they're not what they claimed to be when he joined.
	- He's apologetic and trying hard to reconcile with the party.
	- He explains how he is a low ranking member and the higher he climbs, the less he likes of what he sees.
	- He goes on to explain he's searching for the key to immortality. He was searching out [[Guinevere]], [[Guinevere|the Lady of Telandor]], whom the party was also searching for, and did not find her before she dies.
- We put him at ease and explain our wariness of the wardens.
- The party settles in for a long rest and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] cooks up a meal. ![[Session 28 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] focuses on the [[The Kilns|kiln]] he rescued, pondering on how it could be used.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] thinks on the loss of the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] and [[Guinevere|the Lady of Telandor]] and the party's failure to summon [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]].
- [[Dharshoon]] feels discouraged and lost, not knowing what to do next. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] likewise.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] comforts [[Dharshoon]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], explaining their survival is a miracle.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] chooses to take comfort in their survival and what [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] has planned for them. He meditates and prays.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] ponders the religious aspects that has led us here and the impact it has on the people around him.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] considers how rescuing his sister can play into the quest they find themselves on.

## Long Rest

 - We awake and prepare for the long journey out of [[The Blight|the Blight]].
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] expresses that he wishes to see the [[Sable Wardens|Wardens]] thwarted and wishes to be a part of it. The party readily agrees, and decide to bring him on as a traveling companion.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] notes we have to leave a different way than we came in. It will be treacherous, but he'll do his best to keep us safe.
- We make it out of [[The Blight|the Blight]] and into [[Ra'Haad]] proper on [[The Blight|the Blight]]'s north edge.
- We see a settlement in the distance with many soldiers gathering toward that settlement. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] worries it’s a sign of a gathering invasion force.
- We move in to investigate, coming up the backside of a hilltop.
- What we see is a massing of well over 100,000 troops. <mark>Invasion</mark>.
