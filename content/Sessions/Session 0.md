[[Joran Jotunsson]] is a member of [Godric Arden](Godric%20Arden)'s personal guard. He's a colossal man and a renowned fighter. ![[Joran Jotunsson art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
Been in service for ~2 yrs
[[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s kids are [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]] (16) and [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] (14). Wife is [[Celia Bruce-Arden|Celia]] (formerly a [[House Bruce|Bruce]]).
Currently stationed on [[The Green Isles|the Green Isles]].

[[Elara]] is an aasimar bard. ![[Elara art 1.jpg|hh-pop|24]]
Aasimar are thought to be imbued with divine blessing, blessed by the gods for reasons unknown.
6' tall. Enjoys the drama of life. Opinionated. Likes being center of attention. Plays as dumber than she is. She glides elegantly like a ballerina. Leans into her heritage as an aasimar. Opportunist.
She prefers to dance. Some singing.
[[Celia Bruce-Arden|Celia]] has taken her on on retainer.

It's currently May.

Currently holding a feast to catch up with his local lords.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] finds them tedious, but necessary.

After a long evening, [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]] can take no more. He has [[Celia Bruce-Arden|Celia]] dismiss them formally.

It is sunset.

The lords and attendees head to their quarters in the castle for the evening.

[[Elara]] heads back to her room. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] changes shifts.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] goes to give [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] a [[Olivia's Lioness Carving|lioness]] woodcarving he's been making in his free time.

[[Sir Willis Braxton|Willis Braxton]] guarding her door appreciates the gesture.

[[Elara]] wanders the hall hunting for gossip.

We each go to sleep. LONG REST.

<mark>We each have an identical dream.</mark>

We see a field that turns dark as if on fire. We see a city on a hill in the distance burning. In the field below it, we see the boney <mark>corpse of a stag</mark> with many wounds. A <mark>dead lion</mark> lies next to it. Between them is a <mark>broken sword and crown made of steel and rusting</mark>, like they've been there a long time. No sign of life anywhere around. Everything is grey, black, and ash. In the distance we see a <mark>dark figure</mark> staring at the <mark>burning city</mark>.

[[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] are both present in the dream and can see each other. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] calls out to the figure, which does not respond. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tries to approach it, but cannot as a presence weighs heavy on his mind. [[Elara]] tries to approach too. She cannot. The two talk to each other, realizing the other is really there, trying to understand what is happening.

The figure raises a hand and produces a flame that feels it cannot be sated. It shoots the fire from its hand and we wake up. It is morning.

All seems well for both [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]].

[[Elara]] seeks out [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] to confirm what they saw.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is shaken, but goes to see replace [[Sir Willis Braxton|Sir Braxton]].

[[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] is studying with a cleric. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks the priest about the figure from the dream. He doesn't know anything about it. [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] is quietly concerned about this line of inquiry.

[[Elara]] finds [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] with [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]]. They confirm they were both in the dream/vision. They don't know what it is or how to find out more.

The priest double checks with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] dismisses him, lying about why he's interested in the topic.

[[Elara]] decides to stick with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] informs her that he must inform [[Godric Arden|Godric]] of what has transpired. She agrees.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gives over [[Olivia Arden|Oliva]] to another guard and heads to [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s study.

The guards grudgingly let him in, as [[Godric Arden|Godric]] has asked not to be disturbed.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tells all. [[Godric Arden|Godric]] seems annoyed.

The stag in the dream is in reference to house [[House Grey|Grey]]. That is all else we're able to tell from the dream. [[Godric Arden|Godric]] dismisses [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]].

Riders approach the castle. 10 of them bearing the sigil of a white wolf on a blue field. Neither [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] or [[Elara]] know the house.

The head rider says he has a message for [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s ears only. [[Sir Willis Braxton|Sir Braxton]] goes to retrieve him.

The wolf lord has a son with him about 16.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] recognizes the man as [[Lord William Marshall|Lord Marshall]]. He gives him a [[Godric Arden|Godric]] a letter, sealed by the royal court.

The knights yell out. "<mark>Hail his grace [[Godric Arden|King Godric I]]</mark>!" [[Godric Arden|Godric]] summons his war council, asking [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]] to come as well. (this indicates his older brother [[Godfrey Arden|Godfrey]] is no longer king of the realm.)

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] begins the meeting by announces his brother's death. [[Edward Grey|Lord Edward Grey]] has been captured by [[Catherine Lancaster]]. [[William Arden|William]]'s bastard parentage has come up again. His father supposedly being [[Sir Avery Mortimer]].

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] declares [[William Arden|William]] officially as a bastard, endorsing his own claim to the throne. He has the scribes copy down messages to send throughout the realm.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]] and 2 others are meant to infiltrate the [[Brightguard]] castle in [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]], currently occupied by the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].

[[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]] are the names of the 2 other individuals joining us in our raid on the seat of [[Brightguard]]. They both grew up there and knows secret ways inside.

The plan is to have [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s main force and the [[House Bruce|Bruces]] from [[The Northeast|the Northeast]] march down through [[The Swordlands|the Swordlands]] to assault the walls of Birghtguard to give us cover to slip in and take the castle.

END PART 1

START PART 2

We set sail with [[Godric Arden|Godric]] and his army. He approaches us ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]]) and asks us about the dream again.

- Neither of us have had any more insight.

He starts telling us of when his castle was under siege in the last war.

- Things were hard.

[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says he's been thinking and believes that law and order will lead to less death and he is less upset about picking a side in this war.

- Though he has no intention of killing more than he has to.
- [[Elara]] agrees.

[[Lord Adam Croft]] will be one leading the royal navy against the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] and [[William Arden]].

We continue sailing until we make landfall at night near a cave on the beach near [[Brightguard]] castle.

- [[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]] are with us.
	- The two are loyal fighters and sworn to house [[House Arden|Arden]]
	- [[Thomas]] carries a longsword.
	- [[Gerrit]] carries a battleaxe and a crossbow.
	- They are veterans of the last war.

The cave is dark and [[Thomas]] leads us by torch light.

- We make it through the caves and come to the lower levels of the castle interior.
- As we rise in levels we come to ground and are near a courtyard where we hear some commotion.
- Soldiers are getting ready for a siege.
- Several sitting by a fire near the right side of the courtyard.
- It is completely dark aside from these torches.
- Roughly 20 men in the courtyard. 2 pairs patrolling the area. More men up on the ramparts.
- 3 sitting at the fire.
- 5 guards at the gate.
- 6 huddled at a guardpost in a corner eating and killing time.

We discuss having [[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]] to start a fire on the opposite side of the castle grounds as a distraction to peel men away from the courtyard.

- [[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]] will go create a fire as a diversion, then come back and climb the ramparts, dealing with the men up there.
- [[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] will deal with who's left in the courtyard and open the gate.
- Everything has to happen fast or an alarm will be sounded and we'll be captured or killed.

We enact the plan. 3 guards remain on the ramparts. 4 at the gate.

- The guards at the gate are [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men, not [[William Arden]]'s men.
- [[Thomas]] and [[Gerrit]] sneak up the ramparts.
- [[Elara]] steps into the light and begins a performance as a distraction.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] attempts to sneak around.
- [[Elara]] waits for 2 guards to get close and she casts Thunderwave.
- We make short work of the guards on the ground and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] runs toward the gate to open it.
- With all guards knocked out or dead, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]] manage to open the gate.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s army having crept up to the castle under cover of darkness, sweep into the castle with his men chanting his name.

- They make short work of the remaining guards, capturing many of them.

We notice hanging in the courtyard the body of a man who's been hanging there decomposing for about a month.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] walks up and asks who's in charge, finds a guard who disrespects him, but tells him it was a thief who stole from Lord [[William Arden|William]].

- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] says this was not the correct punishment for the crime.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s men take down the banners, burn them, and replace them with [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s banners.

The castle is taken in full over the next few days, [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s family arriving the next day.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] has reclaimed his ancestral seat.

His men continue to make repairs to the castle and bolster its defenses and food stores.

15,000 [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s men occupy the castle surrounding area and nearby town.

- Yet this is only a fraction of the men at his command.

After a few days a rider approaches, bearing a message that [[Edward Grey|Edward Gray]] has been executed by [[William Arden|William]] for the crime of treason.

- [[Henry Grey|Henry Gray]] has declared himself King of an independent state and is marching to war with [[William Arden]] and [[House Lancaster]].
- [[Titus Lancaster]] having made alliances grows his forces as well.

[[Godric Arden|Godric]] summons [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]].

- He sees [[Henry Grey|Henry Gray]] as a traitor and tasks us with joining him and bringing him to heel.
- The [[House Grey|Grays]] currently have captured [[Robert Lancaster]], chief heir to their house.
- The [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] have captured [[Sophia Grey|Sophia Gray]], [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s sister.
	- Either [[Henry Grey|Henry]] renounce his kingship and bend the knee, or at the very least become an ally for the time being.
	- We accept the task and ride south.
