**Quests in play:** [[11 Free Beatrice and Therond|Free Beatrice and Therond]]

## Previously

- The party infiltrated a [[The black tower|black tower]] where a [[The mortician|mortician]] was bloodletting elves to create a life-extending serum for his leader; the elves were freed, and [[The mortician|the mortician]] killed by an escaped captive.
- Upon ascending the tower, the party experienced personal, disturbing visions (including [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] seeing a dead [[Godwin Arden|Godwin]] and an abducted [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]] by [[Ra'Haadi forces]]), and realized three weeks had inexplicably passed.
- They discovered a hidden library of magical scrolls that reveal hidden messages (now readable by [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]) and encountered [[The man in black|the man in black]] who had abducted [[Elia]].
- In combat, the party killed [[The man in black|the man in black]] ([[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] delivering the final blow), who revealed with his dying breath that [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s freed-slave city had been discovered. [[Elia]] was found unharmed.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], upon touching a black crystal orb, received a terrifying vision of [[Grimhold]] under siege, stars falling, and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] killing [[Henry Grey]] at [[Titus Lancaster]]'s command.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gained the ability to read the hidden labels on the scrolls, finding "correspondences" and an image of the sword [[Starbinder]].
- Upon returning to [[Na'Buchar]] with [[Elia]] and the scrolls, they found [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]]'s estate empty.

## Now

- It's about 9 AM.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] and the few people who remain are finishing packing everything up to leave. A few armed guards with him, minimal staff.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] thanks us for rescuing [[Elia]] and asks us how things went. [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] explains all.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks why [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] is leaving. He explains his freed-slave settlement has been exposed. He plans on heading there and continuing his exodus will the freed slaves heading further north.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests we accompany him for protection. The party readily agrees.
- We talk further of experience in [[The black tower|the black tower]]. [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] produces the [[The Palantir|palantir]] and shows it to [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]].
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] is amazed to see one and urges us to keep it hidden.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks for clarification on the [[Sable Wardens]]. [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] thinks they are very old and very dangerous. He has little other info.
- We begin to leave after finishing helping [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] load his final belongings.
- We walk past the slave markets, noting many [[Harrenor|Harrenoran]] women among the slaves at market.
- [[Dharshoon]] overhears the slavers talking amongst themselves about this influx of quality slaves from [[Oldport]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is worried is sister might be among them. After checking, he notices her among the captured.
- [[Dharshoon]] listens in and notices these slaves have already been sold. [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] attempts to barter for some, but is told it is against the rules.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests following [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s sister's new owner until they have an opportunity to forcibly free her in an opportune spot.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] insists on moving onward, passing us a map to his destination.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] catches a quick glimpse of an abyssal flame medallion on the man who bought the slaves.
- We hastily attempt to throw together a plan.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Dharshoon]] approach one of the guards to inquire where the slave women are headed.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] bribes the guard with 10 gp to tell him they're headed to a slummy part of the city.
- We relay the information to the group and try to get there before the slavers and slaves to scout it out.
	- We beat them there. It's a ruined Harranorian church that used to be a stable. It's completely abandoned now. ![[Session 10 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- We suspect we've been duped.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] scurries to the rooftop to scout the area.
	- He sees gleaming helmets coming our way.
- We all hide for an ambush.
- We see the man who bought the slaves, his two guards, the slaves, and the rest of the knights arrive.
- Most of the them enter the church, two guards left outside.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] scuttles to the top of the spire of the church and jumps down onto one of the guards, assassinating him.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] sneak up and assassinate the other guard.
- The three of them hide the bodies. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Dharshoon]], keeping an eye out, thinks we managed to kill them without detection.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] finds a bag on 10 gp to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. It was the guard he talked to in the city.
- We opt to head in to investigate, stealthily. No one is inside in the immediate area.
- As we investigate the area, [[Dharshoon]] finds a loose tile on the ground. Once depressed, it opens a stairway behind the sliding alter.
- We enter the stairway down.
- We see a door at the bottom of the stair. Opening it we see 4 [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] in a room. We attack immediately.

COMBAT!

- The [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] are poorly armored and we make quick work of the ones we get the drop on.
- They are adept in the use of magic and manage to paralyze [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] temporarily.
- He breaks free of the spell in short order and takes a short bow shot at the remaining [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] rushes the last [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] and offers a quick death for information.
	- The [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] mocks him.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] cuts of his hand.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] cuts off the other hand.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] cuts his Achillies tendon.
	- The man is in shock and no longer useful.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] throws him to the ground and stabs him through the heart.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] suggest more effective tactics to glean information next time. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] agree.

## End Combat

- There's a chest in the room with 30 gp and a scroll of cure wounds, and a map of this level.
- We divvy up the spoils and give the scroll to [[Dharshoon]].
- There's two doors in the room. We hear screams of a woman behind the one of them.
- The door is trapped. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] disarms the trap.
- As we enter, we see gaunt undead figures (howlers) that are making the false noise of the distressed women. 6 of them.

## Combat

- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] charges in and attacks the first one.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] runs into the room, torch lit, goading the monsters.
- 2 of them attack [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and land hits.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] finish of the ones by [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], while the rest of the group clears the rest.

## End Combat

- [[Dharshoon]] sees a cracked flagstone in this room. Underneath it is a +1 dagger that looks to have been placed here by the original creators of the church.
- He hands it off to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] hears more wailing from where we came.
- There 2 doors in this room. We opt to look into one that leads to an old library.
- Inside we find and old tome written in [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]], which [[Dharshoon]] translates the title as <mark>"On the Occult and Their Practices."</mark>
	- It's well used with worn pages. In the back it details a belief of the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] about a man that descended to the right hand of "the enemy" and became a powerful demon, achieved through immense human sacrifice.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] sees on a table, an open scroll with pen and ink nearby, freshly written on.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] has [[Dharshoon]] translate it.
	- What stands out is <mark>"we're attempting to make him break his vows, but nothing seems to work. We will continue in our attempts."</mark>
- Two more doors in this room. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] checks the one to the south. It is trapped. He disarms it easily.
	- It's a mostly empty store room. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] finds minor healing potions in the crates. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] finds rations.
- We head back to the previous room. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] attempts disarms the traps on that door as well, failing this time managing to dodge a bolt that flies out from the wall.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] both are unable to disarm the door, so [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] opts to open it, dodging 3 bolts.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] narrowly dodges a blade that falls from the ceiling.
	- It appears it was a false room meant to dispatch anyone who entered.
- We backtrack further and open another door, which is not trapped.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] opens the door and sees two Elite Cataphracts inside the room who jump to attention and attack us.

## Combat

- We engage quickly. [[Dharshoon]] launching arrows and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] moving in for quick attacks with daggers and rapier.
- These enemies are much hardier, but [[Dharshoon]] manages to fell the first with an arrow.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] wants us to spare the 2nd.
- We knock out the 2nd elite soldier.

## End Combat

- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] takes the reigns interrogating the man, taking a finger but not gaining anything.
- He curses at us in [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]]. [[Dharshoon]] translates.
- [[Dharshoon]] asks if he likes bees?
- [[Dharshoon]] prompts his bees to swarm the man.
- The man finally breaks. He tells us to hit the back door. That's where the slave women were taken.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] kills him quickly.
- We move through the door in the room where the man said to go.
- There's a hallway, a set of stairs, and a small alcove with a table where small [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]] sits.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] grabs the [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]]. It's made of eternium, with two ruby eyes.
	- The [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]] provides [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] with a sense of instant comfort.
- We opt to head down the stairs.
- We find ourselves in a dark dining hall, empty of any life within. Two doors are within.
- The first door we approach is locked. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] attempts to pick the lock.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] hear howlers potentially on the other side of the door.
- We check the other door. Light shines from beneath. We hear human voices within.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] quickly opens the door, noticing the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] inside are engaged in a ritual and do not notice us.

----------- BONUS SESSION -----------

- They don't seem to notice us, so we surround them, assassinating them, but being assaulted by a wave of psychic energy.
	- They were gathered around a demonic flame that is now extinguished.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] captures 1 of the 4 [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] attempts to intimidate him into telling us where the slaves are. It is ineffective.
	- [[Dharshoon]] suggests waterboarding him.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] stabs the man through his foot.
	- The [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] doesn't speak but looks towards a door, giving away what he's trying to hide.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] pops the man in the nuts with his quarterstaff.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] decapitates the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]], spitting on the corpse.
- We open next door, hearing more howlers within.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], getting enraged by the evil done here, charges in, yelling at the creatures, weapon raised, the rest of the group right behind him.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] gets in a good shot and eviscerates the howler he attacked.
	- [[Dharshoon]] casts a web spell, restraining a small group of howlers.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] destroys a howler with his martial prowess.
	- A few howlers scream, doing more psychic damage.
	- We end up killing the rest of the howlers, but we are worse for wear.
- We enter a southerly door, entering a dungeon, with cells lining the walls on either side.
- Among the slaves is [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s sister [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]].
	- She is terrified and shaken, clinging to [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] for her life.
- We free her and the others, noting one of the slave women we tracked here is missing.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices down a hallway he's guarding, there is an empty room with a corpse holding a vial.
- We hear shouts coming from the direction we came from.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thinks quickly and runs into the other room to grab the vial, finding it to be a vial of [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'haadi]] fire.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] run into the room with chanting, having the 7 women follow.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Dharshoon]], guard the rear.
- In the room the chanting is coming from, we see 6 [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] finishing a ritual, using the 8th slave woman to summon a demon from dark flames, which rises and moves toward us with a fiery mace.
- The demon taunts us saying, "we've been waiting for you."
- [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]], now holding [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s amulet, runs past the demon toward the passageway in the other side of the room.
- She yells at [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], "Something tells me I need to go in here!"
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] bangs his fist together, his tattoos glowing and growing in size (large), he picks up as many of the women as he can carry and races to the door with them with enhances speed, narrowly avoiding the demon and [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] on his way through the passage. The rest of the party following suite.
- We enter the room which is pitch dark, slamming the door behind us.
- Those of who can see in the dark, see a massive ancient [[Therond|dragon]] chained up in the room. ![[Session 10 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
- It turns to greet us and calls us out as wretches, and another quaint offering, seeming unamused.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] takes point in the conversation, asking for safe passage.
- The [[Therond|dragon]] speaks of its vows and how the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] constantly attempt to get him to break them.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] shows that we're here with slave women, including his sister that we're rescuing from the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]].
- The [[Therond|dragon]] asks which woman is [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s sister. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] truthfully tells her.
- The [[Therond|dragon]] asks [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] if this is true. She acknowledges it.
- The [[Therond|dragon]]'s fire that was beginning to gather in its throat dissipates as he calms.
- The [[Therond|dragon]] [[Therond]] he is called, as he tells us of his vows of penance. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] recognizes who he is.
- He is a prisoner here.
- He asks us how we've gotten here, and we are truthful with him. Rescuing slaves from [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]].
- The [[Therond|dragon]] curses the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]].
- The [[Therond]] asks us to free him.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] attempts to loose the chains, but even in his larger form is unable to do so.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] grabs the [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]] from [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] and inserts it into the locking mechanism on the chest of the [[Therond|dragon]], freeing it.
- The [[Therond|dragon]] looses flame in exultation, spewing it all over the room, breaking down the door to the room we came from, moving over the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and the demon, and now gathered soldiers, destroying all but the demon with ease.
- As the demon is all that remains, the [[Therond|dragon]]'s flame changes from flame to purple arcane energy, narrowing on the demon and disintegrating it.
- [[Therond]] continues to move through the rest of the dungeon destroying it an anyone in his path.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] grabs the [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]] off the ground as we watch on in awe.
- After the [[Therond|dragon]]'s rampage is finished, he nuzzles up to [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]], her hand on his nose, promising his aid if she needs him in the future.
- He says he must leave now that he has brought low this place. He thanks us, and we him.
- Finally, he blows out the side of a wall, opening it to open air, as he flies off into the sky away from the city, the dead slave girl used to summon the demon in tow.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s size shrinks back to normal.
- We take a moment to reevaluate the scope of what we're engaged in now.
- We notice as the [[Therond|dragon]] flies away that during the fight with the demon, [[Therond]] took a black mark on his chest. We worry about the implications.
- We manage to salvage several hundred gold, some health potions, and some other unique treasure.
- We are able to find a cheap draft horse to transport all of us and the women, two to a horse to [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s freed-slave city.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks about the women's journey from capture to here.
	- The were captured in [[Oldport]] a month ago.
	- The city was easily sacked by the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] with much of its defense dedicated to the war in [[Harrenor]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks after his father ([[Matthew Silver|Matthew]]). He was fighting at the siege, but his whereabouts are unknown.
- We ride a week straight.
- We arrive at the settlement, which we realize is a small town, walled off, set up near a natural oasis, a belltower in the center of the town.
- We also notice a variety of mercenaries guarding the town. Elves, [[Ra'Haadi forces|cataphracts]], northmen, and occasional [[Harrenor|Harrenorian]].

[[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] specifically notices the elves are from [[The colonies|the colonies]] in the gulf between [[Harrenor]] and [[Ra'Haad]].
