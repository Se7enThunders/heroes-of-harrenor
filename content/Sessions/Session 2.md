# Session 2

## Previously

- In the lowlands toward the boarder with the Southlands.

- [[Joran Jotunsson]], [[Elara]], Starwishiper, [[Theodore Silver]], [[Draven Throssenheiser]] (half-elf), [[Dharshoon]] are the party now.

A man in a red mask with a fire emblem is present.

- [[Baylor Stelaris]] is wearing a red mask and the fire robes of [[Order of Holy Fire|Order of the Holy Fire]]
- His twin brother [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]] is there.
	- Has a greatsword called [[Starbinder]].
	- [[House Stelaris|Stelaris]] is an old and storied house of [[The South|the South]].
	- Sigil is upward facing sword with a sword on tip of the blade. Purple field.

We're tasked with headed back to the same town [[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] passed to convince the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] to leave.

- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes they passed this town and explains what happened.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] is notably shaken hearing the account of the rape and murder of the family.

Our task is discussing with [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] how to rid the [[Stoneheath]] of [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men.

- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baylor]] and [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] will be backing us up, leading the main bulk of the forces while we go ahead.

NOW (early morning, just before sunrise)

- On our way to [[Stoneheath]].
- An elderly [[The old trader|trader]] who does not give us his name tries to sell us health potions. ![[Session 2 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- The health potions are too much.
	- We buy magic tokens instead. 2 per man.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] is asking for strong booze.
	- Also too much.
- We pay him to tell us about [[Stoneheath]].
	- He tells us the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] have it well in hand.
		- ==With the exception of a small door on the north side that is poorly guarded==.
- We pass the homestead of [[The farmer|the farmer]] whose family was killed by [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men. ![[Session 2 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- It seems [[The farmer|the farmer]] is not here.
	- We dismount to investigate.
		- [[Dharshoon]] stays with the horses.
	- We find a man squatting in the house.
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] questions the man.
			- He's seen no one and arrived last night.
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] makes a deal with him to watch over the house and keep it up until [[The farmer|the farmer]] returns.
			- The man agrees.
		- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] notices the man carries a uniquely military dagger and asks [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] to hold the man.
			- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] does so.
		- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] grills him.
			- He says he's not a [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]]. He asks why the king would send us after him.
			- We ask his name. It's [[Mark]]. He was conscripted by the [[House Cromwell|Cromwells]].
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] releases the man. The deal remains the same.
- [[Dharshoon]] sees some torchlight coming up the road. ![[Session 2 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- We all get in position, waiting for them to approach.
	- They approach and are [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men.
	- The spot [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Elara]] in the doorway of the house. ![[Session 2 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
		- They grill them and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is honest about their purpose.
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks about the men who killed [[The farmer|the farmer]]'s family.
			- The soldiers say the men were hanged and buried already.
		- They notice [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s colors and attack.

COMBAT!

- We make quick work of the men, killing 2 of the 6.
	- [[Dharshoon]] saves one with healing magic.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] interrogates the one we healed. He's a tough nut to crack.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] knocks him out.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] doesn't know what to do, and the group thinks we have to kill them all.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] walks away as the others dispatch the survivors. He hates killing, but sees no other way here. He won't be a part of it. ![[Session 2 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- One of the survivors before dispatched outs [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] as the son of [[Jean Sanderfell]], bannerman of [[Titus Lancaster]].
		- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] explains he left that life.
- The last of the survivors are killed.
- We bury the bodies in [[The farmer|the farmer]]'s field to disguise the graves.
	- [[Elara]] goes into the house and confronts the deserter, seeing if he'll talk. ![[Session 2 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
		- She feels confident he won't.
