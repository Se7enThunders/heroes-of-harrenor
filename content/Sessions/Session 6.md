## Previously

- Eastward Journey & Mercenary Deal: [[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] part from allies, stopping at a border inn where [[Elara]] recruits [[Jamie]] and nine sellswords for [[Godric Arden|King Godric]]—negotiating a bold deal without royal approval.
- **Arrival at Camp & War Council**: They reach [[Godric Arden|Godric]]’s 65,000-strong army, where [[Elara]]’s mercenary hire causes tension but is accepted. Assigned under [[Sir John Marshall]], they prepare for the [[10 The Siege of Kingsmont|siege of Kingsmont]].
- **Siege & Rout**: During the battle, [[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] fight with precision, but a devastating [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] cavalry charge shatters [[House Arden|Arden]] lines. [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is forced to retreat, losing 10,000 men.
- **New Mission**: In the aftermath, [[Godric Arden|Godric]] secretly tasks [[Elara]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] with waging war on the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] in [[House Grey|Grey]] territory. [[Jamie]]'s mercenaries survive and remain open to continued service.
- **Return & Reward**: Back in [[Brightguard]], [[Godric Arden|Godric]] grants [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] a magic greatsword. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks after plate armor, shares a quiet farewell with [[Olivia Arden|Olivia]], and prepares for the road ahead.
(Meanwhile in the other half of the party.)

- **Recon in [[White Harvest]]**: Sent to scout [[The Heartlands|the Heartlands]], the party visits [[White Harvest]], buying more [[Strange Coins|strange coins]] and noting the presence of [[House Corbet|Corbet]], [[House Sanderfell|Sanderfell]], and [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers.
- **Scouting the Bars**: [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] gather intel in local taverns while [[Dharshoon]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] uncover a [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’haadi]] man consorting with a presumed [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] spy.
- **[[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] March Revealed**: [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] confirm the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] plan to break the siege at [[Kingsmont]], but their sober exit raises suspicions.
- **Intercepting the Spy**: The party tracks the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’haadi]] man; after a tense chase, they injure his mount and capture him alive despite his suicide attempt.
- **Mission into [[Ra'Haad|Ra’haad]]**: Delivering grim news to [[Henry Grey|King Henry Grey]], the party is thanked and tasked with stopping the raids—reuniting with the others after [[Godric Arden|Godric]]’s failed siege.

## Now

- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is tasked with aiding the rest of the party in the mission to [[Ra'Haad|Ra'haad]].
- [[Elara]] decides to come, but will meet us after she's checked up on family. Will meet us after we've entered [[Ra'Haad|Ra'haad]] through [[Grimhold]].
- We set out from [[Brightguard]] across [[The South|the South]]. Approximate ==travel time is a month.==
- We pass through [[Stag's Rest]], the capital seat of the province of [[The South|the South]], ancestral home of the [[House Grey|Greys]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] does a lot of cooking on the road, and takes after [[Dharshoon]]'s bees.
- The weather is turning as mid-November chill sets in. Reports of the first snows up north.
- As we approach the border with [[Ra'Haad|Ra'haad]], we find ourselves entering a long valley through the mountains.
	- [[Grimhold]] lies on the at the entrance to this valley. ![[Session 6 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- It is both a fortress and a seat of power, populated by lords and ladies.
	- [[House Grimston]] rules here. Banner is a Grizzley Bear on its hind legs on a silver field.
	- They are bannermen of Hosue [[House Grey|Grey]]
	- The people here are hardy and tall, looking much like [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]. Appearing to have migrated here, maybe from the far north?
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] has a letter to deliver to the Lord of [[Grimhold]].
- As we pull up, the guards halt us. ![[Session 6 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] calls out in Varanoran language and crossbows are trained on him. (they speak the language or at least recognize it.) ![[Session 6 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] steps in and explains things. We're allowed to pass.
- We are greeted by [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] "[[Torren Grimston|Red Claw]]" [[House Grimston|Grimston]], an old warrior with a left eye replaced with an orb of obsidian.
- He says [[Lord Raymond Grimston|Raymond]] (his brother) is the Lord here, when [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] makes the mistake of referring to him as such.
- He says we're welcome here, while they assess the intelligence we've brought and what they're already going through.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is to be watched, as his people (Varanorans) are not trusted here.
- We take in the scenery here and assess the viability of the fortress before dinner. It is well built and fortified.
- We're taken to one of the towers for dinner.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] is there, welcoming us in.
- Another man is there, looking [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'haadi]], but is quite friendly. He is introduced as [[Ahmed Al-Zahim]].
- The discussion beings and [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] asks about what news there is of the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'haadi]] alliance with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].
- Only 1000 men guard the fortress currently. It is vulnerable to [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'haadi]] attack if they were to assault the fortress.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] asks the possibility of [[House Arden|Arden]] forces reinforcing them. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] feels it's possible, but isn't in a position to say for sure.
- It is noted that elves are traded as slaves in [[Ra'Haad]]. All eyes are on [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is quizzed about slave trading between [[Varanor]] and [[Ra'Haad]]. He admits he took part it in many years ago.
- It comes out in the discussion that [[Dharshoon]] speaks [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]].
- We need to head south the discover more about the conspiracy to ally [[Ra'Haadi forces]] with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests we pose as slave traders, with he and [[Dharshoon]] being in charge of
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] will be accompanying us, taking us to his master, [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] will pose as a [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] liaison.
- That leaves [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] as our 2nd slave.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] warns us of what lies ahead. He says we need to go through [[The Blight|the Blight]] to avoid unwanted attention.
- We will pass the city of [[Ashfall]] in [[The Blight|the Blight]], on our way to [[Na'Buchar]] a trading hub, where Ebon'Sayid waits for us.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] warns us to keep our guard up in [[The Blight|the Blight]]. Dark things live there that are friendly to none.
- We turn in our attire for disguises befitting our cover.
- We all have unsettling dreams.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] sees a demon grab him by the neck, the same they fought when he killed his cousin.
	- The demon stabs him and he wakes up in a cold sweat.
- We awake the next day.
- A woman brings a wax sealed letter for [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]. A message from [[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Barring]]. She gives him to it privately.
	- He reads it and it's a request for a favor. [[Lord Thomas Baring|Barring]] requests that when visiting [[Ra'Haad]], there's a contact [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] needs to seek out and deliver information too.
- We saddle up on horses and set out.
	- [[Dharshoon]] and Akmed take lead, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] are "tied up" as slaves, and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] take up the rear posing as a slaver and a [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] man.
- We travel for a week before coming to a settlement.
	- We notice the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] there practicing their religion of [[Sahirism]]. Many of the people there are slaves and servants to these men, or more so caste system.
		- Alnur is the god of [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]], the founder of [[Ra'Haad]].
		- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] is the first prophet.
		- His descendants are the Kalifs, rulers and successor prophets after him.
		- (a mirror of Islam more or less)
	- Some [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] men are demanding Jizya (tax) from some of the "lesser" people.
- We continue to travel and come across a crucified man. "death to those who refuse to pay the Jizya" the sign reads. He's been dead for a week.
- More and more crucifixes as we travel. All men. No women and children.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] speaks aloud how these men are better off to have died than what fate holds for the women and children who have been made slaves.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] explains how his master was once a [[Sahirism|Sahirist]] and owned [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]]. He renounced the religion and now rescues slaves and sends them to safety.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is looking forward to meeting Sabir, as he feels they have much in common.
- We make it to the edge of the blight and see the river there acting as the natural border. ![[Session 6 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
	- The river is tainted with a deep green color. ![[Session 6 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] explains it's been cursed since [[02 The Calamity|the Calamity]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks about [[02 The Calamity|the Calamity]]. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] has little knowledge of it.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] tells us to dismount and guide our horses by hand, as animals act strange and skittish within [[The Blight|the Blight]].
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] teaches [[Dharshoon]] some phrases in [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] to speak to his horse in to keep it calm.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] tells us to drop the slave disguises, as there is no one within [[The Blight|the Blight]] they need to worry about.
- We travel into [[The Blight|the Blight]] and begin to hear shrill cries of what [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] says is a Wyvern.
- [[Dharshoon]] sees something crawling across the ground in the distance, not toward us.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] warns us not to go near it and keep an ear out for vibrations in the sand.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks about water here. Akmed tells us of wells scattered throughout the region that are still good. Recommends we camp there.
- We find one and settle in for the night, taking watches in shifts.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] takes first watch and see human silhouettes in the distance.
	- He raises [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], but the figures are no longer there.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes second watch, which is uneventful. ![[Session 6 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] takes 3rd watch, which is also uneventful.
- We wake and continue to travel. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] is worried we're being followed.
- Night falls once more and we take watches. [[Dharshoon]], [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] take watches in that order.
- [[Dharshoon]] sees a figure in the distance. He rouses [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], but the figure is gone again.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] takes 2nd watch and rouses all of us quietly, making the a signal that there are six figures out there and we are in danger.
- We prepare to fight staying quiet.
- We hear guttural clicking noises.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] has [[Dharshoon]] look over the dune they're on to see what's there.
- They see 6 gaunt figures, 3 on either side, humanoid, seemingly wights of some kind. 100 yards away. ![[Session 6 art 7.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We all get in position. [[Dharshoon]] fires at them, baiting them in.

COMBAT!

- When in range, the rest of us fire our ranged weapons at them.
- Our weapons that aren't magical or seem to be less effective.
- The enemies start to close in.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] yells to stay close so none of us are surrounded and outnumbered.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes a massive hit, but puts the wight on its back with a riposte. ![[Session 6 art 8.jpg|hh-pop]]
- The wight's hands glow as they take swipes at us with their hands between weapon strikes.
- The wights are the toughest opponents we've fought yet, taking damage we inflict in stride.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] downs the first wight.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] downs the second.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] kills the 3rd.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] kills the 4th.
- [[Dharshoon]] kills the 5th (after having little luck with hits, and finally getting a nat 20).
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] kills the last one with his quarterstaff.

END COMBAT.

- We collect ourselves and take a long rest, recovering from the hard fought battle, ready to continue our journey.
