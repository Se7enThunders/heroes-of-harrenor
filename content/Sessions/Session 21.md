## Previously

- [[Henry Grey]] formally accepts [[Godric Arden]]’s alliance; [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is promised official lordship of [[Sunwatch]] after the war and recognition in all but name immediately.
- [[Dharshoon]] observes a covert exchange ([[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] colors passing a parchment to a blond courier), leading the party to the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]] Tavern and a suspicious noblewoman contact.
- The blond man bears a silver [[The Mockingbird|mockingbird]] pin matching the Bandit Lord at [[Sunwatch]]; he claims to be only a courier and implicates the woman instead.
- Tracking leads to an ambush in a sealed plaza by a highly skilled [[The bounty hunter|bounty hunter]] and five men—linked to the [[Sable Wardens]]. The party defeats them; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is nearly overwhelmed before [[Dharshoon]] kills the leader.
- A surviving [[The Janissary|Janissary]] confirms [[Sable Wardens|Sable Warden]] involvement; instead of executing him, the party decides to turn him over to the [[House Grey|Greys]]. Each member claims 100 gp from the fallen.

## Now

- Later in the day, [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] meets with the [[House Grey|Greys]] to discuss details of inheriting [[Sunwatch]].
- Upon turning over [[The Janissary|the Janissary]] to the [[House Grey|Grey]]'s they decide to imprison instead of execute.
- As the day stretches on, snow continues to fall heavily. Odd, for being this far south.
	- One would be forgiven thinking it is unnatural.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] takes some free time to purchase a shield, seeing as danger finds us even here in [[Oldport]].
- As evening comes, we head back to the castle, and the snow finally starts to settle. We catch up with [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] messages [[Brutus]] with his [[The Sending Stone|sending stone]].
- As other lords are exiting the hall, we notice that many of them seem very tense.
- We note [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] conferring with [[Lord Otto Cromwell|Lord Cromwell]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] notes that many of the lords are worried that [[Henry Grey]] made the correct call or not in bending the knee.
- We decide to head back to the [[Oldport|Hopping Horse]] to discuss our next course of action.
- On our way there, we note that businesses aren't thriving, but inns and taverns are.
- Once we arrive, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thanks the party for their assistance in completing his task of bringing the [[House Grey|Greys]] under [[Godric Arden|Godric]]'s kingship.
	- While he will need to return to service in the coming months, he is ready and willing to assist them in whatever tasks they have.
	- He orders food and drinks for the table as the party digs into their planning.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] brings up that [[Sunwatch]] needs to remain at least in appearance in its rundown state.
	- [[Henry Grey]] is worried elevating [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] now could jeopardize the new alliance with the [[House Arden|Ardens]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is expected to return to [[Oldport]] by spring to fulfill his duties here.
- While the [[Sable Wardens]] are a marked threat, we think we should prioritize heading north to investigate the nature of [[Dharshoon]]'s dream.
	- Though [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes, while we're here, investigating the [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] connection to the [[Sable Wardens]] is worth considering.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] suggests letting the [[House Grey|Greys]] handle the [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] issue. The party agrees.
- We decide to have [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] communicate with [[Brutus]] to determine if he knows if the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] can somehow teleport us to [[Varanor]].
	- If he does, we got back to [[Sunwatch]] and use the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]]. If he does not, we sail north.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] messages [[Brutus]] and [[Brutus]] does not know anything other than his master would enter and disappear for hours often.
- Realizing that the [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite trees]] tend to grow in the west of [[Varanor]], we decide it may be more valuable to head overland instead of sailing north along the eastern side of the continent. Walking across all of [[Varanor]] doesn't seem like a realistic option.
	- And since we're heading that direction, we should stop by [[Sunwatch]] first to see if we can figure out the teleportation [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] dismisses himself for the night to pray. He heads to the [[Oldport|Grand Cathedral]] of [[Oldport]].
	- The church is beautiful with vaulted ceilings and stained glass depicting scenes [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is unfamiliar with.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks for direction from the priest who greets him ([[Brother Michael]]) and instructs in the basics of prayer.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] prays for guidance and protection as the party prepares to head north into [[Varanor]].
	- He also asks for whatever sway the old gods have over him to be severed.
	- He notices in one of the stained glass windows a man by the name of St [[St. Maximian|Maximian]], famous for 1000 years ago establishing the first church there, felling an everwhite to construct it.
		- The everwhite was a symbol of the evil gods and magic of the land.
	- With that [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] leaves.
- The party all finds a shop that sells winter traveling clothes and purchases a set each.
- We settle in for the night.

## Long Rest

- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] has a private conversation with [[Dharshoon]] in the morning.
- We saddle up and head west to [[Sunwatch]]. A week later, we arrive. No snow during this time, but it remains cold.
- The refugees are doing well, with one noteworthy encounter a child had with a ghost, but nothing came of it.
- The facility repairs are coming along well. Still a long way to go, but progress is good.
- We head into the room where the everwhite [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] lies, and we see that [[Brutus]] has gotten it cleaned up and has a fire going.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] feels anxious here, remembering everwhite trees and how their leaves turn red when human sacrifice is made near them.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires after a journal. [[Brutus]] notes he once had one, but suspects it has since been lost.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes that the handle of the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] appears to have dried blood stains on it.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] pricks his hand and opens the door handle, opening it to see the ruins of another castle, with a blizzard blowing through it intensely.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] step through to insure there's a way we can get back.
- On the other side, a castle in even worse condition greets us. It is freezing, and an old [[House Aurelius|House Arelius]] banner sits against the wall.
	- On the other side is a [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] we've stepped through that appears to operate the same way.
- We all step through. We hear howling that doesn't sound like wolves. Something darker.
- We make our way down the a hall following the sound of something that sounds like flapping sheets. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes lead.
	- The sound is coming from behind a door. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] opens it and steps in.
	- We see a fire in the center of the room. Windows in the room are covered with cloth to keep out the wind or keep light from escaping.
	- By the fire is the man we met after killing the assassins. [[Artorius]]. (We suspect him to be an angel.)
	- He asks us why we're here. [[Dharshoon]] explains his visions and the bells we are to ring.
	- He warns of a coming darkness, that hasn't happened in an age.
	- Creatures from the old world are coming. That is why [[Artorius]] is here.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks if [[Artorius]] is an angel. [[Artorius]] skillfully dances around the question.
	- He confirms we are in [[The Northwest|the Northwest]] of [[Varanor]]. A ruined city now that once stood against the forces of darkness.
	- [[Artorius]] says we must be going, including himself.
	- [[Artorius]] offers a bandage to mend [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s cut hand. He wraps it and feels unnatural warmth.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices a crimson breastplate under [[Artorius]]' armor and asks if he's a member of [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]]. He is not, leaving [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] somewhat confused. This man is a great curiosity.
- [[Artorius]] leads the way, taking us through the old city. ![[Session 21 art 1.webp|hh-pop]]
	- We think we hear strangle clicking sounds, but see nothing. We move quietly.
	- We get through the city, and find ourselves on the tundra. Nothing but baren land and snow.
- We trek for several hours, following [[Artorius]]' until he leads us to a cave that only someone who's been here before could know its location.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] ask's about the city's name. It has been lost. [[Artorius]] does not know it, nor did he visit it when it stood.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks how much he knows of [[House Aurelius|House Arelius]]' exploits on this side of the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]]. He says it was exploratory in nature, but never really amounted to anything.
	- We are to stay in the cave for the night with no fire. We do not want to attract what wanders the wastes.
	- As we begin to bed down, we note the blizzard seems to be dying down.
	- On first watch, as the snow has stopped, [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s eyes catch something in the distance climbing over the hills. The proportions seems strange and the figure gaunt.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] wakes [[Artorius]] asking what it is. He says it is something from the deep. Evil incarnate.
- The rest of us wake and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] informs of us what he saw.
- As we resume our journey, [[Artorius]] gets more info on [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphim]] from [[Dharshoon]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] thinks he hears something near. It sounds almost insect-like. We keep moving with no encounter.
- We eventually reach a forest of everwhite trees. Their leaves are green. We move into the wood. ![[Session 21 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Dharshoon]], keeping an eye out, sees a pair of blue eyes staring at us from a distance. Then another. Then 20.
- [[Artorius]] tells us to run. So we do.
- We run through the forest until we reach a cliff wall stretching high above us.
- Whatever chases us is getting closer. We run along the cliff edge, gaining height until we're at the top of a plateau.
- Atop the plateau, we see the temple with the bells from [[Dharshoon]]'s dream. We race toward it. ![[Session 21 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
- Once inside, [[Dharshoon]] magically erects a spike growth in the doorway.
