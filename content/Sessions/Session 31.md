## Previously

- On the road to [[Sanctuary]], [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] said he comes from an island west of [[The West|the West]]. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] thinks his unknown [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] sister is a half-sister through his father, a scoundrel city lord in the east.
- The party joined [[Henry Grey|King Henry Grey]]’s army, which was worn down everywhere except [[House Cromwell]]’s. At the [[Black Bear Inn]], [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] lost 100 gp arm-wrestling [[Lord Raymond Grimston]], and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] lost 50 gp at darts to [[Roose Cromwell]].
- At [[Henry Grey|Henry]]’s war council, [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] warned that the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’Haadi]] will hit [[Grimhold]] within a month. [[Henry Grey|Henry]] doubted the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] were allied with [[Ra'Haad|Ra’Haad]], and the party came around to thinking the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] had inadvertently told [[Ra'Haad|Ra’Haad]] how weak [[Harrenor]] is by paying them to raid within its borders.
- At the [[Sanctuary]] church, [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] revealed his deal with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]. The “[[The false clergy of Sanctuary|clergy]]” turned out to be [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers in disguise. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]’s father stabbed [[Henry Grey]] with "[[Titus Lancaster]]’s regards,” and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] were taken prisoner.
- Outside, [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] and [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] forces routed the [[House Grey|Grey]] army. The rest of the party, with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]’s sword glowing and [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]]’s fire spells, fought free, then retreated with the survivors instead of going back for their friends.
