# Session 11

← [[Session 10]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 11|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 12]] →

## Previously

- The party rejoined **[[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]]**, who was hastily evacuating his estate after the location of his freed-slave city was exposed. The party agreed to follow him for protection.
- In the city's slave markets, they discovered an influx of [[Harrenor|Harrenoran]] women, including **[[Theodore Silver|Theodore]]'s sister,** **[[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]]**. They planned a rescue after she and other slaves were sold to a man with an **[[Cult of Abyssal Fire|Abyssal Flame]] medallion**.
- The rescue plan led them to a ruined church. After stealthily eliminating two guards, they found a secret stairway leading to an underground [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] lair.
- They fought their way through several rooms, battling [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and **howlers**. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] brutally interrogated a [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] for information on the slaves' whereabouts.
- A [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]] of **eternium with ruby eyes** was discovered, and it gave [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] a sense of comfort.
- They found **[[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]]** and other women held captive. A ritual was underway to summon a demon using the last captive woman.
- Using his Goliath Large Form, **[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]** **transformed and grew in size**, and the party led the women to an inner room to escape the demon and [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]].
- Inside, they found an ancient, chained **[[Therond|dragon]] named** **[[Therond]]**. [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]]'s [[Theo's Talisman|talisman]], when inserted into the chains, freed the [[Therond|dragon]], which then annihilated the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and demon.
- [[Therond]], promising his aid to [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] in the future, flew away with the dead captive woman. The party escaped with the rescued women.
- After a week of travel, we arrive at [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s freed-slave city—[[Larad]]—a walled town near an oasis, guarded by a variety of mercenaries.

## Now

- As we arrive (4 days from [[Na'Buchar]]) , attendants within the city intercept up us and take our the rescued women and [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]] from our charge.
- [[Larad]] is easily defended, with only one entrance into it. It is walled with basic walls and defense towers.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] and [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] come out to greet us upon arrival.
- We explain our encounter with the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and the [[Therond|dragon]] before our arrival.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] takes us to a building service as a meeting space and armory, beckoning us all to sit around a table with a map laid around it.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] informs us he feels [[Larad]]'s location has been compromised. He would have the entire city evacuate and head north, taking the weeks long journey to [[Harrenor]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] questions how he would protect the city's populous as it flees. He says the 60 mercenaries he's hired will do the job.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] speaks for the group, saying we will as well.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] suggests we escape through [[The Blight|the Blight]] and hope we are not pursued.
- As we're discussing, a messenger comes to tell us of a rider at the main gate, carrying a [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] banner.
- We follow [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] to the gate and watch the interaction.
- The messenger declares he has been sent on behalf of the Imir.
	- If [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] turns over the slaves he will be allowed to live and retain his property and estates.
	- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] argues that he has paid lawfully for these slaves.
	- The messenger says that since [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] is a pagan, his rights are forfeit.
	- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] asks if he and his slaves may leave in exile.
	- The messenger says [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] may leave, but his slaves are forfeit and they may not.
	- The messenger declares [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] has 2 days to decide, then flees.
- The party thinks we should flee within the next day, but [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] is convinced it's too late and we're likely surrounded already.
- We discuss our options and decide the city must be evacuated tonight, with no exception.
- We start passing out what arms there are to any men who looked capable.
- As we do so, a one eyed large [[Harrenor|Harrenorian]] man approaches us, asking if he gets to fight Rahaadi if he fights alongside us. We tell him, yes.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tests him with a sword flourish. He reacts magnificently, then introduces himself as [[Tiberius]]. ![[Session 11 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] checks in with his sister and lets her know what's going on.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Dharshoon]] acquire a bunch of caltrops and scorpion venom with the help of [[Dharshoon]]'s bees.
- We ensure [[Tiberius]] is equipped with the right weapon to defend himself. He prefers a greataxe or a maul. ![[Session 11 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- As we're preparing, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notices a garden statue depicting a younger [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] and a woman who appears to be his wife.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] confides this information with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] who suggests keeping it to themselves until after we've fled the city. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] agrees.
- At this point, we've armed roughly 100 additional men, though they are untrained.
- We consult [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] on how we should make formation. After consulting with [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] and other trusted men, he's decided he wants us toward the front, leading the people forward with some archers.
- We set out, following [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], who knows the land best, being careful to use as little light as possible, to keep our movement hidden.
- As we exit the rocky hills around the city, a vast desert and an star filled sky lies before us.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] constantly looks over his shoulder, worrying about [[Beatrice Silver|Beatrice]].
- [[Tiberius]] travels toward the front, near the party. ![[Session 11 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- We travel through the night without incident. We see the sun begin to rise.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] sees a disturbance up ahead, worried it's evidence of a scout ahead.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] and [[Dharshoon]] run up to where the man stands, attempting to kill him before he's able to report back.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] attempts to chase him down, but [[Dharshoon]] shoots the scout as he's attempting to escape, killing him with one well-placed shot.
- We continue through the rest of the day with no issue. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], leads us well.
- At sunset, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] helps [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] navigate using his elvish darkvision.
- We continue onward and being to hear a slight rumble in the ground. A dust cloud forms in the distance in front of us. A massive force approaches.
- We and the men with us, form a line and prepare to defend the people. [[The Almighty|Almighty]] help us.

COMBAT!

- The enemy approaches on horseback and begins firing arrows. Both sides start taking casualties.
- The mounted archers charge and gets close enough for us to retaliate.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] rushes the mounted archers and attempts to dismount one and steal a horse. He does not succeed.
- We and [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]'s men pepper them with arrows and they are forced to retreat.
- We give chase and kill several more, though we do lose a few men in the retreat.
- Just as we think we've fought them off, a secondary group of horsemen charge from behind them, this group with scimitars.
- We clear the riders, which are then followed by several [[Ra'Haadi forces|cataphracts]] and light archers on foot. ![[Session 11 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
- We manage to use our archers and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s caltrops to leverage ourselves the advantage we need to succeed in the fight.
- Despite our efforts, more and more attackers come forward, several slipping past us to harass the defenseless people.

## End Combat

- Just as we think we've lost hope, a deep bellow and the sound of reptilian wings beat in the air. [[Therond]] has arrived.
- The endless sea of men streaming towards us is suddenly lit aflame as [[Therond]] sweeps overheard, swiping at those who aren't burned with his claws.
- The heat from his breath is so intense, it turns the sand to glass.
- We retreat to the front line and find [[Tiberius]], [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]], and [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] alive, though [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] is grievously wounded.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] takes the potion of healing he has and gives it to [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]], but [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] pushes it away, knowing it won't help.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]], wincing through the pain, says his work is done, and confesses that [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] looks so much like his mother.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] takes [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]]'s hand and says "lead them to paradise for me," then dies.
- [[Dharshoon]] uses [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s cloak to wrap the body.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] offers to carry the body. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] wishes for [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] to be buried.
- [[Therond]], having fully killed or scattered the remaining [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] force, flies away, his work here done.
- As [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] slowly carries up the body through the shocked people, a single elven woman starts singing and elvish funeral dirge.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] knows the language. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks for a translation, and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] obliges.
- We rest for the night, and move onward.
- We lost a total 30 souls, between mercs and armed freemen.
- Over the next month, we and the refugees travel with few other incidents not easily handled before finally arriving at the gates of [[Grimhold]].

**Ancient Elvish Funeral Dirge for [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]]******
_(Sung in a clear, sorrowful voice, with notes that carry both the weight of grief and the lightness of a soul set free.)_

_The desert wind, so harsh and old,_
_Now sings a name, a tale of gold._
_Not gold of coin, but heart and hand,_
_A sun that rose in shadowed land._

_(The melody softens, becoming more personal.)_

_Sahir, our shepherd, kind and true,_
_He saw the chains that bound us, too._
_He gave us hope in dusty air,_
_A whispered promise, a answered prayer._

_(The chorus swells with a blend of sorrow and strength.)_

_Sleep now, friend, your journey's done,_
_Beneath the gaze of moon and sun._
_The chains are broke, the spirits fly,_
_A star returns to desert sky._

_(The final verse is quiet, a promise and a final farewell.)_

_Our freedom song, a lasting plea,_
_Will echo now, for all to see._
_And though you rest, your light remains,_
_A blooming flower in the pouring rains._
