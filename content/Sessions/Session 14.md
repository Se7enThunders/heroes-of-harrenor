# Session 14

← [[Session 13]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 14|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 15]] →

## Previously

 - The party encountered a mysterious [[Artorius|Outrider]] after finding a dead [[The Black Hands|Black Hand]] assassin, noting his strangely warm hand and ancient gear. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and the party speculated he might be a supernatural being.
- The party's plan to save [[Alanna Selmy|Alanna]] was a complete failure. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] was sent into the [[Blackbear Inn]] alone, was beaten, and his brother-in-law [[Garlan Selmy|Garlan]] was killed by [[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Baring]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] was tasked with a new mission by [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]]: to retrieve [[Robert Lancaster]] from the [[House Grey|Grey]]'s camp and deliver him to [[Kingsmont]] where he will meet [[Titus Lancaster]], or else his sister will be tortured.
- The party decides to double cross the [[House Grey|Grey]]'s and trade [[Robert Lancaster]] for [[Alanna Selmy|Alanna]] instead of [[Sophia Grey]], but quickly have that plan thwarted.
- The party, with a new purpose, traveled back to [[Henry Grey]]'s camp. They were met by [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]] who was assigned to escort the party to [[Kingsmont]] to trade [[Robert Lancaster]] for [[Sophia Grey]].
- Upon arrival at [[Kingsmont]], [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] was debriefed by [[Titus Lancaster]]. Shortly thereafter, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] overhears [[Catherine Lancaster]] informed [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] that [[Sophia Grey]] had escaped, leaving the party in a precarious and uncertain position.

NOW (currently in [[Kingsmont]] castle under [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] control)
 - [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] returns to the quarters where the party is being housed, along with [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] and his men.
- He breaks the news that [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] is missing.
- We turn to [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] for his guidance in the matter.
- He tells us we should wait for the [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]]'s next move. He's worried they might try to switch [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] with another girl.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] tells us our next meeting is tomorrow, so [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests we peruse the city, while secretly looking for [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] likes the idea.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] informs us his family has an account with the bank here. We could potentially take advantage of that.
- We decide to exit the palace grounds and explore the city.
- The city is sprawling. One of the biggest and busiest on the continent.
- We head to the colonial bank first.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] buys some candied dried fruit on the way there.
- We're stopped by guards upon approaching the bank.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is permitted entry while the rest of us wait outside.
- As we're waiting outside, [[Dharshoon]] makes himself a honey treat. A wealthy man walks by and comments on it.
- The man is fascinated and asks to purchase some honey. He pays 20 gold for it, then asks about franchising.
- [[Dharshoon]] kindly declines, only because he's not from the area.
- The man gives him a token and tells him to go to any colonial bank and present the coin to begin negotiations, offering [[Dharshoon]] a 30% cut of the profits.
- He introduces himself as [[James Goldblum]].
- The party informs [[Dharshoon]] the offer is ridiculous. [[Dharshoon]] is aware. He was just being polite.
- Meanwhile, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] meets with the head banker.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] asks for 5k gold and is informed his family owes 100k. He is denied entirely and asked to remind his father that they want their money and soon.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] is escorted out.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] informs us of the bad news.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] urges the party forward to see if they can find [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
- As we move toward the outside of the city, we notice a large foundry, easily the biggest on for many leagues.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] goes into the foundry and tries bargaining for a well-made shortsword. They stand firm and he buys it.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] finds an alley with some street urchins hanging out in it. He offers them his candied fruit for information. ![[Session 14 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
- They don't know where a woman matching [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]]'s description might be, but says he should check near the north gate.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thanks them each with another treat and a gold piece per child, asking them to seek them out if they do see [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] anywhere.
- Most of the rest of the party stocks up on better equipment while we're in the area.
- We head to the north gate. It's a lower class neighborhood.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] finds some children playing who tell him that a woman matching [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]]'s description was seen leaving with a man in armor wearing armor and ([[William Arden|William]]) [[House Arden|Arden]] colors.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gives the children the rest of his candied fruit in thanks for this information.
- He brings this info to the group. We think someone disguised as an [[House Arden|Arden]] soldier took her out of the city.
- As the sun begins to set, we return to our quarters and inform [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] of what we've learned.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] requests that [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] sneak about the castle, listening in on conversations of the guards. He does so. ![[Session 14 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] overhears a conversation explaining that a rogue [[The King's Guard|kingsguard]] escaped with [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
	- They've procured a new hostage quickly. [[Sir Eamon Douglas]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] realizes he's overhearing [[Titus Lancaster]] and his daughter [[Catherine Lancaster|Catherine]].
	- He makes a stealthy exit and returns to the group.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] talks the [[Dharshoon]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] about an idea he has, basically to defraud Mr. [[James Goldblum|Goldblum]], making sure not to involve [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] in the conversation.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] rejoins the party and informs us of what he's learned.
- We chew on this new info, then head to bed.
- The next morning we head to the meeting. [[Catherine Lancaster]] is there.
- They do as we suspect and offer to trade [[Sir Eamon Douglas|Eamon Douglas]] instead of [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]].
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] does no initially agree to the exchange. We are dismissed for now.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] tells us to ride north and see if [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] has been taken to [[Godric Arden]].
- We leave [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] and head to east to [[Brightguard]] on horseback. The road is wide and even. It is an easy trip.
- We do notice the countryside has been devastated by the war.
- We see a slain knight of [[Godric Arden]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] convinces the group to stop and bury him, taking his sword, armor, and other possessions back to [[Godric Arden|Godric]] to return it to his family.
	- The sword has a distinct green emerald in its pommel.
- We meet the same old man on the cart with the donkey that sold us the [[Strange Coins|strange coins]] and other trinkets. ![[Session 14 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- We buy a bunch of the [[Strange Coins|strange coins]] from him as well as some healing potions.
	- [[Dharshoon]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] purchase magically infused arrows.
	- We conclude our business and the strange old man disappears.
- We make our way to [[Brightguard]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] talks to a guard at the gate and gets an update on things.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] has been talking to a strange monk of late.
- We make our way to see the king.
	- It's a rainy day.
	- We note the banners of the [[House Arden|Arden]] household are different now. Above the lion's head is the symbol of [[The Almighty|the Almighty]] emblazoned in fire. ![[Session 14 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- We enter and see [[Sir John Marshall]]. He was at the battle of [[Kingsmont]].
	- He warns us the king has been somewhat unpleasant lately.
	- We open the door to the throne room to see [[Godric Arden|Godric]] looking out the window.
	- He's with a knight in crimson armor trimmed in gold, a knight of [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] tells all.
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] says the war has not been going well.
	- He introduces [[Sir Ignatius]] with [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] of [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Fire]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks after [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]], who has not been seen in [[Brightguard]].
	- [[Godric Arden|Godric]] weighs his decision to ally with [[Henry Grey|King Henry Grey]].
	- He tells us to visit with [[Sir Ignatius|Ignatius]] in the meantime.
	- He also gifts [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] a set of plate armor for his service.
