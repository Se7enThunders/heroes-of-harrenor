**Quests in play:** [[22 Wake Guinevere|Wake Guinevere]]

## Previously

- The party defeated [[The molten demon|the molten demon]] haunting [[Telandor]]. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] used [[Starbinder]] to dispel magical darkness, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] knocked the creature from a stone spire, and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] landed the killing blow, decapitating it before it exploded in flame.
- After the battle, the city's unnatural fires began to die out. The party recovered a strange ember-like crystal from the demon's remains and discovered evidence that a massive blackened [[The red dragon of Telandor|red dragon]] is patrolling the ruins overhead.
- Exploring deeper into [[Telandor]], the party encountered lingering victims of the city's fall, learned more of [[Guinevere|Gwenivere]], and discovered signs that the city had been preparing for war against something called "winged fire."
- The party infiltrated a ruined church filled with treasure, charred corpses, and evidence of ancient [[Sable Wardens]]. After accidentally drawing the [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]]'s attention, they escaped through hidden catacombs beneath the altar.
- While exploring the catacombs, the party fought zombies and berserkers, uncovered records of the [[Swords of the Church]], and found a note describing an [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] in the heart of the city that always receives light, providing a likely lead for continuing their search for the Dark Below and the secrets surrounding [[Telandor]]'s fall.NOW - The note about the [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] notes that it is underground and yet still receives light at all times.
- The party continues to explore the catacombs, hoping to find it.
- Looking through one of the rooms, we find some old artwork depicting the [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] shining in moonlight with a hooded man holding a bow sitting in front of it.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] recalls a man from [[Ra'Haad]] talking about a [[The Watcher|Watcher]], tasked with caring for [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhites]] that grew in [[Ra'Haad]], which were/are exceptionally rare.
- Next room we explore is filled with gargoyle statues and a small stone pool. In a corner of the room is a long dead Varanoran man. ![[Session 26 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
	- It appears he was killed by a large raking claw slash across his chest. He's been here for centuries at least.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] loots a gold ring from the body.
- [[Dharshoon]] notices a small folded piece of parchment tucked in one of the gargoyle's wings.
	- It details a man recounting of a friend going off to fight invaders and not returning and a reference "[[The Iron Fist|Ironhand]]."
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] leads us on, finding a locked door. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] picks the lock, revealing an small archive within.
	- The records are kept on scroll mostly. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] steps in, commenting the amount of hidden knowledge here.
	- There are dead scribes here and blood stained parchment. The scribes look to have been killed by sword/spear.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] discovers a scroll that describes the church we're seeking. It holds a [[The Kilns|kiln]] that holds an ember of holy fire.
	- The remainder of the scrolls are too old to be of use, turning to dust in our hands.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] manages to carefully unfurl one that describes the lady of the city. The [[The Kilns|kiln]] was safeguarded by the lord of the city, passed down from each generation to safeguard, but was guarded by the lady when he went out for battle. [[Guinevere|Gwenivere]] is mentioned, but not her husband.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] finds a blood stained scroll detailing info on the sable wardens and their quest for immortality, noting higher crime rates and human trafficking in the east.
	- [[Dharshoon]] finds a scroll detailing the catacombs we're in that describes the [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] being a level up, and a hidden alter a level below dedicated to [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]].
- The next room [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] investigates is trapped, nearly killing several of the party with a heavy bolt. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] growls.
- It leads to a locked vault. All our attempts to open it fail. No key we possess opens it.
- We circle back and find the stairs to the level above us.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon]] has [[Dharshoon]] recap him on the party's purpose. He recounts their quest to ring the 3rd bell. He is satisfied.
- We ascend to the upper level, noting corpses in the hallway, some bodies having bones charred black.
- We find a large circular room with natural light coming from above on a large [[Everwhite Trees|Everwhite tree]] sitting in the center. ![[Session 26 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
	- A dead man lies next to the tree. It's the only one in the room filled with hundreds of bodies that are not burned.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] moves up to inspect the dead man, noting a pressure plate to avoid, but missing another one under a corpse, stepping on it.
	- No visible change happens until we hear 3 loud horn blasts.
	- We hear the beating of wings overhead. We retreat, fearing the [[The red dragon of Telandor|red dragon]]'s wrath.
	- [[Dharshoon]] notices the bow on the man dead against the tree is special, but it's too far so we wait for now, hoping to return.
	- We retreat tactically.
	- The [[The red dragon of Telandor|dragon]] sticks its head in the hole above the room and finds us, shooting a blast of fire our way.
		- We are out of reach of the fire blast, but the debris hits several of us, including [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]].
		- The chamber collapses behind us. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes we can get to from the surface again later if needed.
- We're back in the level below, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] noting that gargoyle statues are now in the room that weren't there before.
	- They're flanking a room we were in before. We head in to investigate a door inside we didn't check previously.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] finds a trap door in the room and successfully disarms it.
	- [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] ([[Godfrey the Squire|Balon's squire]]) notes the Gargoyles are looking into the doorway.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] continues to lead the way, noting torches burning in the hallway ahead.
- We move up further and find a secret doorway. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] hears noise behind it. It's not locked or trapped. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] moves through first.
	- He sees four [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] in the room ahead. We move to ambush, as they haven't seen us yet.

## Combat

 - [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] makes quick work of the first [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]].
- The rest of the party moves in, then [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] runs in and cuts two [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] clean in half, cross body, then up the middle.
- We kill 3 [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]], but one escapes, heavily injured. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] and [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] pursue.
- Rauoen also pursues, the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] cornered in a circular room fill with skeletal remains.
- The last [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] is completely surrounded. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] demands his surrender.
- The [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] teleports out of our grasp and makes for another secret door, using an amulet to gain entry and flee.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] pursues and hits him with a bow shot as the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] attempts to feel down to the next sublevel, incapacitating the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] catches up quickly and pins the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] to the ground by his wrist. The [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] spits and curses.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] kills him, making a quick judgment call that he won't be of use for information.

## End Combat

 - [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] loots a [[The Crimson Gladius|crimson gladius]] shortsword from the dead [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]].
	- [[Dharshoon]] notes it's a health stealing blade.
- We head for stairs downward, finding 20 gold and a healing potion per man in a small adjacent room on the way down.
- The stairs open into a massive chamber with a lit brazier glowing dimly in the center of the room.
- Many bodies fill the space, strewn about, wearing the garb of the [[Swords of the Church]].
- We hear the clash of swords as we see [[The Grand Marshal|the Grand Marshal]] wielding a glowing white sword, battling some still standing zombified [[Swords of the Church]].
- He sees us and lifts his sword to us in threat. Other bodies stand up as he does so.
