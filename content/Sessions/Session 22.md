# Session 22

← [[Session 21]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 22|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 23]] →

## Previously

- [[Henry Grey]] finalizes terms with [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]: [[Sunwatch]] remains politically understated for now, but [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]’s lordship is effectively secured; the captured [[The Janissary|Janissary]] is imprisoned rather than executed. Tension grows among the southern lords, especially with [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] and [[House Cromwell|Cromwell]] conferring.
- The party decides to prioritize [[Dharshoon]]’s northern vision over pursuing the [[Sable Wardens]], planning to test the everwhite [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] at [[Sunwatch]] before attempting a long northern route.
- At [[Sunwatch]], [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] activates the blood-marked [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]], transporting the party to a ruined castle in northwest [[Varanor]]—an abandoned [[House Aurelius|Arelius]] outpost in a blizzard-ravaged land.
- They reunite with [[Artorius]], who warns of an ancient darkness returning and guides them across tundra and ruins toward the everwhite forest and the [[Bell temple of Varanor|temple of bells]] from [[Dharshoon]]’s vision.
- Pursued by numerous undead, the party retreats into the temple, seals the entrance with ranger magic, and prepares to fight within as the undead press in.

## Now (Resuming Combat)

- We deal with a small group of undead at the main entrance, but realize quickly there's many more on the opposite side of the ruins, led by a powerful [[The undead knight|undead knight]].
- [[The undead knight]] throws an orb of fire at us, doing heavy damage. ![[Session 22 art 1.webp|hh-pop]]
- Despite being surrounded, [[Artorius]] moves at inhuman speed, defending himself skillfully.
- [[Artorius]] calls down holy fire, igniting everything around him. ![[Session 22 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] go down, are helped back up, but then [[The undead knight|the undead knight]] closes in, knocking down [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] again.
- We manage do destroy all the undead with exception of [[The undead knight|the undead knight]], [[Artorius]] stepping in.
- We surround [[The undead knight|the undead knight]]. It blasts us all with an orb of fire again in retaliation.
- After we all wear it down, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] gets the final blow on [[The undead knight|the undead knight]], skewering it with his rapier. ![[Session 22 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
	- The magic holding it together breaks and it crumbles into a pile of bones and ash.

## End Combat

- [[Dharshoon]] heals [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] helps him to his feet. We are all worse for wear.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks [[Dharshoon]] to do what he came here to do, so we can move on quickly, fearing further conflict in our wounded state.
- As we walk through the ruins we notice other bodies, belonging to the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] of [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|Abyssal Fire]], as well as what we can only assume are [[The Black Hands|Black Hand]] Assassins.
	- It appears they killed each other.
	- [[Artorius]] informs us they've been at war for ages; the [[The Black Hands|Black Hand]] looking to become gods, and the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|Cult]] looking to bring their evil gods into control of the world.
- We notice a chest tucked away in the corner of the ruins.
	- Inside is 5 potions of greater healing and 10 healing potions.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] finds a [[The Black Ring|black ring]] of that is not cold near the body of [[The undead knight|the undead knight]].
	- He puts it on and begins attuning to it. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] questions the wisdom in it.
- We locate the belltower, and help [[Dharshoon]] to up the interior.
- [[Dharshoon]] climbs to the top and takes in the view. He can see the sea to the north.
	- He rings the bell and nothing happens initially.
- We attempt to rest. [[Dharshoon]] hears wolves in the distance.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] worries we missed something or did something wrong. He steps away to pray, and feels a sense of peace and agrees to leave.
- We begin making our way back to the magical door we came from.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices a dire wolf in the distance, noting it isn't hunting us, but is watching us.
	- It keeps pace with us, getting closer as we go.
	- Eventually, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] turns to confront it, approaching it slowly, weapons stowed.
	- The dire wolf approaches [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] letting the it smell it. It lets him pet it.
	- We notice lots of other dire wolves around us.
	- We hear a female voice say "it's not often he takes a liking to people."
	- We see a small girl with platinum hair, wearing a simple dress, barefoot in the snow appearing out of nowhere. ![[Session 22 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
	- [[Dharshoon]] talks to the girl, discerning she is a [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] of Lady [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphim]].
	- They talk for a time, and she tells us of the 3rd bell far to the south in the sands of the desert.
	- The wolves seem particularly fond of [[Artorius]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks what happens when we ring the 3rd bell.
	- The girl tells us that once we ring all 3 then Lady [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphim]] can return to the world and begin her work of restoring the world to its rightful state.
	- She says her name is [[Leana]] when [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] asks.
	- She agrees to accompany us back to our destination. Her and 30 dire wolves.
- We return to the ruined castle where the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] door still lies.
	- We thank her for her escort.
	- [[Artorius]] must depart as well.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] leads us back to [[Sunwatch]], [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] going last.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] screams at the sky before stepping through, =="Father! I curse you, in the name of the true God! [[The Almighty|Almighty]] curse you! You will lose, and I pray my God will let me be the instrument of your destruction!"== He spits on the ground and then step through the door.
- We take a long rest. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] crashes hard and rests longer.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is uncomfortable with the ring he picked up, asking [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] for advice.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thinks if it's cursed, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] wouldn't want to part with it.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is uncomfortable with it still so [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests giving it to [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], since he seems less worried about the ethics of things like this.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] thinks the ring might be used to track us. He puts it on anyway, asking us to keep an eye on him.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] feels [[The Palantir|the Palantir]] in his pack is active when [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] wears the ring.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] takes out [[The Palantir|the Palantir]] to confirm, unwrapping it making sure not to touch it with his skin.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] takes off the ring and [[The Palantir|the Palantir]] becomes less active.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] theorizes that the ring is a key to using [[The Palantir|the Palantir]].
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] asks [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] to hold his arm while he touches [[The Palantir|the Palantir]]. Just before he does, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] releases [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s arm and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] touches [[The Palantir|the Palantir]] alone.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] connects with [[The Palantir|the Palantir]] and we sense he's being shown something.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] pulls his hand away of his own accord.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] says he was shown a vision similar to our collective dreams, but it felt like he had more control of what he saw this time.
		- he see [[Henry Grey]] stabbed in the heart by who you think is [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]].
		- [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s Mother, [[Audrey Grey|Audrey]], weeping tears of blood.
		- [[Sophia Grey]] carried off by unknown men.
		- men lying dead on a series of ramparts, Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Balon Stellaris]] among them.
		- a towering figure of a man, shadow its skin and wearing a cloak of hellfire.
	- We agree to stow both the ring and [[The Palantir|Palantir]] separately, but keep them on us. [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] will carry the ring, finding a chain to make a necklace of the ring.

## Long Rest

- We awake and notice the bite of winter is beginning to loosen. We hear birds coming back to the trees and see snow beginning to melt. Spring is nearly here.
- [[Brutus]] has continued to repair and restore the castle. It has a full mess now, to help feed those who've come to aid in its restoration.
