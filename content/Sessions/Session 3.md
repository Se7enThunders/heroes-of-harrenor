**Quests in play:** [[04 Take Stoneheath|Take Stoneheath]]

## Goals

- Head to [[Stoneheath]] and infiltrate via secret entrance we learned of from the old man vendor on the road.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]] and his men are backing us up and will be coming up behind us.
- Traveling with [[Elara]] (Rebekah) (aasimar), [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen Lawson]] (human) (Josh), [[Theodore Silver]] (human) (Preston), [[Draven Throssenheiser]] (half-elf) (Jake), [[Dharshoon]] (human) (Atmeos).

- Early evening
- after the encounter with the [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] men. (all killed and buried)
- We discuss the plan moving forward.
	- The plan is to get everyone in the hidden entrance, then use that as a base of operations to gather more intel from those of us who can disguise themselves well.
- We see a single rider coming up the road.
	- He's a [[House Grey|Grey]] man.
		- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] sent him ahead to check on us. He asks if we've been able to scout ahead yet. We have not.
		- The army is a few miles out. He offers to march it up to [[Stoneheath]] and back us up yet tonight.
		- He will have the men in position by nightfall.
- It's a cloudy night, which will aid in our stealth.
- We make it to town. There are more guards on the walls than anticipated. ![[Session 3 art 7.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Dharshoon]] ties up the horses out of sight and we dismount.
- We sneak through the forest around to the north side of the town and find the secret entrance is a sewer culvert.
	- [[Elara]] finds a spot near the entrance to hide and keep an eye on things. ![[Session 3 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]] ![[Session 3 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- We decide if we don't check in within an hour, that she needs to head back to the army and inform them of our failure.
		- [[Elara]] seems annoyed, but doesn't say so, and agrees.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] takes point, as he's the stealthiest.
	- As he leads us forward, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Dharshoon]] opt to stay in the sewers and keep an eye on things while [[Theodore Silver|Theadore]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] move through the camp gathering intel. ![[Session 3 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
- As we move through the sewer, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] vomits from the smell. ![[Session 3 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We come to a grate we can see light through.
	- We manage to dislodge the grate and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] lifts [[Theodore Silver|Theadore]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] through. ![[Session 3 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- The same rules apply. If they're not back within an hour, he'll leave and head back to [[Elara]] and [[Dharshoon]] and then the army.
	- [[Dharshoon]] heads back to tell [[Elara]] they've made it this far.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theadore]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] make it into the city wearing [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] attire. ![[Session 3 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- They find themselves among homes within the city.
	- They see some people moving around the town. In their disguises, no one cares about their presence.
	- Two soldiers pass them talking about nothing of importance.
- [[Dharshoon]] returns after checking in with [[Elara]]. He decides to scout ahead, further down the sewer, while [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] stays put.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theadore]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] continue scouting. Managing to get on some rooftops to get a birds eye view.
	- The climb down and stumble over a pauper.
	- They talk to the old man, trying to get information from him.
	- [[Sir James Stone|James]] and [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver Stone]] are in charge of the city. Twin knights.
	- They give him 2 gold for his trouble.
- [[Dharshoon]] scouts ahead and sees some more exits from the tunnel.
	- He sees a knight in plate armor patrolling the halls above.
- Having seen all they can from where they are, [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] decide to move closer to the command post for more intel.
	- It's well defended from the south by men with longbows.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] heads back to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] to fill him in on what they've learned.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] makes it back and checks in. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Dharshoon]] exchanges information with him.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] has [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] help him up the grate as far down as we can go to do more exploring.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] get spotted.
	- [[Sir James Stone|James]] and [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver Stone]] have spotted them and ask them why they're here (not knowing who they are) ![[Session 3 art 8.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] pretends to be drunk.
		- The twins buy the act, threatening to flog him in the future.
	- They turn to [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], claiming he looks familiar.
		- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] says his name is John (his father's name).
	- They're suspicious and ask them both to say what [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]]'s name is at the same time.
		- They don't both say the same name.
		- The twins draw their swords.
		- They call their men up and capture [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], taking them in for questioning.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gets up through the grate and finds himself in the main stronghold.
	- He sees [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] captured.
	- He sneaks back and fills in [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Dharshoon]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says he'll stay and the [[Dharshoon]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] should go back to [[Elara]] and meet up with the army, letting them know what we've learned.
	- They leave to catch up with [[Elara]] and fill her in.
	- As they speak. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]] approaches, a few guards with him.
	- The 3 fill him in.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] has 350 men with him.
	- They move up to the sewer and begin to sneak inside.
- Back with [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], they are interrogated. ![[Session 3 art 10.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- They're found out to have killed the patrol.
	- [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver]] realizes [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is John [[House Sanderfell|Sanderfell]]'s son.
	- They argue about which side is right and wrong.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] is mouthy, but the twins seem to be more interested in [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]].
	- They toss his sword to him, telling him to pick it up. ![[Session 3 art 9.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Sir James Stone|James]] squares off to fight him.
- The rest of the group, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] and 50 of his men make their way into the sewer.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] greets [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] and asks his plan.
	- He and his men in the sewers will head in through the 3rd grate into the main fortress.
	- Once there and we all get inside, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gets us into the courtyard.
	- We're spotted and an alarm is spotted.
	- We can tell the rest of [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]]'s men have begun their attack as well.
	- We get to where [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] are being held and see [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] fighting [[Sir James Stone|James Stone]].
	- The Stone brothers hear the alarm and stop the fight.

- COMBAT!
	- We move in to attack.
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] goads [[Sir James Stone|James Stone]] with a javelin toss, pulling him away from [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]. ![[Session 3 art 11.jpg|hh-pop]]
		- [[Elara]] transforms into her celestial form and joins the fray. ![[Session 3 art 12.jpg|hh-pop]]
		- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gets a kill on a pikeman.
		- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] moves in to defend [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] who is surrounded, disarming [[Sir James Stone|James Stone]].
		- The party managed to kill both of the twin knights and all but one of the men at arms (who surrendered).
- END COMBAT
	- After the defeat of these knights, Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] came up to the courtyard to tell the party of their capture of the town.
		- A [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldier in platemail attacks Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]], but is effortlessly cleaved in half by the knight’s sword, [[Starbinder]].
		- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] recognized that [[Starbinder]] was made of Aeternium, a material that is indestructible as far as men know.
			- These swords are extremely rare and highly prized among nobility.
			- Their edge never dulls, they are incredibly hard, strong, and sharp, and very difficult to forge.
			- [[Starbinder]] is one of two supposedly belonging to House [[House Stelaris|Stellaris]], the other being [[Flamebinder]] (though it has been rumored to have been lost).
	- After Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] gives a report of the casualties and the remaining holdouts in [[Stoneheath]], the party notices that a dying [[Sir James Stone]] is crawling towards his dead brother [[Sir Oliver Stone|Oliver]].
		- He dies of his wounds before embracing his brother one last time.
			- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] takes note of this and continues his internal questioning of the path he's on.
		- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] makes some questionable remarks of his thoughts on the lives of captured soldiers, which disturbs Sir [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]].
		- After this, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] informs the party that he will be searching the Twin Knights’ quarters for information.

## Now

Hopefully we get there in time to rescue [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]].
