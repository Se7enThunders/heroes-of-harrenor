# Session 30

← [[Session 29]] | [[index|Home]] | [[Joran's Journal#Session 30|Joran's journal entry]] | [[Session 31]] →

## Previously

- Spotting a 100,000+ [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’Haadi]] host near [[Grimhold]], the party chose sabotage over suicide — poisoning wells along its route and warning [[Sunwatch]] and [[Stag's Rest|Stag’s Rest]] — while reluctantly riding past a slave caravan (tied to [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]’s old crime syndicate, [[The Bloody Knives|the Bloody Knives]]) they had no realistic chance of saving.
- [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra’Haadi]] riders caught the party just short of [[Grimhold]]’s walls; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gave up his horse, took giant form, and carried [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Godfrey the Squire|Godfrey]] the rest of the way before 50 knights under [[Torren Grimston]] broke the charge, losing only one of their own.
- [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] revealed [[Henry Grey|King Henry Grey]] has mobilized south amid rising tension with [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] (whose men struck at [[Godric Arden|Godric]]’s), and that the party is now summoned north to a town called [[Sanctuary]], two weeks away.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] identified [[The Iron Fist|the Iron Fist]]’s ring as a cursed necrotic weapon (bonus damage, paid for in haunting voices); [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] wanted no part of it and handed it over to [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]].
- Repairs and defenses at [[Sunwatch]] are progressing under [[Brutus]]; a side trip through the [[The Everwhite Wardrobe|wardrobe]] to consult the [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]] [[Leana]] brought bad news (the rescued [[The Kilns|Kiln]]’s flame is dead, only [[Lady Seraphyne|Seraphyne]] can relight it) and a bombshell — [[Leana]] claims that [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]’s long-unknown sister is another [[Handmaidens of Seraphyne|handmaiden]].

## Now

 - We gather our strength and wits and hit the road for [[Sanctuary]], noticing the countryside is sparse, as well as the people. War has taken its toll.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] has a look of someone who's never been here before, asking about where he comes from.
	- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] says an island to the west of the Western province. He didn't get out much until recently.
- We discuss the happenings of our time recently, including what [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] might know about his sister.
	- He suspects he may have a half-sister, as his mother never said anything, but his father (a city lord in the east) was a scoundrel and not in his life at all.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notes his mother has passed.
	- No good leads there at the moment unless they sail half-way across the world to find [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]]'s father.
- We continue to travel. A few days out from [[Sanctuary]], we come across [[Henry Grey]]'s army marching toward [[Sanctuary]].
	- (we've been requested there by [[Henry Grey]], specifically [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], to be his token of force, as we've made a name for ourselves.)
- We approach the army, letting [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] take point.
- We note the most Houses' forces look to be depleted aside from [[House Cromwell]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] gives us a quick speech, reminding us of our purpose here, cementing our resolve (inspiring leader feat).
- We make camp nearby overnight, then agree to enter the camp together.
- We enter the [[Black Bear Inn]], hoping to make contact with whom we need to.
- As we enter, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] towers over all, but [[Lord Raymond Grimston|Raymond Grimston]], Lord of [[House Grimston]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] walks up to him and introduces himself as a friend of his brother [[Torren Grimston|Torren]].
	- [[Lord Raymond Grimston|Raymond]] pretends to come off as a haughty lord, but drops the façade quickly, warming up to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] quickly.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] turns to drinking while he waits, getting many cups deep, the challenges [[Lord Raymond Grimston|Raymond]] to an arm wrestling competition.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] struggles in the competition, but wins round 1, [[Lord Raymond Grimston|Raymond]] wins round 2 and 3. Losing a wager of 100 gp.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] jokes the table was slippery.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] challenges [[Roose Cromwell]] ([[Lord Otto Cromwell|Otto]]'s son) to a game of darts.
	- [[Roose Cromwell|Roose]] looks down his nose at [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], but the wager of 50 gp gets him interested.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] loses, but barely.
- Before turning in for the night, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] heads into the woods, following some other soldiers who are praying to [[The Almighty|the Almighty]], he stays a little longer, begging for guidance.
	- He receives no direct answers, but feels like [[The Almighty|the Almighty]]'s hand has led him to where he is now, and that gives him solace. He heads to bed.
- The next morning [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] rises early and notes an [[The escort at the Black Bear Inn|escort]] leaving one of the rooms with a small coin purse. He decides to follow quietly.
	- He follows her downstairs as she attempts to leave the inn, as a guard asks of her services. She agrees to it for a price.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] obtusely interrupts the "business exchange." He suggests the man's liege would be disappointed. The [[The escort at the Black Bear Inn|escort]] suggests otherwise.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] leaves them alone, then heads outside for fresh air and a sunrise.
- [[Henry Grey]] has called the lords together in the inn for a meeting. We listen in.
	- He notes we're 2 days from [[Sanctuary]], expecting to meet up with [[Godric Arden|King Godric]] [[House Arden|Arden]]. No word recently, but that's still the plan.
	- The plan is to meet with [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] in the church and discuss his options.
	- He then brings up [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], and asks him to debrief on what we saw in [[Ra'Haad]], and what's likely coming. The [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] march on [[Grimhold]].
	- This news unsettles everyone. [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] estimates they'll attack in force within a month.
	- [[Henry Grey]] is sending emissaries to their allies and enemies in [[Harrenor]], asking for a truce while they and the realm deals with this [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]].
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] speaks up, and insists the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] support the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]], as well as other dark organizations practicing dark magic.
	- [[Henry Grey]] has difficulty believing the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]]' involvement. We don’t have any tangible proof however, despite what we've seen.
	- [[Henry Grey]] dismisses the lords, but holds back the party.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] is curious about the new member ([[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]]) as well as the dark magic [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] mentioned, giving us a chance to prove what our claims.
	- As we consider what we think we know, perhaps we've jumped to conclusions about the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] involvement with [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] invasion specifically, though the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] can still be blamed for informing the [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] of the weakness in [[Harrenor]] currently, made worse by the [[Sable Wardens]]' involvement.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] feels this is more likely, as do we, having considered the whole of things.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] asks the rest of us to stay with the army while [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] helps negotiate with [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]].
	- He dismisses us and leaves.
- The army continues on the march to [[Sanctuary]] until it arrives. [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglass]]'s forces are arrayed to the north with a large force, larger than [[Henry Grey|Henry]]'s but won't be as large as the combined forces of [[Henry Grey]] and [[Godric Arden]].
- [[Sanctuary]] and the surrounding fields are as barren as any. The war here as taken its toll.
	- It's a not a large town, and is clearly built around the church with a group of buildings that are akin to a monastery making up a large portion of the town. ![[Session 30 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
	- The [[The false clergy of Sanctuary|clergy]] who run it are offering bread and wine to those who enter, promoting guest rights and order.
	- Only [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] enter and partake. The rest of the party holds back with the army.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] enter the church and see [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglass]] and some of his closest men waiting. [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] enters as well.
	- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] seems to think he has the upper hand and has no plans to negotiate his surrender.
	- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] notices [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] and mocks them both.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] proceeds with terms of surrender. [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] laughs and declines.
	- [[Henry Grey|Henry]] reminds that [[Godric Arden|Godric]] should be closing in soon. [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] is surrounded.
	- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] smiles and informs [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] that [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is weeks away and it's [[Henry Grey|Henry]] who's surrounded, as he's made a deal with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] notices that all [[The false clergy of Sanctuary|the clergy]] have blonde hair, and sees several wearing chainmail. It's a trap.
	- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglass]] cries out, "kill them!" The attack on [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] and his men commences.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] and his men are vastly outnumbered by these [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers in disguise.
	- Chaos ensues and the [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] fight for their lives alongside [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] and the few men with him.
	- One of the knight's removes his helm, revealing himself to be [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]'s father. He stabs [[Henry Grey]] in the chest, saying <mark>"[[Titus Lancaster]] sends his regards."</mark>
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]]'s men are killed, and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] is spared, per order of [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]]'s father.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] spits in his face, his father slaps him and orders him carried off.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is taken prisoner.
	- They're both taken outside to see the Douglasses and the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]] attacking the [[House Grey|Grey]] forces.
- (we switch the party being attacked by the two armies)

## Combat

- The party and along with the army and the camp becomes a battlefield in an instant, the party is swarmed by grunt level soldiers, axemen, and bowmen.
- We spring into action quickly, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] saying a prayer to [[The Almighty|the Almighty]], and his <mark>sword beginning to glow</mark>.
- Our attackers are poorly trained and fall quickly.
- [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] shows his worth, by casting fire spells that do tremendous damage.
- We quickly mop up the remaining soldiers attacking us.

## End Combat

 - Despite our victory, The [[House Grey|Grey]] forces have lost and are in full retreat.
- While we desperately want to retrieve our friends, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] pleads with the party to retreat with the [[House Grey|Grey]] forces. We do so.

(The party is split at session end. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] are captured, while [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], [[Dharshoon]], [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]], and [[Hadron Ironwood|Hadron]] have retreated the remaining [[House Grey|Grey]] Forces.
