## Goals

- Traveling with [[Elara]] (Rebekah) (aasimar), [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen Lawson]] (human) (Josh), [[Theodore Silver]] (human) (Preston), [[Draven Throssenheiser]] (half-elf) (Jake), [[Dharshoon]] (human) (Atmeos).
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] needs to find a maul and/or better armor.
- <mark>REMEMBER TO ROLL DAMAGE DICE TWICE! (once per turn) (savage attacker)</mark>

## Previous Summary

- [[Stoneheath]] is taken with [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]] and 350 men; party rewarded and levels up.
- [[Audrey Grey]] arrives to govern; [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] honors fallen knights and upgrades to splint armor.
- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] (red mask, [[Order of Holy Fire]]) reports townsfolk and [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] soldiers vanishing into the [[The cult temple|western woods]].
- In the forest, the party finds a [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cult]] site tied to the [[Cult of Abyssal Fire]]—including ritual murder and a demonic temple.
- After defeating [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultists]] and a summoned demon, [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] destroys the temple and declares [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] in the party’s debt.

## Now

- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] awakes in the morning to find he has been gifted fine [[Bracers of the Holy Order|bracers]] of the [[Order of Holy Fire|Holy Order]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] catches up with [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor Stelaris]] in the morning, just as [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] is heading out.
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] says he's off to investigate more [[Cult of Abyssal Fire|cultist]] activity.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires about joining the order.
	- [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] appreciates his interest, but says he can't help with that at the moment.
	- He will try to catch up with [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] later to pursue his interest.
- Our next task is to ride out with [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]] and [[Audrey Grey|Lady Audrey Grey]] to meet with [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglass]].
	- [[Dharshoon]] stays behind to see after his bees. (player couldn't make session)
- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]], with ~300 men meet us on the road.
	- Banner is red fox on a green field. ![[Session 5 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] is traveling with the party and ~50 men.
- [[Lord Callum Douglas]] is an older man that does not seem to be proficient in war. ![[Session 5 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Elara]] and [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] seem to recognize each other.
	- They exchange words. [[Elara]] seems to be dislike the man for some reason (backstory).
	- He looks on the party with disdain (several of the party determine he's racist).
- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] wants the party to go save a [[The hamlet|hamlet]] on the outskirts of [[Stoneheath]].
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] speaks ups and [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] shames him.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] chides [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and we turn away.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is aware that [[Leo Lancaster]] (an old acquaintance of his) has been tasked by [[Titus Lancaster]] to terrorize [[The Goldwood|the Goldwood]] and cause mayhem throughout the land.
	- [[Leo Lancaster|Leo]] is the one in charge of the soldiers and mercenaries that have invaded [[The hamlet|the hamlet]]. He is likely not there.
- We continue on through the lowlands and low hills (mirroring Appalachia irl) until we see [[The hamlet|the hamlet]].
	- The church in the village is on fire. ![[Session 5 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- People are being rounded up out of their homes.
- The party decides on a direct approach.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] charges in with [[Elara]]. The other 3 get in position. ![[Session 5 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- A man in the center of the village looks to be a northerman like [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], a mercenary.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] feels a faint sense of recognition.

COMBAT!

- The northman mercenary recognizes [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], calling him out and attacking him.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]'s horse is shot out from under him as he charges at the man.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] recognizes him as his cousin [[Ulf]]. ![[Session 5 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Ulf]] is a battle hardened veteran and will be difficult to kill.
- [[Elara]] whips up some wild magic blinding some of the attackers. ![[Session 5 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] recognizes one of the mercenaries as a fellow member of the crime syndicate he used to be a member of. A man named [[Constantine]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]], [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]], and [[Elara]] start taking shots at [[Ulf]]. ![[Session 5 art 7.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] manages to knock down [[Ulf]] who says "well played."
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says, "it's not a game," then decapitates [[Ulf]].
- The rest of the mercenaries scatter, including [[Constantine]].
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] takes a shot at the fleeing man and wounds him, but he continues to run on horseback.
	- We let him flee.

## End Combat

- The rest of the mercs flee, and the church is all but burned to cinders.
	- Valuables are left outside the church, the mercs unable to make off with them.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] discloses to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] that he knew one of these men, [[Constantine]], and describes their relationship to him.
	- They were both from a different continent. It's been 8 years since he's seen the man.
	- Now that [[Constantine]] has seen him here, he's worried what the implications are.
- We stay and aid the villagers in putting out fires and burying the dead.
- We collect 150 GP from (30 GP per man split 5 ways).
- We return to [[Stoneheath]] and debrief [[Audrey Grey|Lady Audrey Grey]] and Lord [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon Stelaris]].
	- We tell all and are congratulated.
- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] is concerned about what to do with [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]].
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] vouch for him.
	- Still, [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] is concerned.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] acquiesces imploring we keep an eye on him.
- We are to accompany [[Audrey Grey|Lady Grey]] and Lord [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] to negotiations with [[House Douglas]].
- At sunset, we set toward neutral ground outside [[Stoneheath]] and meet [[Lord Callum Douglas]] for negotiations.
- We enter the main tent where negotiations are to take place.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] produces [[Ulf]]'s head to prove our victory.
- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Lord Douglas]] accuses [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] of kinslaying.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] does not deny it, saying he destroys evil where he finds it, kin or no.
- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] is offered lands, title, and incomes, but refuses.
- Instead he wants to rise in station by having his son(s) marry into royalty.
- [[Audrey Grey|Lady Grey]] offers the hand of her daughter Sofia in marriage.
- [[Lord Callum Douglas|Douglas]] likes this and wants also for his daughter to marry [[Audrey Grey|Lady Grey]]'s son, to further solidify their alliance.
	- She agrees.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires of [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] as to why [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] joined the order and he did not.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] says that [[Baylor Stelaris|Baelor]] always wanted his sword [[Starbinder]], and didn't receive it as he wasn't the primary heir.
	- He then sought out a different relic and found [[Order of Holy Fire|the Order]] through it.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] inquires as to their next text. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] says he will ponder this and let us know tomorrow.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] asks [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] if he should tell [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] about [[Constantine]]. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] says he should.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] does so.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baelon]] takes the news well, not terribly concerned about it, but glad to have the information.
- We rest for the evening, all wondering what the war has in store for us next.
