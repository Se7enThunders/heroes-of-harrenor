Previously

- Departed from [[Brightguard]], headed south toward [[Ra'Haad|Ra’haad]] on a secret mission to investigate Lancaster-Ra’haadi alliance.
- Passed through [[Stag's Rest|Stag’s Rest]]; cold weather setting in.
- Entered [[Grimhold]], ruled by [[House Grimston]]; greeted by [[Torren Grimston|Torren]] “[[Torren Grimston|Red Claw]].”
- Tense moment at the gate—[[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]’s Varanoran tongue draws suspicion.
- Met [[Ahmed Al-Zahim]], a reformed ex-slave who offers to guide the party into [[Ra'Haad|Ra’haad]].
- Agreed to pose as slave traders ([[Dharshoon]] & [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]]), with [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] as [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] liaison, [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] & [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] as “slaves.”
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] warns that we must travel through [[The Blight|the Blight]]—dangerous, cursed land.
- Destination: [[Na'Buchar|Na’Buchar]], to meet [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]].
- [[The Blight|Blight]] Details: Cursed [[The green river|green river]], strange creatures, wyvern cries at night.
- Night watches reveal strange silhouettes; suspicion grows of being followed.
- Encountered six humanoid wights; intense combat with limited effect from non-magical weapons.
- Everyone scored a kill; all enemies defeated after a difficult battle.
- Took a long rest, recovering from the hardest fight so far.Now
- We awake still in [[The Blight|the Blight]]. The temp is shifting from cool to hot rapidly.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] congratulates us on our victory, but cautions us to keep an eye out for wyverns.
- We travel for half the day until we come to [[Ashfall]].
	- It was once a great city and trading hub, but now is now a crumbling shadow of itself. ![[Session 7 art 2.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- We see some signs of civilization within.
	- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] warns us that it is not living men that live there. It is things worse than what we've seen thus far.
	- Still, he says it is a better bet than wandering through the blighted desert.
	- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] says he has a safehouse here. We will hole up here.
- We move forward and the horses are spooked. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] calms them.
- We dismount and lead the horses, moving as quietly as we're able.
- The architecture is notably shades of black and grey, decrying the name [[Ashfall]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] keeps an eye out and feels like we're being watched. ![[Session 7 art 1.jpg|hh-pop]]
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] assures us we're better off leaving alone whatever "living" beings we find.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] pulls his dagger to stay at the ready, but [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] tells him to put it away. The glint of steel can attract them.
- We see a statue to an old god in the ruined city center. [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] recommends to go around it.
- [[Dharshoon]] recognizes the statue. A strange old god known to be associated with child sacrifice.
	- He warns us away from it. It's from his homeland.
- We continue through the ruined city and come upon what once a mansion/palace.
- [[Dharshoon]]'s bees are also spooked.
- We set up in the building to rest for the night, [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] recommending the 2nd floor.
- The house is configured in such a way our horses are able to accompany us to the 2nd floor.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] takes first watch, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] 2nd, [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] 3rd.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] hears the cries of some kind of monsters in the night. Perhaps more wights.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] also hears the cries, this time a female, and a figure with yellow eyes notices him. ![[Session 7 art 3.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] tells us it will likely not attack, but stay alert.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gives watch to [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] and has an unsettling dream.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]'s watch is uneventful, thankfully.

## Next Day

 - We awake, and [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] comments on how uneventful the night was.
- As we are about the leave, we hear new sound. A screech from high above.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] confirms Wyverns have caught our scent. ![[Session 7 art 4.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We take cover in the building, waiting for them to pass.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] keeps an eye on them while we wait them out.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] gets a good luck and thinks there's only one.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] suggests luring it ground and killing it.
- We discuss and come up with a plan. We bait in the wyvern and ambush it, killing it.
- The plan is to have [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] bait it into the courtyard, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] jumps off the roof pouncing on it, [[Theodore Silver|Theo]], [[Dharshoon]], and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] through javalins at it tied to ropes anchored to stone of the buildings they hide in.
- We execute the plan.
	- We lure the wyvern in flawlessly.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] pins it, knocking it prone.
	- After a round of attacks and impacting it with the javelins, the wyvern gets up and snaps all the javelins, freeing itself.
	- The party surrounds it.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] gets the killing blow on the wyvern with his quarterstaff.

## End Combat

 - [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notices another Wyvern flies overhead, but has 4 limbs and red scales.
- It's a [[The red dragon of Ashfall|red dragon]] flies overhead, coming from the south.
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] says to take the horses and ride east as fast as we can.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] quickly cuts off the tail of the wyvern, taking it and its stinger with them.
- As we attempt to escape, the [[The red dragon of Ashfall|dragon]] gives chase and roars at us.
- The [[The red dragon of Ashfall|dragon]] flies past us and lands on a building in front of us, roaring loudly. ![[Session 7 art 5.jpg|hh-pop]]
- It exhales fire at us, narrowly missing us with its fire breath, singing our and our horse's hair.
- We split up, while [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] outrides us all, all of us still heading east.
- We escape the [[The red dragon of Ashfall|dragon]] and meet back up with [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]].
- [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] didn't know that any dragons claimed this territory.
- We continue down the road, crossing a road that leads us out of the blight.
- In the distance we see a city. ![[Session 7 art 6.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We return to our ruse of [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] as slaves, [[Dharshoon]] and [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] as leaders, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] as a fellow slaver, and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] as a [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] man.
- We approach [[Na'Buchar|Na’Buchar]]. We must be cautious.
- Several crucified demis line the streets. City guards in light armor approach us as we approach the city.
- They speak in [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]]. Only [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] and [[Dharshoon]] can understand and speak it.
- The guards spit on the "slaves."
- The city gates open for us and we enter the city.
- We arrive at our destination and a female elf servant greets [[Ahmed Al-Zahim|Ahmed]] in the common tongue. ![[Session 7 art 7.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We are able to drop the slavers ruse now.
- We are greeted and taken inside to a dining room. We are all set to recline and await our host.
- Our host arrives [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid]]. He welcomes us kindly. ![[Session 7 art 8.jpg|hh-pop]]
- We inform him of the potential alliance between [[Ra'Haadi forces]] and the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].
- He is unfamiliar with it, but will investigate.
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] has us introduce ourselves, which we all do.
- He wishes us to accompany him tomorrow with his meeting with the city [[Emir Hadi ibn Ghassan|Emir]] (city lord).
- He tells of a strange man attending the city meetings. He wears all black. He is not [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]]. ![[Session 7 art 10.jpg|hh-pop]]
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theodore]] suspects this is a [[House Lancaster|Lancaster]] agent.
- We sell [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] our Wyvern tail for 500 gp and split the profits. ![[Session 7 art 9.png|hh-pop]]
- [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] asks if we have someone who can translate for us in the meeting tomorrow, which [[Dharshoon]] can.
- After the conversation, [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] approaches [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and asks about [[Godric Arden]], specifically his character.
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] explains how [[Godric Arden|Godric]] is the rightful king of [[Harrenor]], not because of any blood claim, but because he is the better man.
- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] tells [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] about a voice that says <mark>"I've searched for thee, dark stricken creature."</mark> and sees the fortress of [[Grimhold]] on fire.
- The language feels familiar to [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]], but he can't put his finger on it. All he can tell is it is something old and powerful that watches [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]].
- The party relaxes and recuperates and prepares to attend the meeting with [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]] tomorrow.
