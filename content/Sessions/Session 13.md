## Previously

 - The party and a large group of freedmen arrived at [[Grimhold]], a fortress on the border of [[Harrenor]]. They were greeted by [[Torren Grimston]], who promised a proper burial for [[Sahir Ebon'Sayid|Sahir]].
- The group learned that the war in [[Harrenor]] had gone poorly, with [[Ra'Haadi forces|Ra'Haadi]] pirates sacking [[Oldport]] and [[Stag's Rest]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] revealed his true identity as a spy for [[Lord Thomas Baring]] and received a letter with a two-week deadline, detailing a trap to capture him, using his sister and brother-in-law as bait at the [[Blackbear Inn]].
- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] gave a speech and recruited two new men named [[Marcus Freeman|Marcus]] and [[Lucias Freeman]], they and the party setting off to rescue [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]]'s family.
- They were ambushed by [[The Black Hands|Black Hands]], a group of [[Sable Wardens]], who were skilled in combat and magic. The party fought and defeated them, gaining some gold and valuable experience.
- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]]'s [[The Palantir|palantir]] started acting up. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] thinks attuning to it could be helpful.
- The party finished setting up camp after their difficult battle, preparing for the rest of their journey to the [[Blackbear Inn]].

## Now

- We rest and wake to a to warm sunlight. We break camp and set out on a path leading towards our destination in [[Giant's Gap]].
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] has the [[The Palantir|palantir]], but is cautious using it. He keeps it stowed for now.
- We proceed down the road through the wood. As we do, we notice a body on the road with tracks leading away from it.
	- The body is a black hand assassin, [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] notice.
	- [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] inspects the body and notices clean cuts through the armor (ring mail).
	- This cool looks to have happened last night.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notes the only weapon he knows that could have done this is [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]]'s [[Starbinder]].
	- We decide to move forward hoping the enemy of this enemy is a friend.
- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] decides to use the [[The Palantir|palantir]] to scry on his sister. ![[Session 13 art 1.png|hh-pop]]
	- She's tied up somewhere and looks distraught.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] feels like something/someone was watching him as he watched.
- We intercept the tracks again as we move down the path.
- A man sits ahead with something in his lap. He appears to not have noticed us.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] and [[Draven Throssenheiser|Draven]] approach him cautiously while the rest of us (including [[Marcus Freeman|Marcus]] and [[Lucias Freeman|Lucias]]) hold back.
	- He's polishing a sword.
	- They say hello. He's not alarmed.
	- This is clearly the man who made the kill of the assassin.
	- His blade doesn't appear to be made of aeternium, but is clearly unique. There is a ruby in the pommel.
	- He says people call him [[Artorius|the Outrider]]. ![[Session 13 art 2.png|hh-pop]]
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] shakes his hand, it's strangely warm.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] thanks him for killing the assassin, mentioning they were hunting us.
	- This gets [[Artorius|the Outrider]]'s attention, he asks after how many. We tell him honestly.
	- The man stands and stashes his sword in an immensely well-made golden gilded scabbard.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks about it. He says it was a gift in the line of duty.
	- We say goodbye and carry onward.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] notices all of his gear aside from his sword seem very old, much older than him. He mentions this to the group once they're out of earshot.
	- We speculate as to his origin. Perhaps an angel?
- We continue on until dusk when we reach the area outside the inn, noticing the smoke in the air above it. ![[Session 13 art 3.png|hh-pop]]
- We agree that [[Dharshoon]] and [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] will enter the in and pretend to be slavers.
	- We take the aliases Bjorn and Hasan.
	- We estimate 10-12 men are camped around the building, all wearing plate.
	- [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] and [[Dharshoon]] approach and are promptly told to piss off even after offering a bribe.
	- We piss off and return to the group.
	- The only way we can see forward is to send [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] in by himself.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] heads in and is led in, seeing his sister, brother in law, and [[Lord Thomas Baring|Lord Baring]].
	- [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] has him beaten right away.
	- They search him and find the [[The Sending Stone|sending stone]] we sent in with.
	- [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] knows at least 2 friends of his are outside, then has one of the guards kill [[Garlan Selmy|Garlan]].
	- The two trade verbal jabs, until [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] has a guard put a blade to [[Alanna Selmy|Alanna]]'s neck.
	- [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] says that [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] needs to find [[Henry Grey]]'s camp and retrieving [[Robert Lancaster]]. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] is to meet [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] at [[Kingsmont]] after that, where he'll be reunited to his liege lord, [[Titus Lancaster]].
	- He has his guards beat [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] some more to remind him who's in charge.
	- He's thrown out of the inn (grabbing the [[The Sending Stone|sending stone]]) and returns to the group.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] explains to us what transpired.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] suggests we exploit the situation to rescue [[Sophia Grey]] in [[Kingsmont]].
	- We spend a tremendous amount of time debating on what to do next.
	- We decide to approach [[Henry Grey]] about exchanging [[Robert Lancaster]] for [[Sophia Grey]], but in reality, we intend to exchange him for [[Alanna Selmy|Alanna]]. We complete the trade for the wrong person. We then head north to [[Godfrey Arden|King Godfrey]] [[House Arden|Arden]], hoping [[Henry Grey]] assumes we were double crossed, making him more upset with the [[House Lancaster|Lancasters]].
	- We head back to the [[House Grey|Grey]] camp. Their force is significantly smaller than it was previously. They're spread thin at the moment.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]] is there.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] talks with him about the whereabouts of his father. [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] knows little.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] greets us and escorts [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] alone in the [[Henry Grey|King Henry Grey]]'s Council. ![[Session 13 art 4.png|hh-pop]]
	- [[Lord Callum Douglas]] is there. They are arguing when [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] arrives.
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] announces [[Theodore Silver|Theo]].
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] mentions he's sword to marry Lord Callum's daughter, and asks [[Lord Callum Douglas|Callan]] to fight harder on his behalf. Callum agrees grudgingly.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]]'s mom [[Audrey Grey]] is there as well as [[Lord Irwin Tempest]], Lord of [[Oldport]], where [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] is from. As well as his two sons, Sir Harold and Sir Allen.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] explains part of our adventures in [[Ra'Haad]], careful not to explain that his sister can summon a dragon.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] wants [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] to go to [[Brightguard]] and strike an alliance with King [[House Arden|Arden]].
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] assigns [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon Stelaris]] and 10 of his men to accompany us.
	- [[Henry Grey|King Grey]] leaves [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] to speak with [[Lord Irwin Tempest]].
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] asks after his father, [[Matthew Silver]]. [[Lord Irwin Tempest|Lord Tempest]] has heard nothing. He apologizes.
	- [[Lord Irwin Tempest|Lord Tempest]] tasks his sons to looking after his whereabouts.
	- [[Theodore Silver|Theo]] leaves to proceedings, finding the party and informing us in the snag in our plan (us having an escort).
	- [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]] approaches us to inform us it's time to grab [[Robert Lancaster]] and head out.
	- [[Audrey Grey|Lady Grey]] is a hot mess, and grateful for us going to retrieve her daughter.
	- We retrieve [[Robert Lancaster|Robert]] who insults us at every chance he gets. [[Joran Jotunsson|Joran]] attempts to backhand him, but is stopped by [[Sir Baylon Stelaris|Baylon]].
	- We set out for [[Kingsmont]], arriving in three weeks' time. It is mid-February.
	- [[Kingsmont]] is a large majestic city, one of the finest in [[Harrenor]].
	- We approach the main palace, having not been intercepted yet by [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]].
	- We're told [[William Arden|King William]] [[House Arden|Arden]] is unavailable, but [[Titus Lancaster]] will see us soon. We're escorted to quarters.
	- [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] is requested by [[Titus Aurelius|Lord Titus]] personally.
	- [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]] debriefs him. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] is compliant and is shortly dismissed.
	- [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]] intercepts [[Raouen Sanderfell|Roauen]] in the hall outside [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]]' chambers. [[Raouen Sanderfell|Raouen]] gives him a sealed letter he received from [[Titus Lancaster|Titus]] that was meant for [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]].
	- [[Catherine Lancaster]] intercepts [[Lord Thomas Baring|Baring]], asking him for a favor.
	- [[Sophia Grey|Sophia]] has escaped and cannot be found.
